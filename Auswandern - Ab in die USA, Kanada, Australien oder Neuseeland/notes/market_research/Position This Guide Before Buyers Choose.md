# Position This Guide Before Buyers Choose
The book has a credible market position as a **four-country decision guide for German-speaking adults**, but available evidence still cannot establish a 10/10 sellability score or predict sales. The revised REV126 description is 140 words and has a clean one-page render; the new typeset cover candidate meets KDP’s recommended 1,600 × 2,560-pixel dimensions, and its 160 × 256 thumbnail has been checked. Those are concrete production improvements, though the cover art was upscaled from 992 × 1,586 pixels and neither asset has been assessed in the live Amazon listing. Competitors support a distinct comparison-first lane, but Amazon.de demand, search visibility, buyer response, and conversion remain unknown. This research snapshot reflects sources checked on 14 October 2026; REV126 updates below are based on the current project review.

## Four-country comparison gives the guide a defensible lane
The clearest current comparables occupy different positions: Hayit’s 2026 Canada and New Zealand handbooks provide destination-specific procedural coverage; *Der amerikanische Traum* offers a broad USA handbook, while *Neustart USA* focuses on the E-2 investor route. Australia titles located in this review were older or insufficiently verified, so the evidence suggests a possible opening for current, sourced Australian guidance but does not establish that the market lacks newer books. ([Hayit Canada](https://www.hayit.de/kanada-info), [Hayit New Zealand](https://shop.hayit.de/shop/auswandern-nach-neuseeland/), [Fachmedien USA guide](https://www.fachmedien.de/product/pdf?productRef=01019783868819724), [Neustart USA](https://www.neustartusa.com/neustartusa), [REISE Know-How Australia guide](https://www.reise-know-how.de/sites/default/files/Australien_Auswandern.pdf))

That mix supports a focused promise: **compare four destinations, then plan the relevant next steps**. The REV126 copy identifies the adult audience, four destinations, USA’s larger share, a 12-month plan, German formalities, and a legal/tax/immigration-advice boundary. This is sound expectation-setting for a broad guide, while the uneven country depth should stay explicit so readers do not mistake it for four equally deep specialist manuals. Destination-specific competitors foreground edition currency, checklists, official contacts, or personal experience; the book can answer with dated sourcing and concrete reader outcomes where the manuscript supports them. ([Current KDP description, local file](../../KDP_Beschreibung_REV126.docx); [KDP description guidance](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201189630?lang=en))

## The revised description makes its promise easier to scan
The REV126 description is **140 words**, near KDP’s approximate 150-word editorial suggestion, and its review render fits cleanly on one page. That resolves the earlier concern about a longer, repetitive pitch. Its quick-start and comparison benefits should remain prominent, along with the clear scope, uneven country depth, and advice boundary. KDP’s 150-word guidance is not a hard limit; the formal description ceiling is 4,000 characters including HTML. The four destinations distinguish the offer, while “Auswandern” alone is generic, so the destination subtitle needs to remain visible and legible wherever buyers first encounter the listing. ([KDP description guidance](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201189630?lang=en), [KDP metadata rules](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201097560))

Public migration figures establish context, not a buyer pool: Destatis counted 9,200 German citizens moving to the United States in 2023 and called it the most popular destination outside Europe, while warning that emigration destinations are often unknown in official statistics. Neither these counts nor competitor listings measure how many people are considering these four destinations, willing to buy a guide, or satisfied by a multi-country format. ([Destatis, 4 October 2024](https://www.destatis.de/DE/Presse/Pressemitteilungen/2024/10/PD24_N048_12_91.html), [2024 migration statistics](https://www.destatis.de/DE/Themen/Gesellschaft-Umwelt/Bevoelkerung/Wanderungen/Publikationen/Downloads-Wanderungen/statistischer-bericht-wanderungen-2010120247005.html), [quality report](https://www.destatis.de/DE/Methoden/Qualitaet/Qualitaetsberichte/Bevoelkerung/wanderungsstatistik-2024.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=2))

## The cover meets pixel guidance but remains a candidate
The preferred REV126 v2 typeset cover candidate measures **1,600 × 2,560 pixels**, matching KDP’s recommended size and 1.6:1 height-to-width ratio. It uses a two-line destination subtitle. At **160 × 256 pixels**, the thumbnail check keeps the title clear and the destinations distinguishable, though the subtitle remains small. The source art was 992 × 1,586 pixels before upscaling, so final-size compliance does not by itself confirm the finer detail will look sharp. Keep the image as a candidate until its full-size quality, metadata match, and behavior in Amazon’s actual preview are confirmed. ([REV126 typeset cover candidate, local file](../../Kindle_front_cover_REV126_typeset_candidate_v2.png); [KDP cover guidance](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G6GTK3T3NUHKLEFX))

## Live discovery and reader evidence remain open
Amazon.de product pages were inaccessible during the market research, so no live Amazon price, review count, rating, rank, category placement, or competing Kindle availability was verified. The project’s live title, author, description, cover, and format linkage still need direct KDP/storefront comparison. KDP permits up to seven relevant keyword fields and up to three relevant categories, but the best phrases and placements for this specific book cannot be derived from the help pages alone; verify Amazon.de searches/autocomplete and the actual category selector, then confirm the resulting metadata in the live listing. ([KDP metadata rules](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201097560), [keyword guidance](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201743260), [category guidance](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200652170))

Retailer observations elsewhere cluster around €19.95–€28 for some print guides and €14.99–€15.99 for a small number of ebooks, but these are not a representative survey and do not establish a suitable price for this title. KDP search placement changes and is not guaranteed. A stronger commercial score requires actual listing and competitor snapshots plus reader evidence—ideally conversion and sales or page-read results over a defined period, supported by buyer feedback. ([KDP search results guidance](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GATLM5TG2NFRXQ89))

## Conclusion
REV126 improves two visible storefront assets: a concise, cleanly rendered description and a correctly sized cover candidate with a checked thumbnail. The book’s defensible position remains a sourced comparison across four destinations, with transparent limits on depth and professional advice. A 10/10 commercial score still awaits live Amazon.de metadata and keyword/category checks, competitor visibility, and real reader and conversion evidence.
