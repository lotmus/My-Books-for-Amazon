# Amazon KDP listing rules for a German emigration guide

Research checked 14 October 2026. Scope: official Amazon KDP requirements and recommendations relevant to a German Kindle eBook/detail page. Amazon may revise these rules; check the live KDP setup pages again before submission.

## What rules apply to the title and subtitle?

### Takeaway
Use only the actual title shown on the cover; title and subtitle together must stay below 200 characters under KDP’s Metadata Guidelines. Match the cover, manuscript, and metadata exactly, and do not use the title/subtitle as a keyword-stuffing field.

### Cited Findings
- KDP says the title field must contain the actual cover title. Prohibited examples include generic keyword repetition, unauthorized references to other authors/titles/trademarks, sales-rank claims, promotion language such as “free,” HTML tags, and title text made only of punctuation. [Metadata Guidelines for Books](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201097560)
- Title and subtitle together must be fewer than 200 characters; a subtitle is optional. KDP’s separate Books Titles & Editions help page says “200 characters or less,” so the official pages differ at the exact boundary. Use fewer than 200 characters to satisfy the stricter wording. [Metadata Guidelines for Books](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201097560); [Books Titles & Editions](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GW7J4WEKBVU25YEC)
- The title, subtitle, author name, series information, and ISBN in the eBook manuscript must match title setup. Cover title/subtitle/author/series information must also match corresponding metadata. [Upload and Preview Book Content](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200641240); [Metadata Guidelines for Books](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201097560)
- KDP says published Kindle eBook titles and subtitles cannot be edited in place; an update to locked details requires a new edition/new title process. [Update your book details](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200736410)

### Inferences
- For a multi-country guide, keep title wording descriptive and accurate about its scope, while putting extra search concepts in the keyword fields rather than adding promotional or repetitive phrases to the title.
- Resolve the intended author name (the project identifies Kevin Drew Peters) and cover wording before upload, because post-publication title/author corrections may require a new edition process.

### Gaps
- These rules do not determine the strongest market-facing title or whether the current cover and KDP fields match; that requires inspecting the exact current listing and assets.

## What description length, formatting, and content rules apply?

### Takeaway
The KDP description limit is 4,000 characters, including HTML tags. Basic formatting is supported, but URLs, review quotes, review requests, promotional material, pricing/availability, emojis, and keyword/tag phrases are prohibited.

### Cited Findings
- The description limit is 4,000 characters, and the editor counts HTML tags toward the limit. [Write a Book Description](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201189630?lang=en)
- Supported basic formatting includes paragraph/line breaks, bold, emphasis/italics, underline, headings h4–h6, and ordered/unordered lists; h1–h3 are not supported. [Write a Book Description](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201189630?lang=en)
- Amazon recommends making the description simple, compelling, and professional; it suggests an approximately 150-word paragraph that is easy to scan. This is guidance, not the maximum allowed length. [Write a Book Description](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201189630?lang=en)
- Prohibited description content includes phone numbers, physical mailing addresses, email addresses, website URLs, reviews/quotes/testimonials, requests for reviews, ads/promotional material, time-sensitive information, availability or price, alternative ordering information, Unicode emoji, and keywords/book-tag phrases. [Metadata Guidelines for Books](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201097560); [Write a Book Description](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201189630?lang=en)

### Inferences
- Use concise German prose, clear benefit-led paragraphs, and restrained supported HTML; count the tags when checking the 4,000-character cap.
- State evergreen contents and reader benefits. Avoid embedding volatile visa dates, price claims, external links, or testimonial-style endorsements in the description.

### Gaps
- KDP does not prescribe a required word count for every genre; the 150-word suggestion should not be treated as a hard cap.

## How many categories may be selected, and how should they be chosen?

### Takeaway
KDP permits up to three categories during setup. Categories must accurately fit the book; the available choices depend on the primary audience and selected marketplace, and the live taxonomy should be checked in KDP at submission time.

### Cited Findings
- Authors may select up to three categories during title setup, based on primary audience and marketplace. [Metadata Guidelines for Books](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201097560)
- KDP advises choosing accurate, relevant categories and says misleading categories are not tolerated. Its category help suggests balancing relevance with category popularity and recommends researching similar books; these latter points are advice, not requirements. [KDP Categories](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200652170)
- A primary marketplace must be selected before categories; it also informs list pricing. Category updates may take up to 72 hours to display. [Metadata Guidelines for Books](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201097560); [KDP Categories](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200652170)

### Inferences
- For this German-language guide, select the intended primary marketplace first, then choose only relevant non-fiction/travel/immigration categories actually offered for that storefront and audience.
- Category names and placements should be recorded from the live KDP selector rather than assumed from an old category list, since options are marketplace-specific and can change.

### Gaps
- Public KDP help does not name the best three categories for this specific book. The final category menu is account- and marketplace-dependent and must be verified in the active KDP setup.

## How many keyword slots are available and what restrictions apply?

### Takeaway
Up to seven keyword fields are available. Choose accurate reader-search phrases; KDP recommends 2–3-word phrases and prohibits misleading or unrelated terms, other authors/books, sales-rank claims, promotions, and HTML.

### Cited Findings
- KDP allows up to seven keywords related to the book’s content and advises avoiding vague terms. [How do I add/change my keywords?](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201743260)
- KDP says a keyword may be a word or phrase and recommends 2–3-word phrases. Inaccurate or unrelated keywords that mislead readers are prohibited; examples include other authors, other authors’ books, sales rank, promotions, unrelated content, and HTML tags. [Metadata Guidelines for Books](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201097560)
- KDP explicitly bars keyword or book-tag phrases in the description. [Metadata Guidelines for Books](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201097560)

### Inferences
- Keywords can cover distinct relevant intents such as destination, emigration planning, visa pathways, or practical settlement topics, without repeating irrelevant competitor names or promotional wording.
- Keep each phrase honest to the actual manuscript, which covers the USA, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand.

### Gaps
- The official pages reviewed do not specify a per-slot character limit or explain the complete search-ranking algorithm. Confirm field validation and policy at upload; do not infer an undocumented limit or ranking guarantee.

## What price and royalty constraints matter for the German Kindle eBook?

### Takeaway
For Amazon.de, the current 70% royalty list-price band is €2.69–€12.99 inclusive; 35% pricing has a separate file-size-based floor and a €215 ceiling. The 70% rate is conditional, calculated after VAT and delivery costs, and the eBook must be priced at least 20% below any Amazon physical edition.

### Cited Findings
- KDP offers 35% and 70% royalty plans. Germany is an eligible 70% sales territory; other territories outside the eligible list pay under the 35% option. [Digital Book Pricing Page](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200634500)
- KDP’s current euro table lists 35% floors of €0.89 for files under 3 MB, €1.79 for 3 MB to under 10 MB, and €2.69 for 10 MB or larger, with a €215 maximum. The 70% range is €2.69–€12.99. [eBook List Price Requirements](https://kdp.amazon.com/de_DE/help/topic/G200634560)
- For eligible 70% sales, royalty is 70% × (list price minus applicable VAT minus delivery costs). Amazon.de delivery cost is €0.12 per MB (file size is rounded up to the nearest KB, subject to a €0.01 minimum). The 35% formula is 35% × (list price minus applicable VAT), with no delivery-cost deduction. [Digital Book Pricing Page](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200634500)
- The 70% option requires the eBook’s list price to be at least 20% below the Amazon list price of any physical edition of that digital book. Public-domain-dominant books are only eligible for 35%. Rights/distribution and other program requirements also apply. [Digital Book Pricing Page](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200634500)
- If Amazon price-matches the eBook below the 70% minimum list price requirement, the applicable royalty falls to 35%. Price matching may mean the selling price differs from the publisher’s list price. [Digital Book Pricing Page](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200634500)
- KDP lists the German Kindle eBook VAT rate as 7% in its royalty example; actual royalty shown in the setup screen should be checked for the selected marketplace and file size. [Digital Book Pricing Page](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200634500)

### Inferences
- A file over 10 MB could make the 35% floor €2.69, equal to the current 70% floor; check the final delivered file size, not just the source EPUB size.
- For a long, research-heavy guide, compare royalty estimates at candidate prices using KDP’s displayed calculation because delivery costs lower 70% earnings and the physical-edition discount rule can constrain price.

### Gaps
- This research does not inspect the current KDP account, selected royalty plan, physical edition price, final Amazon-converted file size, or marketplace-specific list price. The exact payout cannot be determined without those values.

## What cover image specifications are requirements versus recommendations?

### Takeaway
A Kindle eBook needs both a separate marketing cover and an internal cover image. KDP recommends a 1600 × 2560-pixel RGB marketing cover at 300 DPI/PPI or higher and 5 MB or less; the numeric image specifications are presented as recommendations, while covers under 500 pixels on the shortest side will not display on the website.

### Cited Findings
- KDP requires a marketing cover image for the Amazon detail page and an internal cover image within the eBook. It warns that QA can fail an internal cover that is too small. [Cover Image Guidelines for Kindle eBooks](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G6GTK3T3NUHKLEFX)
- Recommended marketing image specs: 2,560 pixels high × 1,600 pixels wide, 300 DPI/PPI minimum, file size 5 MB or fewer; JPEG preferred; RGB color profile (Kindle does not support CMYK). KDP calls dimensions/recommended image-quality settings recommendations. [Cover Image Guidelines for Kindle eBooks](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G6GTK3T3NUHKLEFX)
- If the marketing cover is below the 2560 × 1600 recommendation, KDP displays a reminder on upload; covers under 500 pixels on their shortest side are not displayed on the website. Amazon strongly recommends creating a new correctly sized image instead of stretching a small one. [Cover Image Guidelines for Kindle eBooks](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G6GTK3T3NUHKLEFX)
- Cover image must not infringe copyright or mention price/temporary offers. KDP recommends checking that title and author appear, cover metadata matches the detail page, text is legible, language matches the book, and there is no spine, barcode, back cover, watermark, or placeholder text. [Cover Image Guidelines for Kindle eBooks](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G6GTK3T3NUHKLEFX)
- The internal cover should match the detail-page title and author, be legible, avoid pricing/promotional claims, and not be duplicated. KDP says it should fill at least 50% of the allocated space and warns against adding a separate HTML cover page in addition to the cover image. [Cover Image Guidelines for Kindle eBooks](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G6GTK3T3NUHKLEFX)

### Inferences
- For a four-destination book, visual differentiation of the four locations and readable title/author at small thumbnail size are useful quality goals, but not stated numeric KDP rules.
- Keep the same exact title and intended author (Kevin Drew Peters) on cover, metadata, and internal cover; check the image’s pixel dimensions, color mode, file size, and thumbnail legibility before upload.

### Gaps
- KDP does not give a minimum type size or a measurable thumbnail-readability threshold. Actual cover quality and exact current cover dimensions were not inspected in this rules-only task.

## What should be verified in the live listing before publication?

### Takeaway
Official help supplies the constraints, but the live KDP interface is needed to confirm the selected marketplace, actual category menu, royalty estimate, and validation of uploaded assets. Treat the rules above as current on the access date, not permanently fixed.

### Cited Findings
- KDP says to use its Online Previewer to check the eBook on phone, tablet, and Kindle-reader modes; its quality panel checks issues such as spelling and low-quality images, with some checks limited by language. [Upload and Preview Book Content](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200641240)
- KDP notes that title, subtitle, author, series, and ISBN in the manuscript must match title setup. [Upload and Preview Book Content](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200641240)
- KDP’s German pricing page records the updated 70% euro band starting 7 July 2026, so older guidance that caps the 70% band at €9.99 is outdated. [eBook Listenpreis-Anforderungen](https://kdp.amazon.com/de_DE/help/topic/G200634560)

### Inferences
- Before publication, verify exact metadata against the cover and interior; validate description character count with markup; select accurate live categories; confirm all keyword fields; and compare the royalties shown by KDP at the current uploaded file size.
- Save the KDP screen/export or a dated checklist so any later metadata or price revision can be checked against the same baseline.

### Gaps
- No account-level listing, live category selections, price, keyword values, or cover file were audited in this research task.
