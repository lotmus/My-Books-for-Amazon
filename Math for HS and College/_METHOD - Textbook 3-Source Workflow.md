Chronological steps for commercial use (AI plus  Manual)

Manual: Select 3 high-quality textbooks on the same topic.
AI: Condense each textbook to ~50 pages of precise core material (definitions, key theorems, essential derivations, minimal necessary examples). Instruct: keep only quintessential content, preserve technical accuracy, do not copy wording unnecessarily.
Manual + AI: Review each 50-page condensation for accuracy and completeness. Correct any technical errors or important omissions the AI dropped.
AI: Create a unified nomenclature / notation glossary from the three condensations.
AI: Rewrite all three condensations so they use the exact same nomenclature and notation.
Manual: Review and finalize the unified nomenclature table. Make any expert adjustments.
AI: Merge the three equalized 50-page versions into one coherent text. Instruct it to combine overlapping material, preserve useful alternative explanations, and produce a single logical flow.
Manual: Decide the final high-level structure. Re-order, merge, or split sections so the overall architecture differs from all three source books.
AI + Manual: Go section by section. Use AI to generate alternative explanations, then heavily rewrite them yourself so wording, rhythm, and explanatory moves are independent.
Manual: Replace or redesign examples and analogies. Do not keep the original choice, order, or framing.
Manual: Create new problem sets / exercises from scratch (or substantially redesign any retained ones).
AI: Run a similarity audit. Ask it to compare the current draft against each of the three original condensations and flag remaining close parallels in examples, pedagogical devices, argumentative flow, and distinctive presentation.
Manual: Review every flagged passage and rewrite or replace it until no close parallels remain.
Manual: Add original value (new perspective, updated material, better bridging text, original diagrams, etc.).
Manual: Final expert review for technical accuracy, pedagogical quality, and overall independence from the source texts.
Manual: Prepare for publication (editing, formatting, legal review if desired).
This sequence keeps AI in the high-volume condensation, equalization, merging, and auditing roles, while putting the critical originality and quality-control work in human hands.

**Yes.** Here are practical ways to cut working hours for both pure scientific textbooks and popular science books while keeping commercial safety and quality acceptable.

General Shared time-savers  )

- **Limit sources to 2 instead of 3** when the topic is mature and well-standardized. The third book adds diminishing returns and extra condensation/audit work.
- **Work in layered passes, not perfect first drafts**  
  First AI pass = rough condensation + merge.  
  Second pass = nomenclature + structure.  
  Third pass = only the sections that failed the similarity or quality check.  
  Avoid polishing everything equally.
- **Create a reusable “style and constraints” document** once (target length, tone, notation rules, forbidden close patterns). Feed it to the AI at every step so you stop re-explaining.
- **Batch the similarity audits**  
  Run one strong AI audit on the full merged draft rather than section-by-section audits early on. Only do detailed manual fixes on flagged parts.
- **Use AI for first-pass redesign of examples/analogies**, then only manually upgrade the weakest 30–40%. You do not need to hand-craft every single one.
- **Set hard time boxes** per stage (e.g., 90 minutes max for reviewing one condensation). Perfect is the enemy of finished.

Extra reductions for pure scientific textbooks

- Keep more of the original logical order when it is already standard in the field. Only rearrange where there is clear pedagogical gain. Structural overhaul is expensive.
- Let AI generate 2–3 alternative short explanations for each key concept; pick the best and do light editing instead of full rewrites.
- Reuse and lightly adapt standard exercise types rather than inventing every problem from scratch. Focus manual effort on the 10–15 most important exercises.
- Accept “good enough” diagrams: AI-generated or simple redraws are often sufficient; only redraw the critical ones by hand.


Highest-leverage overall strategy

1. AI does almost all volume work (condensation, equalization, first merge, first alternative explanations, similarity flagging).  
2. You only touch the material that affects legal safety, technical correctness, or reader experience.  
3. Everything else stays at “competent AI draft” level.

Used this way, many people cut total human hours by roughly 40–60% compared with the full careful version of the method, while still producing a commercially defensible result. The biggest remaining time costs will almost always be: (1) fixing technical precision and (2) restoring voice/engagement in popular science.
