# Science Book Evaluation Rubric

Supplied by Lothar on 11 Oct 2026 as the rubric for scoring his science books. Use it with the four-angle strict scoring in `notes/LOTHAR_RULES.md` (Scoring) and record results in `SCORES.md`. It suits popular science books, textbooks and science communication. Scores here are 1–4 per criterion.

## Criteria (score each 1–4)

| Criterion | Excellent (4) | Good (3) | Satisfactory (2) | Needs Improvement (1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scientific Accuracy | Information is current, accurate, and supported by evidence. | Mostly accurate with minor issues. | Some inaccuracies or outdated information. | Significant errors or misleading information. |
| Clarity of Explanations | Concepts are explained clearly and are easy to understand. | Generally clear with occasional confusion. | Explanations require effort to follow. | Difficult to understand. |
| Organization & Structure | Logical flow with strong chapter organization. | Mostly organized and easy to navigate. | Some organizational issues. | Poorly organized. |
| Use of Evidence & References | Strong citations and reliable sources throughout. | Adequate references provided. | Limited references. | Few or no credible references. |
| Visuals & Illustrations | Excellent diagrams, photos, and charts that enhance understanding. | Good visual support. | Some useful visuals. | Visuals are missing or ineffective. |
| Engagement & Readability | Highly engaging and maintains reader interest. | Generally interesting. | Moderately engaging. | Boring or difficult to read. |
| Depth of Content | Covers topics thoroughly with appropriate detail. | Good depth overall. | Basic coverage only. | Superficial treatment of topics. |
| Critical Thinking & Inquiry | Encourages questions, investigation, and analysis. | Some inquiry opportunities. | Limited critical thinking elements. | Little or no encouragement of inquiry. |
| Audience Appropriateness | Perfectly tailored to intended readers. | Mostly appropriate. | Sometimes too simple or too advanced. | Poor match for target audience. |
| Overall Impact | Outstanding contribution to science education. | Valuable and informative. | Moderately useful. | Limited educational value. |

## Score interpretation (unweighted, ten criteria, maximum 40)

| Total score | Rating |
|---|---|
| 36–40 | Exceptional Science Book |
| 31–35 | Strong Science Book |
| 21–30 | Adequate Science Book |
| 10–20 | Needs Significant Improvement |

## Weighted version (for serious reviews)

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Scientific Accuracy | 25% |
| Evidence & References | 15% |
| Clarity | 15% |
| Content Depth | 15% |
| Visuals | 10% |
| Engagement | 10% |
| Critical Thinking | 5% |
| Audience Appropriateness | 5% |

To compute a weighted score, divide each criterion's 1–4 score by 4, multiply by its weight, and add: the result is a percentage of the maximum. Organization & Structure and Overall Impact carry no weight in this version.
