Use one repository per series, not one per volume: keep the books, source, cover assets, builders, release notes, and series-level review material together. Store genuinely shared author guidance and catalog/score data in one small canonical repo; have series repos refer to versioned copies or links rather than depend on paths outside their own checkout.
