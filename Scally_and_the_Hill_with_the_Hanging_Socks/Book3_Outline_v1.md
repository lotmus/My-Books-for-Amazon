# Book 3 outline v1: Scally and the Hill with the Hanging Socks

The Step Stories, Book 3, by Anneliese Tauber. Written 12 Oct 2026 from Lothar's notes. Everything here can change; items marked (ASK) need Lothar's decision.

## Title options (all follow "Scally and the ...")
**Chosen by Lothar, 12 Oct 2026: Scally and the Hill with the Hanging Socks** (the sleeping bats hang upside down like socks on a line; the title never says "bats", so the reveal in chapter 4 is a joke the readers share with the children).
Dropped options: Scally and the Listening Hill, Scally and the Sleeping Wings, Scally and the Hill of Echoes, Scally and the Hill That Went Click.

## Season and time span
First frost in November to the first warm evening in April. The pregnancy starts early in the book and ends with the birth in the Night of the Big Snow. Winter is the quiet half of the book, spring the loud half. Bats hibernate in winter and wake in spring, so the plot and the science follow the same calendar.

## Ages (Lothar's rule: the human children grow; Scally and Hopsi look almost the same and no ages are stated for them)
- Hal about 9 and a half, Titch about 9 and a half, Wen about 9 (new), Tabitha about 8 and a half, Pip about 7 and a half (Book 2 had him at 6 and three-quarters).
- KDP age range: read-aloud from 7, reading alone 8 to 11.

## New characters
- **Wen Liang** (boy, about 9). New to the village. Careful, quiet, a tinkerer who mends clocks and gears. Afraid of the dark and of bats at first. Never takes a shortcut or a risk, so he is the opposite of Scally and they argue happily. He is welcomed with a present, as Hopsi welcomed Tabitha in Book 1.
- **Dr Liang**, Wen's mother, the new village doctor. Brisk, kind and funny; carries a black bag and a small ultrasound scanner (a storybook gadget; say so in the notes).
- **The newborn**: Titch and Pip's baby brother (decided by Lothar, 12 Oct 2026). Pip chooses his nickname (a silly one such as "Biscuit" that sticks).
- (Book 4, not now: the red-haired girl.)

## The two pregnancy threads (Lothar's note)
- **Baby born:** Titch and Pip's mother. She is expecting when the book opens and gives birth in the Night of the Big Snow (chapters 9 and 10).
- **Someone else pregnant:** Hal's mother. Dr Liang finds out at the scan lesson (chapter 7); Hal is told at the end of the book and has to get used to no longer being the only child, which mirrors what Pip goes through.

## Spine
**Pregnancy book, then baby book (Lothar, 12 Oct 2026):** Book 3 is the pregnancy book (waiting, the scan, the secret, ending with Biscuit's birth in the last chapters); Book 4 is the baby book (newborn weeks, sleepless nights, safe baby habits, the naming feast, the redhead arrives). Pending Lothar's confirmation of the split.

A mystery in the hill, a conflict with the Mayor, and a winter storm that needs the doctor. The mystery: Gucci howls at sounds nobody else can hear, and the hill "clicks" at night. It turns out to be a huge roost of hibernating bats in the cave, which the Mayor plans to seal up for his new cheese store. The children must prove the bats are there by looking, counting and testing, not by shouting. In the middle of that, the big snow arrives and Titch's mother goes into labour while Dr Liang is out at a hill farm.

## Chapters (11, about 14,000 words, about 80 pictures)
1. **The First Frost.** A cart arrives with a doctor and her son. Wen mends the mill clock before he has said a word. Scally's scam: "frost insurance" at a penny a house. Hopsi's quiet question stops it, as in Book 2, but this time Wen spots the flaw first (the frost is free). Hal's mother is quiet and tired; nobody knows why yet.
2. **The Doctor's Bag.** Gucci howls at the wall. Dr Liang shows how a doctor listens (a stethoscope, a heartbeat). Safety: never touch medicine, a doctor's bag is not a toy. Pip gets his head stuck in a milk churn (an original Emil-spirit mishap) and is rescued without a drama. The children welcome Wen with a present.
3. **Ice on the Pond.** The first skating day. Pip goes out too far and the ice creaks. Wen refuses to step on it; Titch throws a rope; Scally lies flat to spread his weight (a lesson from Book 2's clay). Science: ice floats, and water expands as it freezes. Safety: reach or throw, never test ice alone.
4. **The Hill That Clicks.** At night the hill makes a faint clicking and Gucci shakes. The children go to the cave mouth with a lantern (a grown-up told, in pairs, back by dark) and find hundreds of small furry bundles asleep, hanging upside down. Hopsi says "I do not know" what they are; Tabitha recognises bats from her gran's sea caves. Rule: look, do not touch.
5. **The Mayor's Cheese Cellar.** The Mayor announces he will seal the cave and keep his cheeses there. Scally's scam: a fake "bat-proof hat" and a petition with made-up names. It backfires in a funny way (the whole village signs silly names and the Mayor is embarrassed). Wen: "Check first."
6. **Wen's Listener.** To prove the bats are there, Wen builds a clever listener from a funnel, a spring and a bell, which in the story turns the squeaks into sounds children can hear. A note says it is a storybook gadget; real bat detectors need electronics. First bat sound heard.
7. **The Picture of the Baby.** Dr Liang gives a sound lesson. She scans Titch's mother first so the children can see: black and white grains on a screen. Sound goes in, echoes come back, and the machine makes a picture. It is the same trick as the bats and as Hal's echo in Book 2. Then she scans Hal's mother, privately, and keeps the news to herself. Hal sees that his mother and the doctor share a secret.
8. **The Sledging Day.** A Bullerby-spirit chapter of pure fun: sledges, a snow fort, a very bad snowman, Pip's first jump. Road safety: stop and look at the lane crossing; no sledging near the water or the road. Scally's tall tale about the biggest snowball ever; Hopsi says "That is a story, and I like it."
9. **The Night of the Big Snow.** Titch's mother's baby is coming early, and Dr Liang is at the far hill farm in the blizzard. Hal, Titch, Wen, Scally and Gucci must fetch her. Safety: tell a grown-up (the baker's wife), go in pairs, keep the whistle, stay on the road, stop if lost and blow three blasts. Wen's caution and Scally's speed finally work together. Hopsi is wrong about the path and says so.
10. **The Baby.** Dr Liang gets back in time with Wen's help and the baby is born. Pip names the baby brother (a silly nickname that sticks; Pip is now a big brother, with two brothers' worth of pride). Scally holds the baby for a moment and is very quiet. For Hopsi, no true sentence is big enough, so she gives the baby a small present.
11. **The First Warm Evening.** The snow melts. The children bring the Mayor to the cave at dusk and the bats stream out in a great cloud, hunting. The Mayor changes his mind and moves his cheeses. Hal's mother tells Hal about the baby. Hal says "Acceptable" and then, for the first time, something else. The last line opens Book 4: a girl with red hair arrives.

## Science (for Hal's Slate or Hopsi's True Things)
Echolocation and ultrasound (bats see with sound as well as with eyes); sound above human hearing; hibernation; ice floats and water expands; snowflakes have six sides; the heartbeat; how an ultrasound scan works; pitch (high and low sounds); one try is not enough evidence.

## Safety taught
Never touch a bat or any wild animal that seems tame or hurt, and tell a grown-up; ice safety, reach or throw, never test ice alone; sledging safety; getting lost in snow (stay put, whistle, three blasts); never touch medicine or a doctor's bag; tell someone where you are going; go in pairs.
