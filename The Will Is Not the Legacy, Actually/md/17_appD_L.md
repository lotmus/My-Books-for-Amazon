## Appendix I. The Video Prompt Card

Print and keep beside the camera. One prompt per recording. (Chapters 27 and 28)

**Before you press record:** window in front of you; quiet room; lens at eye level; ten-second sound test.

**Start every testament-style recording with:** your name, the date, where you are, who it is for. If it mentions property: "This recording is not my will. My will is dated ___ and held by ___."

**Easy starters**

- The first thing I remember.
- The house I grew up in, room by room.
- My first job and what it paid.
- The story I always tell, told properly.

**Life**

- A normal day in the job that defined me.
- Who taught me the thing I'm best at.
- A decision I'd make again; one I wouldn't.

**Love and family**

- How we met, including the unromantic parts.
- Each of my children at the age they are now.
- What I want my grandchildren to know about their parents as children.

**Beliefs**

- What I believe now that I didn't at thirty, and what changed it.
- What I'd say about money that isn't a slogan.
- What I'm still unsure about.

**To one person**

- Their name first. What I love about them, specifically. A shared memory. A hope, not an order. An apology, if one is due, naming the event. Something worth replaying.

**Occasions**

- A wedding. A new baby. A graduation. A hard day. A birthday I won't see.

**Practical**

- The recipe, in the kitchen, camera on the pan. How the house works. The thing only I know how to do.

**Leave out:** property instructions, passwords, accusations you'd regret, medical details you wouldn't want played at dinner.

## Appendix K. California and Germany Side by Side

Checked against official sources in October 2026. Thresholds change; check before relying on any figure. (Chapters 8, 11, 16, 17, 20, 21, 39)

| Topic | California | Germany |
|---|---|---|
| Legal system | Common law, probate courts, trusts | Civil law, notaries, probate court issues certificates |
| Minimum age for a will | 18, sound mind (Prob. Code § 6100) | 16 (BGB § 2229); minors only before a notary (§ 2233) |
| Ordinary will forms | Witnessed (§ 6110), holographic (§ 6111), statutory form (§ 6240) | Notarial (§§ 2231, 2232) or entirely handwritten and signed (§ 2247) |
| Witnesses | Two, present at the same time | None for handwritten; notary for notarial |
| Typed will signed by hand, no witnesses | Not valid as a formal will | Not valid |
| Video or audio will | Not valid | Not valid |
| Joint will | Not a special form; couples often use matching wills or a joint trust | Spouses and registered partners only (§ 2265; LPartG § 10); *Berliner Testament* (§ 2269) |
| Gift to a witness | Will valid, but presumption of undue influence unless two other disinterested witnesses (§ 6112) | Not applicable to handwritten wills; persons benefiting are excluded as witnesses in emergency wills (§ 2249) |
| Protected family shares | Spouse's community property half; omitted spouse/child rules (§§ 21610, 21620) | Compulsory share: half the statutory share for descendants, parents, spouse (§ 2303); gifts in last 10 years partly counted (§ 2325) |
| Spouse without a will | Community property plus 1/2 or 1/3 of separate property with children (§ 6401) | 1/4 alongside descendants (§ 1931), plus 1/4 in default property regime (§ 1371) |
| Effect of divorce | Revokes will gifts and many nonprobate transfers to ex-spouse (§§ 6122, 5040); not federally governed retirement plans | Gifts to spouse ineffective after divorce, or if divorce was pending under conditions (§ 2077) |
| Estate administration | Probate court; statutory fees (§§ 10800, 10810) | Heirs take estate directly (§ 1922); liable for debts (§ 1967) |
| Simplified procedures | Personal property affidavit up to $208,850; primary residence petition up to $750,000 (deaths from 1 Apr 2025) | No probate; banks may not demand a certificate in every case (BGH XI ZR 401/12) |
| Disclaimer | Federal tax rules generally allow 9 months for a qualified disclaimer | 6 weeks (6 months if abroad) (§ 1944) |
| Will custody and register | Custodian must deliver to court within 30 days of death (§ 8200) | Official custody on request (§ 2248); Central Register of Wills; duty to deliver (§ 2259) |
| Death taxes | No state estate or inheritance tax; federal estate tax above $15,000,000 (2026) | Inheritance tax on each heir; allowances: spouse €500,000, child €400,000, grandchild €200,000 (ErbStG § 16) |
| Annual gift allowance | $19,000 per recipient (2026, federal) | Same allowances as inheritance, renewing every 10 years (ErbStG §§ 14, 16) |
| Financial power of attorney | Uniform Statutory Form (§ 4401), notarised | *Vorsorgevollmacht*; Central Register of Lasting Powers of Attorney |
| Healthcare directive | Advance health care directive; notary or two witnesses; optional state registry | *Patientenverfügung* (§ 1827); spouse's emergency health representation up to 6 months (§ 1358) |
| Digital assets | RUFADAA (§§ 870 ff.); provider online tool overrides will (§ 873) | Accounts pass to heirs (BGH III ZR 183/17) |
| Cross-border | State conflict rules | EU Succession Regulation: habitual residence, choice of nationality law (Arts 21, 22) |
