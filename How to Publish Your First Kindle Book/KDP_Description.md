## Description (paste into KDP; plain paragraphs, under 4,000 characters)

You finished the manuscript. The harder part is the file, the product page, an honest review request, and what to do after the book is live.

How to Publish Your First Kindle Book is for first-time and early independent authors who want to publish a Kindle ebook without wasting money or getting lost in platform jargon. Part I is the short road: one action per chapter, following one first-time author and her cozy mystery through a month of decisions, then seven things to do this week. The later parts are the reference: marketplace settings, formatting, covers, keywords, categories, pricing, a launch without an audience, a small ad test, KDP Select or going wide, taxes and reports, the first-review problem, and using AI without losing the book.

Inside you will learn how to:

• Write the reader promise and the sentence for who the book is not for
• Set a Kindle ebook price inside the current 70 percent band when the book qualifies
• Make the cover, description, and sample promise the same book, and test the cover at thumbnail size
• Choose keywords and categories a real reader would use
• Ask for an honest review the way Amazon allows, with no rating requested and no trade
• Find readers and send an advance copy without turning the request into spam
• Read a quiet first month without guessing, and choose one next test
• Answer KDP’s AI-content question for what you actually created
• Avoid vanity presses, bought reviews, and Select exclusivity mistakes

Claims are labelled: a platform rule with a dated note, a worked example, field practice, a recommendation, or a question you can test yourself.

This book is not an advanced Amazon Ads manual, a writing-craft course, a store-by-store guide to going wide, or a system for publishing many books quickly. It promises no income. It gives you an ordered route and a way to check the file.
