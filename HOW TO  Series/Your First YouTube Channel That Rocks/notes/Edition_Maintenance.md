## Platform claim review

For every consequential official-rule claim, check the linked YouTube Help or Blog source directly before release. At minimum, review Partner Program thresholds, Shorts revenue, eligibility by feature, policy and disclosure requirements, and payment rules before each edition. For the October 2026 threshold check, the primary references were YouTube Help’s [Partner Program changes](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009?hl=en), [Shorts monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en), and [how to earn money on YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72857?hl=en). See [YouTube’s under-18 monetization guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2533300?hl=en) and [AdSense age requirements](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/14230?hl=en). The “adult beginners / roughly 16+ with an adult” recommendation is an editorial suitability judgment, not a platform age rating.
