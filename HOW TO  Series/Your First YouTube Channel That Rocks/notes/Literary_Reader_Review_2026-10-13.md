## Reader assessment

The book's strongest literary choice is its running question: will a stranger choose the next video? That thread connects the first page, the video sequence, the analytics, the diagnosis, and the closing without turning the guide into a staged success story. Short, concrete lines give the advice a recognizable voice; the more technical passages now separate rules, examples, and decisions so readers can keep their place.

The book is practical nonfiction. Policy pages, thresholds, workbook prompts, and source notes need compact reference language. Their terseness serves lookup and action, while the recurring viewer-and-return thread carries the book's style across them. I score its distinction against that intended form. This is a literature-fan editorial judgment, not a claim that the text is literary fiction or that every reader will assign the same score.
