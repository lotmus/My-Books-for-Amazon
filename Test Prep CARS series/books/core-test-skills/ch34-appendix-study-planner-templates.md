**More Praxis Tips**

Praxis Tip: Fill in the planner on the same day each week, at a fixed time.

Praxis Tip: Use a different color for review tasks so that they stay visible.

Praxis Tip: Treat the planner as a draft. A plan that bends is better than one that breaks.

Praxis Tip: Keep every completed planner. They are evidence of what actually worked.
