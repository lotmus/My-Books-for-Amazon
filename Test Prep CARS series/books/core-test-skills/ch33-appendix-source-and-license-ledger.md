# Appendix: Source and License Ledger

**What this appendix is for**

This book teaches skills with original examples. When you copy a chapter into an exam-specific project, add exam facts, or borrow anything from outside, you need a record that shows where each item came from and that you may use it. A ledger is that record. It is boring to keep and very useful to have, rather like a smoke alarm.

Praxis Tip: Start the ledger on day one. Rebuilding one from memory in the week before publication is a miserable week.

**What needs a ledger row**

- Any passage, chart, image, table or quotation not written by you.
- Any exam fact (format, timing, scoring, rules) taken from an official source.
- Any statistic or study you cite.
- Any template or tool you adapt.
- Any figure from an open-licensed source, with its attribution text.

Your own original passages, questions and explanations do not need a source row, but note them as "original" so the ledger shows they were considered.

**Ledger template**

| Item | Where used (chapter) | Source title and creator | URL | License | Attribution text | Date checked | Verified by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | | | |

Add one row per item. If a row cannot be filled completely, the item is not ready to publish.

**License basics**

| License or status | What it generally allows | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Public domain | Free use, no credit required (though crediting is good practice) | Make sure the work is truly public domain in your country, and check any added material (new translations, photos, commentary) that may be protected separately |
| CC BY | Use, adapt, even commercially, with attribution | Follow the exact attribution format; note any changes |
| CC BY-SA | As CC BY, but adaptations must carry the same license | "Share-alike" may extend to your adapted material |
| CC BY-NC | Use with attribution, not for commercial purposes | Not suitable for a book you sell |
| CC BY-ND | Use as is, without changes | You cannot adapt it |
| US government works | Generally public domain in the US | Third-party material inside them may be protected; foreign government works differ |
| Facts, ideas, formulas | Not protected as such | Their particular wording and arrangement can be |
| Released exams and commercial prep books | Not available for reuse | Do not reproduce, retype or closely paraphrase |

This table is a summary, not legal advice. Licenses have details and versions, and law differs between countries. When in doubt, ask a qualified professional or the rights holder.

Praxis Tip: Record the license version (for example "CC BY 4.0"), not just the family. Attribution requirements can differ between versions.

**Exam owners' materials**

Official sample questions and practice tests from the exam owner are copyrighted. You may use them for your own study, but you may not copy, retype, summarize in detail or paraphrase them into a product you distribute. The exam owner's published format, content outline and rules are facts you may describe; link to the official page rather than reproducing it.

Praxis Tip: When you describe an exam in your book, cite the official source and the date you checked. A reader can then see what you relied on.

**Trademarks and independence**

Use an exam's name only to say what your book prepares readers for. Do not use logos or imply that the exam owner approved or produced your book. Put a plain statement on the title page, such as: "This book is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the owner of {EXAM}."

**Release checklist**

- [ ] No released exam, official practice item or commercial prep-book text appears, including paraphrase
- [ ] Every borrowed item has a complete ledger row
- [ ] No non-commercial-only material in a book that is sold
- [ ] Share-alike terms honored where they apply
- [ ] Attribution lines are present and correctly formatted
- [ ] No invented citations, URLs, studies or license claims remain
- [ ] All `[VERIFY]` flags resolved by a qualified reviewer
- [ ] Every answer key independently solved
- [ ] A web search of distinctive phrases found no accidental matches
- [ ] Exam name used only descriptively; independence statement present; no logos or "official" claims
- [ ] Health, sleep and anxiety content reviewed by a qualified person
- [ ] Credits page complete

**More Praxis Tips**

Praxis Tip: Keep screenshots or PDFs of the license pages you rely on, with dates.

Praxis Tip: Store the ledger with the manuscript so that they travel together.

Praxis Tip: Re-run the checklist before every new edition.

Praxis Tip: If you reuse a chapter in a second exam book, copy its ledger rows too.

Praxis Tip: Add a row for the exam-specific facts every time you fill a placeholder such as {TIME_PER_SECTION}.
