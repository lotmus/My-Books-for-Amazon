# Appendix: Source and License Ledger

**What this appendix is for**

This ledger records where everything in this book came from and why you may rely on it. For Book 4, the Critical Analysis and Reasoning Skills volume, the record is short, because almost everything is original. Reading passages are the heart of this book, and a reader (or a reviewer) should be able to see at a glance that every one of them was written for it. The few outside items are facts about the exam, taken from the exam owner's public pages, and each one is listed below.

Praxis Tip: A short ledger is still a ledger. Update it whenever you add a passage, a quotation or a fact, not at the end of the project.

**Independence statement**

This book is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the owner of the exam it prepares readers for. The exam's name is used only to say what the book is about. No logos, trademarks or "official" claims are used. Where this book and the exam owner's pages differ, the exam owner's pages win.

**What was and was not reproduced**

No released exam, no official practice passage or question, and no commercial prep book was reproduced, retyped, summarized in detail or closely paraphrased in this book. No passage was excerpted from, or modeled sentence by sentence on, any published article, book, essay or review. Every passage, question, answer choice, explanation and worked example was written by the author for this book.

The topics are general or invented (arts, literature, philosophy, ethics, architecture, music, the history of ideas, economics, sociology, anthropology, political thought, education, linguistics and cultural studies). Where a passage mentions a real-world fact, the fact is common knowledge, stated accurately and in the author's own words. Where a passage mentions a scholar, a book, a district, a school or a study, it is invented, or left unnamed ("some critics"), and is not meant to resemble any real person or work. No passage requires outside knowledge to answer its questions, and none depends on a real author's wording.

The glossary definitions, the trap catalog, the passage-mapping templates and the Praxis Tips are the author's own words.
