**Series author:** U. B. Smart
**Genre in Book Writer:** Exam Prep Course  **Chapters:** 25  **Status:** drafting in progress. See `books/book4-cars/`.
**Covers:** the exam's Critical Analysis and Reasoning Skills (CARS) section: passage-based reading and reasoning with no required outside knowledge.

Before drafting, re-check the exam owner's *current* free format page (number of passages and questions, timing, passage sources, question skill shares). Everything flagged `[VERIFY]` below is from the public description as I understand it.

## How this book fits the series

- **Imports Core Test Skills:** schedule, question method, error log, pacing and test-day routines are imported, not rewritten (Core chapters on reading structure, logic and argument, and pacing).
- **No subject lessons.** CARS is a skills section. The book teaches methods and gives a lot of practice.
- **Every passage is wholly original.** Passages are written for this book (about 450-650 words), in the style of humanities and social-science prose, on invented or general topics, never excerpted, paraphrased from, or modeled sentence by sentence on any published passage, released exam or commercial prep book. No outside knowledge is needed to answer any question. Any real-world fact in a passage is common knowledge, accurate, and checked.

## Premise

> A self-paced course that builds the reading, argument-analysis and inference skills tested in the MCAT's CARS section, with a method for mapping passages, a catalog of question types and trap answers, and many original timed passages with full explanations.
