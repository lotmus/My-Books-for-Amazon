## Premise (paste into Book Writer)

> A self-paced course that teaches the biology and biochemistry tested in the MCAT's Biological and Biochemical Foundations of Living Systems section, and builds the reasoning, experiment-interpretation and pacing skills the section rewards. Each unit has clear objectives, mechanism-based explanations, worked examples, many Praxis Tips (practical study and test-taking techniques, including how to learn pathways and how to read biology experiments), original checkpoint questions, spaced review, and timed original practice. All passages and questions are original or openly licensed; no released exam content or commercial prep-book text is used. Assumes the reader has completed or is working through the chemistry and physics book in this series. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the exam owner.

## Chapter list

### Part 0. Orientation and biology-specific skills

1. **Strategy: Know the Biology and Biochemistry Section** [ADAPT] Section anatomy, passage types, how the skills are tested, how this book links to Book 1 and the Core skills. Praxis Tips: map every lesson to a skill, not just a topic.
2. **Strategy: Learning Pathways and Systems Without Rote Memory** [CONTENT] Drawing pathway maps from memory, regulation logic (what turns it on, what turns it off), compare-and-contrast tables, one-page system summaries. Praxis Tips: learn the logic of a pathway before the names; redraw it weekly.
3. **Strategy: Reading Biology Experiments** [CONTENT] Controls, knockout/knockdown logic, dose-response, reading gels and blots described in text, predicting results of a perturbation. Praxis Tips: ask "what would happen if this step were blocked?" for every figure.

## Notes for drafting

- Generate with chapter count 39 and check that each title keeps its prefix (`Strategy:`, `Lesson N:`, `Practice:`, `Appendix:`).
- Draft in order. Metabolism lessons (4-9) are dense; have each expert-checked before writing the practice sets that depend on them.
- Pathway names, enzyme names, normal values and drug mechanisms are common AI error sites. Mark every one `[VERIFY]` and have a qualified person check the Fact Sheet and answer keys.
- Passage seeds from open-access papers must be CC BY or public domain, recorded in the ledger with the license page checked, and never invented. Otherwise write passages as wholly original scenarios with consistent made-up data.
- Each Practice chapter should hold 2-3 original passages and about 10-12 questions to fit in one response; full-length sections use two chapters each.
