## Premise (paste into Book Writer)

> A self-paced course that teaches the chemistry, physics, organic chemistry and biochemistry tested in the MCAT's Chemical and Physical Foundations of Biological Systems section, and builds the reasoning, data-interpretation and pacing skills the section rewards. Each unit has clear objectives, mechanism-based explanations, worked examples, many Praxis Tips (practical study and test-taking techniques), original checkpoint questions, spaced review, and timed original practice. All passages and questions are original or openly licensed; no released exam content or commercial prep-book text is used. For learners starting about six months before the exam. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the exam owner.

- Skills (approximate): knowledge of concepts 35%, scientific reasoning and problem-solving 45%, research design 10%, data-based and statistical reasoning 10%.

### Undergraduate notes

- For a specific course, use the instructor's syllabus and learning objectives as the "public outline," with the instructor's permission if you plan to distribute.
- Standardized undergraduate exams (AP, CLEP, ACS and similar) are copyrighted like the MCAT. The same rule applies: follow the public format, write original items, never reproduce released ones.
- Student-facing versions can add a short "how to use office hours and past homework" Strategy chapter. It is CORE and reusable.
