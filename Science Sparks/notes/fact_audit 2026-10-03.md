### 5. Ch 78: 'Current data favor dark energy being a true constant' outdated -> DESI DR1/DR2 hint of evolving dark energy (~2.8-4.2 sigma, ~3 sigma after supernova recalibration); not a discovery

- File: `20_physics_and_cosmos.md`
- Old: Current data favor dark energy being a true constant over a dynamically evolving "quintessence" field.
- New: As of 2026 the true constant is still the simplest fit, but not an unchallenged one. Baryon-acoustic measurements from the DESI survey, released in 2024 and 2025 and combined with supernova and microwave-background data, prefer a dark energy that slowly weakens over time, at roughly three standard deviations depending on which supernova sample is used. Bayesian comparisons are less impressed, and physics asks for five standard deviations before it calls something a discovery. It is a persistent hint worth watching, nothing more yet.
- Source: DESI Collaboration DR2 Results II (arXiv:2503.14738, 2025); DESI DR2 Lyman-alpha AP results (arXiv:2607.27410, 2026); Bayesian reanalyses arXiv:2511.10631, arXiv:2603.05472
