## Selected Sources and Further Reading

The books and institutional resources below are reliable places to continue. Newer chapters give key sources at the end of the chapter. For selected time-sensitive, medical, disputed or numerical claims in earlier chapters, sources are listed here by chapter number. This is a guide to further reading, not an exhaustive bibliography.
