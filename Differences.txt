Yes. Looking at the actual final files, the two series should be organized like this:

Series 1 — Lolly Wren
Lolly Wren’s Curious Science Adventures

This is the quantum-science comedy/adventure series.

Book 1: The Ministry of Eventualities
Subtitle: A Novel of Quantum Bureaucracy

Persona: Lolly Wren — the central scientific protagonist. The series is built around her investigations and her encounters with real physics disguised as absurd bureaucracy.

Author: Q.E.D.
Real author: Lothar J. Musiol. The book explicitly uses Q.E.D. as the publishing pseudonym and reveals Musiol in the book itself.

Narrator: Lothar Feynmann — the fictional science narrator/persona associated with the explanatory physics material, not the author and not a character who should be confused with Lothar J. Musiol.

Series 2 — The Relativistic Investigation Bureau
The Relativistic Investigation Bureau

This is the special/general relativity series, centered on the Bureau and its investigations.

Book 1: The Murder That Hadn’t Happened Yet
Subtitle: A Relativistic Investigation

The cover/title-page branding also uses:

Special & General Relativity — A Relativistic Investigation
By Sven Gerald Rupertson, allegedly

Persona: Derek Gent — the central investigator of the Bureau. The opening explicitly establishes that the Bureau exists because Derek started investigating things and that he has an unfortunate habit of noticing things other people would rather not notice.

The supporting Bureau personas include Trevor, Penny, Tabitha, Weinstein, Julius Barbarian, etc.; they belong to this relativistic/Bureau world, not the Lolly Wren series.

Author: Sven Gerald Rupertson (S.G.R.)
Real author: Lothar J. Musiol. The book explicitly states that S.G.R. is the author name and that S.G.R. is Lothar J. Musiol.

Narrator: Lothar Feynmann — for the audiobook/scientific explanatory voice, while Sven Gerald Rupertson remains the displayed author identity.
