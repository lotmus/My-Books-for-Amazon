Boot used to sleep inside the spare boot when it was smaller. The leather held it then. Now the same boot stands empty on the path while Boot lies across Wilf’s foot, larger and less thin. Sparefunk leaves the pair where it is. No one tries to put Boot back inside.

Titch sets out the crust. Boot takes it and stays. Hopsi eats a hop. The light arrives straight after its wrinkles cancel. The way back to the step is still open. The coast is further and wetter. They do not go there yet.

The stream continues. Tomorrow the spare boot will still be empty, and Boot will still be on the foot.
