The Wee Scallywag and the Gentle Elf
(with Wilf, Titch, Sparefunk, Hopsi, and the wolf baby)

The light thinned for the last time that evening.

Wilf set the slate aside, finished. Titch left the hem turned and the crust place empty. Sparefunk counted the tyres, left the spare boots standing dry, and sat in the pair he was wearing. Hopsi ate the final hops, folded the cloth, and put the needle where morning would find it. The wolf baby slept with its nose on Wilf’s boot.

The rectangular eggs kept their corners in the dark. The stream continued. No moonbeam remained to cancel or arrive. The axle bird had already gone. No stranger came. No one ran. No one spoke.

People talked too much and did too little. They knew love and forgot its aim. On the step the four of them — Wilf, Titch, Sparefunk, Hopsi — and the wolf baby simply stayed until sleep took what was left.

Tomorrow would bring whatever it brought. The boots, the slate, the hops, the needle, and the habit of meeting the day quietly would still be waiting.

And the stream continued, as streams do.

The End
