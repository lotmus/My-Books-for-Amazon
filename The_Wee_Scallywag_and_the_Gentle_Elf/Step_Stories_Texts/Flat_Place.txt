They stayed at the flat place until the light thinned. Boot slept. Wilf kept the slate closed. Titch rested the folded cloth on her knee. Sparefunk set the spare boots side by side, empty and ready. Hopsi put the needle through the hem of her own sleeve so it would not be lost.

The mill could still be found by turning. The path could still be followed by walking. Neither was chosen before sleep. The eight-minute brightness faded. The next brightness was already travelling.

In the morning they would wake under light that had left the sun while they slept, and the choice would be there again.

The stream continued through the dark.
