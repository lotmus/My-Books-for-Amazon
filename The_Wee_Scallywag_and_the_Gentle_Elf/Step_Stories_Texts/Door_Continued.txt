Morning they checked the latch first. It held. Boot sniffed the threshold and did not go in. Wilf set the slate down outside. Titch kept the crust on the step. Sparefunk left the spare boots in reach but did not cross the doorway. Hopsi ate a hop and watched the door stay shut.

No one mentioned the dusk opening. The channel was cleared. The hem was turned. The tyres were stacked. By afternoon the door had not moved again. They sat where they could see it.

The way to the bend remained open. The way inside remained unchosen. Boot settled on the foot. The eight-minute light lay across the step as usual.

Tomorrow the latch would be checked again.

The stream continued.
