The Wee Scallywag and the Gentle Elf
A fantasy adventure with science ideas for curious children

When Hal Millersen’s mill wheel jams, he makes a three-day bargain with a wee scallywag: one true name to guess, and one small thing nobody explains. Then a gentle elf, a lonely mill ghost, a toll-taking toad, a wolf pup, and a soft-voiced stranger lead Hal and his friends into trouble—and toward the truth.

Along the way, children explore levers, rainbows, inheritance, refraction, the Moon’s phases, sunlight travel time and tides. Hopsi admits when she does not know, and Hal learns to look, count, test and write down what he finds.

Includes 11 illustrated chapters, short activities, and a guide for parents and teachers. For ages 6–9 to read aloud, and ages 8–10 to read independently.
