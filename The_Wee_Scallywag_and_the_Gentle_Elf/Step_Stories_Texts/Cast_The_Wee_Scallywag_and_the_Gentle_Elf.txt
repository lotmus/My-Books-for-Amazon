The Wee Scallywag and the Gentle Elf — Complete Cast

Core characters (consistent across story and pictures):

1. Miller’s son
   - Human boy
   - Normal (human) ears
   - Ordinary clothes and shoes
   - Starts with the empty sack and old boots
   - Goes to school, carries a slate

2. Neighbour’s daughter
   - Human girl
   - Normal (human) ears
   - Quiet, practical
   - Sometimes sits on the step with her own slate or mending
   - From the village

3. Wee scallywag (Sparifankerl)
   - Boy
   - Pointed ears
   - Messy hair
   - Little devil / imp
   - Owns two pairs of big boots:
     - the pair he usually wears
     - a spare pair that sometimes stands empty (drying or set aside)
   - Cheeky, learns honesty and usefulness

4. Gentle elf
   - Girl
   - Pointed ears
   - Long wavy hair
   - Kind, quiet, mends things
   - Speaks only what is true (or shuts up)
   - Leaves rhymes and practical help

Notes for pictures:
- Faces must stay consistent for each character.
- Ears: only the scallywag and the elf have pointed ears.
- When empty boots appear and the scallywag is already wearing boots, the empty pair is his spare.
- Style: detailed painterly children’s book art, warm natural light, soft textures, ages 5–10.
