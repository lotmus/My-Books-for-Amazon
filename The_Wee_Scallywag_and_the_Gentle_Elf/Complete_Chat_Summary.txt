The Wee Scallywag and the Gentle Elf — Chat Summary
(Compiled from the collaboration)

This is a condensed record of the full conversation that built the book.

Origin: Austrian/Bavarian “schparifankerl” → English “scallywag”.  
Evolved into an illustrated children’s book for ages 6–9.

Title: The Wee Scallywag and the Gentle Elf  
Series: The Step Stories  
Author: Otis Umberlight  
Book 2 working title: A New Path

Final cast:
- Wilf Millersen (8, human boy, slate, channel)
- Titch Mitchel (8, human girl, crusts, hems)
- Sparefunk (8, pointed-ear boy, two pairs of boots, tyres)
- Hopsi / Elfie Didlhop (9, pointed-ear girl, hops, needle)
- Tabitha Smoke (8, Black girl, curly hair in bun, just met them)
- Boot (wolf baby, once slept in spare boot)

Key story elements:
Bargain, name-guessing, least-time light paths, eight-minute sun delay, genetics in the grain, rectangular eggs, stranger-danger run to the bend, way back always open, deadpan British voice, small true things, no lectures.

Science quietly included: QED path cancellation, principle of least time, selective growth, inheritance with variation, tunneling in a culvert, shadows as blocked paths.

Tone: quiet, kind, positive, deadpan. People talk too much; the step does the small true things.

Files produced include the final story text, drafts, cast list, blurbs, and many generated images (some drifted; correct ones show only the real cast).

The stream continues.
