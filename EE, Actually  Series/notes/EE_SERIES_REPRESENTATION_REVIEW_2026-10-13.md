## Findings and disposition

No derogatory language, identity-based character traits, or claims that nationality determines technical ability were found in the reviewed identity-related passages. Professional examples generally use role-based descriptions (“engineer,” “technician,” “laboratory engineer”) without assigning ability or behavior to gender, ethnicity, or nationality. Geographic examples are most specific where specificity matters: regulatory regimes, grid conditions, and locale settings are attributed to named regions or standards.

The author biography repeated across the series names Munich University of Applied Sciences, Germany, the United States, Bavaria, and the author's dual citizenship as personal facts. Its light family and regional humor is self-description, not a claim about a group. No rewrite was warranted; changing personal facts or voice to satisfy a generic representation checklist would risk making the biography less accurate.

The 9/10 scores reflect a technical series with few identity-bearing narratives: the material avoids stereotyping, while the historical and professional examples are not a deliberate survey of diverse people. For a future edition, add individuals from a wider range of backgrounds only where their documented engineering contribution directly supports the topic; name their work and context precisely, and do not imply that identity explains technical achievement.

## Verification boundary

The review searched the current chapter mirrors and manually assessed identity-related passages returned by targeted searches. It does not certify every sentence as bias-free, nor does it establish how readers from all represented groups will respond. No manuscript edits were justified by the findings.
