# Book 1 Reader-Lab Kit Procurement Review

**Date:** 13 October 2026  
**Scope:** The Reader Lab kit introduction in the Book 1 Preface and its tagged mirror.

## Finding and correction

The prior introduction stated that all ten items were sold as small, breadboard-ready modules with pin headers and put every item in one price range. That is too broad: connector formats and accessory costs vary, especially for camera assemblies and a protected 18650 battery setup. The revised text says many sensor/display modules have breadboard-compatible headers, warns that camera/battery connections vary, asks readers to check pinouts and logic voltage, and tells them to budget separately for the controller, camera board, and protected battery assembly. It removes the unsupported blanket price range.
