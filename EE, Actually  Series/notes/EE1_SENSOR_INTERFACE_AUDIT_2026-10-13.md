# Book 1 Sensor-Interface Teaching Audit

**Date:** 13 October 2026  
**Scope:** Chapter 27.5 and the Book 1 Reader Lab's mix of bare elements and integrated modules.

## Finding and correction

Chapter 27.5 said every sensor in the kit converts a physical quantity into a voltage for an ADC, and described all familiar senses as divider/bridge inputs. That does not fit the kit's camera and digital sensor modules, or transducers that require amplification, charge/current conversion, or other signal conditioning. The heading now names the actual subject: resistive sensors and divider interfaces. The text explains that a divider is appropriate for resistive sensing, gives examples of other sensor types, distinguishes bare elements from integrated modules, and tells readers to check the interface before choosing a divider, bridge, or ADC.
