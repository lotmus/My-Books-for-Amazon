**Review date:** 13 October 2026  
**Scope:** Current DOCX masters and tagged-text mirrors for Books 1-9. This is an editorial review of identity wording and specificity in national, regional, institutional, and locale references. It is not community consultation or a demographic study.

## Scorecard

Scores use two separate questions: **language and specificity** asks whether references are accurate, contextual, and free of group-based assumptions; **breadth** asks how widely people, places, and institutions appear in the examples. The second score is not a measure of technical quality.

**Series assessment after the follow-up pass:** language and specificity **9.5/10**; breadth **6.0/10**. No reviewed passage assigns ability, trustworthiness, or behavior based on nationality, ethnicity, gender, religion, or disability. Country and regional distinctions generally explain standards, grid conditions, spectrum rules, or locale behavior. Named organizations are technical bodies, regulators, labs, or vendors in context. The repeated author biography is a personal career account, not a generalization about Germans or Americans.

The main representation limitation is that these are technical books with few human-centered examples and a strong U.S./European focus. The scores are deliberately split: precise, respectful wording does not mean broad representation. Examples should name the applicable jurisdiction or locale when it changes an engineering result, and any future people-centered examples should be checked for local context rather than added as tokens.

## Fixes made

- **Book 1, Chapter 23:** replaced "ELI the ICE man" with a direct explanation of ELI for inductors and ICE for capacitors. This removes a gendered mnemonic while retaining the voltage/current relationship.
- **Book 3:** retained the earlier correction from "foreign atom" to "dopant atom."
- **Book 7:** retained "consistency checks" in place of the mental-health metaphor "sanity checks." The established term "blind via" remains defined as a via that reaches an inner layer from an outer surface; it does not describe a person.
- **Book 8:** retained the de-DE locale wording and the qualification "some European design flows."
- **Book 9:** retained de-DE locale examples and the I2C term "target device" in the problem and answer key.
- **Book 6, Appendix B:** changed the residual phrase "hours of reference insulation life" to "equivalent hours of modeled thermal aging," consistent with Chapter 17's limitation that the calculation is not a remaining-life estimate.

Each changed master was re-extracted to its matching `chapters/BookN` mirror. The corresponding changed phrases were checked in the mirrors. The Book 6 Appendix B correction is also recorded in the technical audit.

## Limits

It does not establish representation breadth, verify every technical claim, or replace feedback from readers with relevant lived experience. Technically established terms and the author's personal history were retained where there was no clearer, more accurate replacement.
