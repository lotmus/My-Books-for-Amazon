# Handover: The World Behaves (Mostly)

## Project status

New companion collection, started from scratch on 2026-10-16. No poems are being carried over from the archive or the nonsense book. The initial poems in the manuscript are original working drafts.

- Working title: *The World Behaves (Mostly)*
- Working byline: Seamus O’Leary
- Manuscript: `The World Behaves Mostly_v01.md`
- Direction: coherent, accessible rhymed poems about any subject, from love through ordinary life to space travel. Humor is welcome, but the poem must have a clear idea or feeling and the rhyme must sound natural aloud.
- Structure is provisional. Current topic groups are Love and Company; Daily Life and Its Machinery; Earth, Sky, and Space; Time and Perspective.
- This is an early manuscript, not a KDP-ready package. Add and revise poems before setting a final title, order, length, or production format.

## Editorial bar

Keep a poem only if its subject is understandable, its rhyme is pleasing rather than forced, and its ending adds a satisfying thought, image, turn, or laugh. Prefer varied forms and rhythms. Avoid making every poem a limerick or forcing every subject into comedy.
