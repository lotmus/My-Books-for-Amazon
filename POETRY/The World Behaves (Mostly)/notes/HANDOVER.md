# Handover: The World Behaves (Mostly)

## Project status

New companion collection, started from scratch on 2026-10-16. No poems are being carried over from the archive or the nonsense book. The initial thirty poems in the manuscript are original working drafts.

- Working title: *The World Behaves (Mostly)*
- Working byline: Kira Vale
- Manuscript: `The World Behaves Mostly_v01.md`
- Direction: original poems on any subject, from love through ordinary life to space travel. Mix funny, serious, intelligent, and thought-provoking work. Most poems should rhyme, with varied schemes and forms such as ballads and sonnets; blank verse and free verse can appear when they suit the subject. Some lyrics can draw on the broad tradition of reflective poems about nature, human feeling, and meaning, in a distinctly modern voice. Every poem should have a clear idea or feeling and sound natural aloud.
- Structure is provisional. Current topic groups are Love and Company; Daily Life and Its Machinery; Earth, Sky, and Space; Time and Perspective.
- This is an early manuscript, not a KDP-ready package. Add and revise poems before setting a final title, order, length, or production format.

## Editorial bar

Keep a poem only if its subject is understandable and its language feels alive. When it rhymes, the rhyme must be pleasing rather than forced. Its ending should add a satisfying thought, image, turn, or laugh. Vary forms and rhythms; do not force comedy onto serious subjects or rhyme onto a poem that wants another form.

The manuscript includes an evocative literary-persona bio for Kira Vale. Do not add invented credentials, awards, reader praise, or claims about personal life experiences.
