## What changed
- **Heavy chapters, wit down.** Africa's resources (Congo, Kongo, extraction), the Atlantic slave trade, the twentieth-century catastrophes, the Great Leap and Rwanda lost their stand-alone gags; facts and the Society's plain observations carry them. Plain bridge sentences ("Capability compounded. Judgment did not.") added to Africa, the slave trade, the Great Leap and Rwanda (Holocaust chapter already had one).
- **Dolphin flaws.** Early chapter now covers calf-killing, killing of harbor porpoises (not eaten), and herding, and says outright that nothing in the book is told from above.
- **Structure.** Japan, Italy and Spain folded into the twentieth-century chapter as sections; Religion in America folded into America's Long Account; one verdict each for the merged chapters. 44 chapters become 40. The Epilogue is now a labeled speculative appendix after the physics appendix, and the dolphins' sign-off closes chapter 44, so the history ends in the present.
- **Verdicts vary** from 21 to about 100 words (seven rewritten shorter, three removed by merging).
- **Depth.** New vignettes: Nanni's complaint to Ea-nasir (about 1750 BCE), Jerome on the sack of Rome (Letter 127), Afonso I of Kongo's 1526 letter to Lisbon, Yang Jisheng and the Great Leap. Each has a Notes entry. Three contested readings (Rome, the 1491 population, disease versus violence) are stated in Notes and Sources with a one-sentence reason.
- **Reader experience.** New front-matter page "The Cast of Recurring Ideas"; "How to Read This Book" after the Prologue; timeline caveat (early dates approximate, recent clustering reflects record survival); all 12 captioned images now have a lead-in sentence in the text; contents rebuilt (51 entries, all linked).
- **Standing rules.** "Xi" is now "Pooh" in prose and in the Notes label (the real Economy bibliography title stays). No banned name anywhere; no MTW-style abbreviations; no Colin line; American spelling.
- **Sentences.** Over-300-character sentences: 96 found (not 85, and almost all in chapter prose, not Notes); 0 remain after about 95 hand splits and rewrites. Checked by script with a splitter that also breaks before "(".
- **Modern-analogy audit.** Only a handful in the whole book (grocery bill, budget decision, railroad-car budgets, move-fast motto, "no bill"); one qualified ("It may be the most consequential budget decision in the book"), the rest are observational and kept. Futurist material is out of the main sequence.

## Before / after
Before (Africa verdict-adjacent quote): "Humans have a word for a continent that keeps being called poor while other people grow rich from what is under it ... The word is robbed."
After: the quote is deleted and the chapter says plainly, "Capability compounded: ships, guns, credit, and contracts all improved. Judgment about who should benefit did not. The arrangement is better described as a transfer than as misfortune."
