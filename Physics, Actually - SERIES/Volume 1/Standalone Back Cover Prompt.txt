PHYSICS, ACTUALLY — Volume 1 — Standalone Back Cover Prompt
==============================================================

CONTEXT: for the ebook (Kindle) file, only the front cover is needed —
no back cover, no spine. This prompt is for a separate, standalone
back-cover image for the PRINT paperback, used together with your
existing front cover via KDP's own Cover Creator (upload front art +
back art separately, enter spine text, and KDP calculates the exact
spine width and assembles the wraparound for you — no manual
stitching needed). This replaces the earlier full-wraparound prompt
if you'd rather let KDP handle spine assembly.

TECHNICAL SPEC
--------------
- Trim size: 6" x 9"
- Bleed: 0.125" on the three outer edges (top, bottom, outer side) —
  no bleed needed on the spine-facing edge, KDP's tool handles that
  seam
- Full back-panel canvas: 6.125" x 9.25" = 1838 x 2775 px at 300 DPI
- Generate at your tool's highest native resolution, then upscale to
  1838 x 2775 px before uploading

PROMPT
------

A single book back-cover illustration, portrait canvas, 1838x2775
pixels (6.125 x 9.25 inches at 300 DPI, 0.125" bleed on the top,
bottom, and outer side edges only).

Style: night sky full of stars fading into a warm orange-to-gold
sunrise/sunset glow along the horizon, layered mountain silhouettes
with pine trees in the foreground, a faint luminous blue
spacetime-funnel/wormhole grid arcing down through the sky toward the
horizon — matching the same visual world as the front cover
(portraits of Newton and Maxwell, a black hole, a steam train, a
radio tower, a figure on a cliff at sunset).

Centered text, gold serif heading near the top:
"PHYSICS, ACTUALLY"

Centered white serif body text beneath it:
"What falls, and why.
What moves, and what does not.
What time is — and what it is not.

This volume rebuilds motion, force, time, and relativity from first
principles:

Newton without the myths,
Maxwell without the fog,
Einstein without the slog.

Clear. Honest. Uncompromising."

Then, in gold: "— Lothar J. Musiol"
Then, in white beneath it: "Volume 1"

Leave a plain, uncluttered dark area in the lower-right corner
(roughly 2" x 1.2") reserved for the KDP barcode — no important art
or text there.

Photorealistic digital painting style, rich detail, color grading
matching the front cover.
