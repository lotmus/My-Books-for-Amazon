## Faults found and fixed in this pass
1. The apple trick made no sense (a pin does not sink an apple). Scally now swaps in a red-painted stone.
2. Floating was explained wrongly ("air pushes aside water"). Now: lighter than the water it pushes aside, for its size.
3. The echo distance was wrong (a third of a mile in three counts). Sound goes about a fifth of a mile in one count, and the cliff is a fifth of a mile away.
4. The boots were muddled (whose, which pair). Simplified, and one line removed.
5. Walt's story did not match the clay neck. His childhood shortcut is now that same neck, and the stranger offers "straight across".
6. Why the hum began now was missing. Added: a stream learns slowly, and tonight it knows the sentence by heart. The stranger "fell into a good many bogs".
7. The lever rescue was unclear. The rope is now knotted to the short end of the log, with Hal and Scally on the long end.
8. Thunderstorm advice now says a house or a car is best, with crouching in a dip as second best, and the True Things line matches.
9. A baby line and a nonsense gosling line were rewritten.
