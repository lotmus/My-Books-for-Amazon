# Math, Actually — series reference

What the four volumes contain, and the facts every volume shares. Built from the
manuscripts and from the working guides. `CLAUDE.md` and
`Math, Actually - Didactic Style Guide.md` point here. How a new idea is introduced stays
in the style guide. How a `.docx` is edited stays in `CLAUDE.md`.

The series was called *The Mathematics Tower* until 2 Oct 2026, with Floors,
Rooms, and the Tower as its vocabulary. It is now *Math, Actually*, the companion
to *Physics, Actually* and *Life, Actually*. Old name → new name:
Floor N → Chapter N, Room N.M → Section N.M, the Tower → the series. Older notes
and backups still use the old names.

An older draft called the derivative Chapter 6 and the limit Chapter 8. Those
numbers are trigonometry and systems of equations. Cite the chapters below.

## The four volumes

One continuous book, Chapters 1–50, by Lothar J. Musiol. Each volume also stands
on its own. The vocabulary is chapters, sections, and the series. Never floors,
rooms, storeys, or the Tower.

| Volume | Chapters | Sections |
|---|---|---|
| 1 From Arithmetic to Calculus | 1–12 | Chapter 1 has Sections 1.1–1.10. Chapters 2–12 have Sections X.1–X.9. 109 sections. |
| 2 From Multivariable Calculus to Set Theory & Logic | 13–25 | Sections X.1–X.9, except Chapter 18, which has Sections 18.1–18.10. 118 sections. |
| 3 From Differential Equations to Abstract Algebra | 26–37 | Sections X.1–X.9. 108 sections. |
| 4 From Category Theory to the Frontier | 38–50 | Sections X.1–X.9. 117 sections. |

## House style

The manuscripts follow these. Match them.

- American English (-ize, skeptical, rumor, toward, disk).
- Curly quotes and apostrophes. No straight ones in the prose.
- Em dashes spaced — like this — on both sides.
- A proper minus (−), not a hyphen, in arithmetic. The arrow is →.
- Author: Lothar J. Musiol, alone.
- Chapters, sections, and the series. No floor, room, storey, or Tower wording,
  and no lift/staircase/building framing. (Ordinary English and mathematics
  stay: the floor function, a noise floor, room temperature, the tower law of
  field degrees, the tower property of conditional expectation, Hilbert’s hotel
  rooms, the spiral staircase in the covering-space picture.)

At a hinge, the style guide bans *clearly, obviously, simply, just, of course,*
and *it is easy to see*. Kept on purpose: a sentence that names the fallacy
(“obviously true is not the same as provably true”), the idiom “as a matter of
course,” and a joke that is not sitting on a calculation. The monad operation
`just(x)` in Chapter 38 is notation, not that ban.

## Layout conventions (copied from *Physics, Actually*)

- **Title page:** series name “Math, Actually” in the Title style; Heading 1
  “Volume N - <subtitle>”; “A Volume in the Math, Actually Series” and the author
  line, both bold 13 pt, color 4F81BD, centered.
- **Copyright page:** copyright, rights, “First edition, 2026.”, the errata
  note, and a small “Math, Actually series” line (9 pt).
- **Contents:** “Table of Contents” as a plain 14 pt paragraph, then manual
  hyperlinked entries with a dot-leader tab and PAGEREF page numbers (Word
  updates them on open). Chapters as “Chapter N: Title”, with their sections
  listed under them. (Physics lists chapters only. Sections are kept here
  because the book is navigated by section.)
- **Front matter order:** Also in This Series, Preface (Volume 1) or About This
  Volume (Volumes 2–4), How to Use This Book, How This Book Is Organized, Where
  the Foundations Are, This Volume’s Chapters.
- **Chapter opening:** page break; the series line “Math, Actually” in the Title
  style (it carries the chapter bookmark); Heading 1 “Chapter N”; Heading 1
  chapter title; then the chapter’s own subtitle line.
- **Sections:** Heading 2 “Section N.M: Title”.
- **Back matter:** Answer Key, Solutions, Symbol & Notation Glossary, Further
  Reading, Subject Index, Epilogue, and “A Note Before You Go” signed Lothar J. Musiol.
- **Styles:** Title Calibri bold 20 pt, color 17365D, bottom border 4F81BD;
  Heading 1 and Heading 2 Calibri bold, color 0000FF, centered.
- **Kept from the old layout on purpose:** US Letter page (the figures are
  sized for it; Physics is 6 × 9 in), the Cambria body font, and a page break
  before each section.

## Cross-references

- Inside a volume, a mention of another section is a hyperlink named by topic
  (“see Complex Numbers”), pointing at the bookmark `sec_N_M`. Chapters link to
  `ch_N`. In the index, glossary and other back matter the link text is
  “Section N.M”. A possessive keeps the number (“Section 1.5’s theorem”).
- A section or chapter in another volume is plain text with its volume:
  “Limits (Volume 1)”, “Chapter 26 (Volume 3)”. Files cannot link to each other.
- Bookmarks were renamed in the rebrand: `room_X_Y` → `sec_X_Y` (so
  `room_18_9_tables` is now `sec_18_9_tables`), `floor_N` → `ch_N`. Every
  anchor was re-pointed.

The conversion was done once by `scripts\build_math_actually.py` (see
`scripts\README_build.md`). The masters are the source from now on.
