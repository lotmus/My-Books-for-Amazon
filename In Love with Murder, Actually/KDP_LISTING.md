## Description

At 6:42 on a Tuesday morning the wind takes the only copy of Tobias Lind's new novel off the end of a jetty in Provence, and the housekeeper walks into the water after it in her apron and cardigan. "It's only paper," he calls. "Não é só papel," says Catarina Vidal. It is not only paper.

Tobias came south after finding his wife, a man from the golf club and his own duvet in the same bedroom in Leeds. The book he lost was about his friend Elise, an editor who drowned off this same jetty in March and who, he is quite sure, did not slip. Catarina has her own reason for being here. Her sister kept this house for two years, saw something one night, and vanished.

Then his publisher offers forty thousand pounds for the unfinished book, with a contract that wants everything in the house. A literary prize turns out to commend everyone who pays the entry fee. A careful auditor has fallen from a balcony in Lyon, and a careful notary has driven off a bridge near Avignon. The people asking questions are a novelist with terrible Portuguese, a housekeeper who reads everything, a widow with fifty years of account books, a nine-year-old who knows his snakes, and one police inspector whose commandant would very much like her to stop.

In Love with Murder, Actually is a warm, wry, very British mystery about grief, fraud, a pelican that pays no rent, and two people learning each other's language one word at a time.

For readers who like their murders solved properly and their jokes told with a straight face.
