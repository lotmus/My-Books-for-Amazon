================================================================================
SHORT-FORM VIDEO HOOK SCRIPT
"The Murder That Hadn't Happened Yet"
For TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts
================================================================================

[Added 2026-09-24. The imported promotion plan (ZERO_COST_LAUNCH_PROMOTION_PLAN.txt)
covers Reddit, Goodreads, and email but has no short-video angle - which is how
most mass-market book discovery actually happens now. This fills that gap.
Designed for a single person talking to camera, or text-on-screen with a
voiceover. Total runtime: 45-60 seconds. No props needed beyond a phone.]

================================================================================
VERSION A - "The premise" (safest, no spoilers, works cold)
================================================================================

Runtime: ~45 seconds. Delivery: fast, deadpan, mock-serious.

[TEXT ON SCREEN / SPOKEN]

"Imagine getting an email that says you were murdered.

Tomorrow.

At 11:03 AM.

With a photograph attached — of you, dead, on your own office floor.

Except it's this morning. You're alive. Fine, mostly. Slightly offended.

And in the photo — frozen in the doorway behind your body — is your
twelve-year-old daughter.

She's at school right now. You just checked.

So which one is real?

That's the actual premise of a book I just read, and the answer is NOT
'time travel' — it's something so much weirder that scientists have a
real name for it, and by the end of the book you'll understand it better
than most people who took physics in school.

It's called 'The Murder That Hadn't Happened Yet.' It's a real mystery,
it's genuinely funny, and I have not stopped thinking about the ending.

Link in bio."

[CUT ON: "Link in bio" — do not over-explain the ending. The withholding
is doing the work.]

================================================================================
VERSION B - "The Rule" (uses the book's own built-in quotable lines)
================================================================================

Runtime: ~50 seconds. Delivery: same energy as Version A, ends differently.

"A man gets proof he'll be murdered tomorrow. The photo's real. The
timestamp's real. His daughter's in the background.

He spends the whole book trying to prove the photo is lying.

It isn't.

Here's the twist nobody tells you going in: the book doesn't resolve like
a mystery. It resolves like physics. And the single sentence that cracks
the whole case is one you can say out loud and it will not sound like a
spoiler, because you won't believe it's the answer until you've read
the 300 pages that make it true:

'A clock can lie by telling the wrong kind of truth.'

That's it. That's the whole case. And the book spends 60,000 words
earning that sentence, with jokes, with an actual cucumber used as a
laboratory instrument, and with a physicist dolphin named Sherlock who
solves the whole thing telepathically.

I know how that sounds. Read it anyway.

'The Murder That Hadn't Happened Yet.' Link in bio."

================================================================================
VERSION C - "For people who hate sci-fi" (crossover angle)
================================================================================

Runtime: ~40 seconds. Delivery: talking directly to a skeptical viewer.

"If you just scrolled past this because it sounds like sci-fi — wait.

You don't need to know ANY physics for this book. Zero. I didn't.

Here's all you need: a dad finds out he's going to be murdered tomorrow,
and the evidence includes his kid standing somewhere she can't actually
be standing.

That's it. That's a parent's worst fear with a deadline on it.

Everything else — the relativity, the equations, the 'wait, is time even
real' stuff — that's just how the mystery gets solved. You'll understand
all of it by the end without trying, the same way you understood Arrival
or Tenet without a physics degree. Except this one's funnier, and
British, and nobody explains anything to you like you're stupid.

'The Murder That Hadn't Happened Yet.' Book 1. Link in bio."

================================================================================
PRODUCTION NOTES
================================================================================

- Post Version A first (safest, broadest reach). Use B for a follow-up
  once the account has some engagement — it rewards people who already
  clicked through once. Use C specifically when targeting non-SF/BookTok
  audiences (romance, thriller, litfic creators doing crossover content).

- On-screen text should appear line-by-line, timed to speech, not all
  at once - this is what makes people stop scrolling.

- Do not show the cover in the first 3 seconds. Hook on the premise
  first; reveal title and cover only at the very end, right before
  "link in bio." The algorithm rewards watch-through, not brand recall.

- The demos site (github.com/lotmus/relativistic-site, sr-simultaneity.html
  in particular) makes a strong second video: screen-record the slider
  moving and voice over "this is an actual real physics demo from the
  book's companion site — drag one number and watch 'now' stop meaning
  the same thing for two people." That's a visual hook text alone can't
  match.

- Do not promise anything the book doesn't deliver. Every line above is
  checked against the actual manuscript text, not invented for effect.
