## Open story questions for Lothar

1. **Byline and KDP author field.** Both docx files now say "By Lothar J. Musiol / writing as Spezala Genara Relavi (S.G.R.)". Per `KDP_METADATA.txt`, the front cover and Audible cover show the S.G.R. byline, and the KDP Author field is still marked [DECISION FOR LOTHAR]. If the rule is "Lothar J. Musiol everywhere", the cover images and `KDP_METADATA.txt` (not docx, so not edited) need to match.
2. **Book 1 threads that Book 2 does not pick up.** Book 1's epilogue explicitly leaves these open. Book 2 never mentions them:
   - the text to Tabitha from Trevor's number, eleven minutes before the non-existent train;
   - the note in Derek's own handwriting inside Case 1047 telling him to keep Trevor away from a clock;
   - the Louvre guard who is not on the staffing record;
   - the Underground woman who knew both kinds of relativity;
   - Weinstein's missing Munich minute.
   LOCALHOST is deliberately unexplained, according to the seam. Decide which of these are being kept for Book 3 and which deserve at least one line in Book 2, so readers don't take them for forgotten threads.
3. **Book 2's morning sheet still has no sender at the end.** This is intended, per the seam. But Book 2 has no "Coming Next" sting the way Book 1 does, so the unsolved sender reads as an open end rather than a hook. Consider a short Coming Next page.
4. **Book 2 course structure.** It has only 6 lessons. Chapters 10-20 and the Epilogue (12 arrows) all point to Lesson 6, while Book 1 has one lesson per chapter. Either split Lesson 6 into the ideas it covers (sign of the gap, record vs warning, interval not point, past/future light cone) or say in a How to Read note that Lesson 6 is the capstone for the second half.
5. **Calendar across the books.** Book 1 runs Tuesday to at least Saturday (Ch 10) and beyond. The epilogue then adds "three days", "the Friday" curry and "three days later". Book 2 opens on a Monday and calls Case 1047 "last week". Confirm which Monday this is and that "last week" is meant loosely.
6. **Book 2 prose tics (line edit recommended).** Per 10,000 words, Book 2 uses "the way" 13.7 times (Book 1: 4.1), "which was" 9.8 (Book 1: 2.8) and "very" 16.9 (Book 1: 9.7). I trimmed five. A full line edit of the Book 2 middle chapters (5-12) would tighten it noticeably without touching plot.
7. **Book 2 front and back matter and KDP set.** Book 2 has no How to Read or Author's Note like Book 1, and no KDP description, keywords or cover yet. Its master file is still named `..._BOOK_2_DRAFT.docx`. Rename it when it is final.
8. **Rebuild warning.** Each book's `scripts/manuscript_text.txt` is now behind its docx. `build_book2_docx.py` also does not apply heading styles, the TOC or the new back matter. Do not rebuild either book from text without carrying these fixes across first.
