## End-state canon

- **Case 1047 is closed as a non-murder.** The camera image, clocks and case system supplied misleading records; Trevor was selected by an automatic field, not by an act. The strange handwritten clock note was an old CASE 0412 note reused by CASEBOOK, not a prophecy. Sophie's railway clock has an ordinary 32,768 Hz quartz movement fitted inside its old case. Her “do not fix it” request was honoured.
- **LOCALHOST/device 3 remains unresolved.** CASEBOOK used its own sweep to send the photograph from an unnamed paired phone. The paired phone/account is still open. This is not the source of Book 2's morning sheet.
- **Book 2's morning sheet has a known physical route and no human author.** The ordinary form printed it on Tuesday evening from fields the office had filled for other reasons; it travelled back once and arrived about thirty-two hours older. Its information closes on itself. This is explained in Book 2, not an unidentified-sender hook. The later record at 16:41 is Derek's refusal; it did not print that sheet.
- **The Paddington slip is a separate open item.** It remains in drawer three without a known sender or route, and with no ring on it yet. The impossible platform remains in the doorway after the case is saved.
- **The old Paris/Louvre room and recurring guard are not in the active Book 1 draft.** Treat the detailed Louvre descriptions in older notes as discarded planning, not canon.

## People and relationships at the close

- Derek and Sophie have begun repairing trust. He kept the agreement to meet her at the ordinary school gate after school, not during double maths. Sophie remains twelve, exacting, and the owner of her notebooks and clock evidence.
- Derek and Tuppence are not reunited. Tuppence has agreed to an ordinary Wednesday school pickup; preserve that small, new trust.
- Derek and Tabitha have acknowledged the old shut-door evenings; they kissed once after Book 2's case closed. The Bureau door remains physically unable to shut because the platform occupies its hinge. Do not turn that obstruction into a renewed rejection.
- Penny is booked to fly London–Athens, Thursday week, at 07:40, landing 13:05 local. She and Weinstein exchanged an understated, mutually understood message about the flight. Their connection is not yet a declared courtship. She also keeps the photograph face down when a case frightens her.
- Weinstein lives and works in Munich and takes real trains. He has no London office door. Herbert Matkowski is his former mathematics professor. Trevor's surname is **Boltzman**; Herbert's is **Matkowski**.

## Open threads to carry consciously

- Who or what is behind Case 1047's still-open device 3/LOCALHOST account?
- Who sent the Sunday Paddington slip, and where did it travel from?
- Sophie's notebook page about Julian remains private. In Book 1 Julian did not read it and left the note “DIDN'T LOOK. — J.”; Tuppence later says there is a dated heading she will not disclose. Book 2 does not reveal that heading.
- Tuppence's eleven-minute pause in the car before entering her house is part of her history; her previous longest was nineteen minutes after a 2022 war-memorial meeting. She still does not discuss that meeting.
- Derek keeps a private envelope labelled `NOT YET`; it contains six dated pages before he adds a seventh, recording Sophie's taking his arm. The manuscripts do not explain the envelope's larger purpose; do not conflate it with CASEBOOK or the morning sheet.
- The Bureau's impossible platform and Sherlock's remote, telepathic consultations remain unexplained by design. Derek and Penny close investigations; Sherlock may witness or comment, not take over as detective.

## Book 3 seed (proposal, not canon)

`SERIES_SEAM.md` contains a proposed fire case: residents share a mistaken recollection that the alarm preceded a blackout, while records put the blackout first. Its thematic question is how independent evidence differs from copied testimony. Treat every plot detail as optional until drafted. Before using the mains-hum timestamp method, verify the proposed forensic technique and accessible evidence path; do not let the science outrun what a phone recording and grid logs can actually establish. The case must not resolve LOCALHOST, the morning sheet, or the Paddington slip merely because they are open.

## Working rules

Preserve British spelling, one speaker per dialogue paragraph, the established dry series voice, and the distinction between well-tested physics, open research, and invented anomalies. Give the next book a new physical question and a fair, observable test. Don't reset settled relationships or turn an unexplained series hook into a convenient answer without building the mechanism on-page.
