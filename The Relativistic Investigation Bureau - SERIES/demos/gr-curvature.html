Pending Geometry interactive

A companion demo for The Murder That Hadn't Happened Yet. Barbarian's garden froze because something nearby was curving spacetime enough to matter. Drag the mass slider and watch a free-falling test particle follow the straightest possible path through a geometry that isn't flat anymore — no force required, just curvature. This is what Derek meant by "gravity is geometry, not a grudge."

Central mass (the "clock") Free-falling test particle Curved coordinate grid Geodesic path traced

The grid lines aren't decoration — they're what "straight" looks like near a mass. The particle never gets pushed. It just follows the only path available to it, the same way Derek's case kept "curving" back toward Sophie no matter which direction he investigated.

Push the mass up and shrink the orbit and you'll eventually see the particle spiral in rather than circle — the point where "orbit" stops being a meaningful word, much like the point where Case 1047 stopped being a meaningful murder.
