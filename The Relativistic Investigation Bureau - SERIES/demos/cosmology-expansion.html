Everywhere, Simultaneously interactive

A companion demo for The Murder That Hadn't Happened Yet. No galaxy is at the center of the expansion — every point sees every other point receding, which is exactly the kind of "nobody is the exception" geometry the Bureau's filing system kept failing to understand. Drag the scale factor forward and watch light redshift as space itself stretches underneath it.

Galaxies (fixed comoving positions) Your galaxy (the observer) Light ray in transit

Pick any galaxy as "home" — the picture looks the same from all of them. Nothing is moving through space toward or away from anything else; space itself is stretching, carrying galaxies apart the way a raisin loaf carries raisins apart as it bakes. Nobody is at fault, and nobody is in the center.

Notice the recession speed can exceed c for the farthest galaxies — that's not a violation of relativity, since nothing is moving through space faster than light. It's the same distinction Weinstein kept insisting on: the map is allowed to stretch faster than anything is allowed to travel across it.
