Case 1047 interactive

A companion demo for The Murder That Hadn't Happened Yet (The Relativistic Investigation Bureau). Drag the velocity slider to see why Derek's photograph, Weinstein's clock, and Mrs Marsh's filing cabinet cannot agree on which event happened first — and why that disagreement is physics, not misconduct.

Derek's worldline (rest frame, x=0) Moving observer's worldline (v) Light cone (45°, c = 1) Line of simultaneity (moving frame)

The dashed light cone marks the absolute boundary of cause and effect — everything Case 1047 actually depends on. The tilted amber line shows "now" according to the moving observer: as v increases toward c, their idea of "at the same time" swings further from Derek's, exactly the way Weinstein's Munich clock and Derek's London clock stopped disagreeing about which second was real.

At v = 0, everyone agrees on "now" — the amber line is flat, and Case 1047 really would just be a murder. Push v toward c and watch the tilt approach 45°: at that limit, "before" and "after" stop meaning anything at all, which is precisely the gap the Bureau's filing system fell into.
