PROMOTIONAL WINDOWS:
☐ Plan Kindle Countdown Deal for days 30-45 (one free day)

  - Usually drives significant Amazon algorithm boost
  - Drives readers to Book 2 notification
☐ Consider running ads on Amazon (Amazon Advertising)
  - Budget: $5-$20/day for first month
  - Monitor ACOS (Ad Cost of Sales)
  - Cancel if ACOS > 50%

☐ Prepare Book 2 announcement
☐ Start pre-launch buzz for Book 2

================================================================================
IMPORTANT REMINDERS
================================================================================

- Your cover is your sales engine. Invest in quality.
- First customer review is crucial for visibility.
- 80%+ finish rate on Book 1 = strong Book 2 sales potential.
- KDP dashboard data is your friend. Check it regularly.
- Be patient. First month is about building foundation.
- Series success depends on Book 1 quality + Book 2 momentum.
- Respond professionally to all reviews (even negative ones).
- Never discount too much; it trains readers to wait for sales.
