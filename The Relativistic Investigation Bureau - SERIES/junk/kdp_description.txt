ABOUT THE BOOK
A murder case with one small complication: it hasn’t happened yet.

At 9:17 on a Tuesday morning, investigator Derek Gent receives an email informing him that he has been murdered. The message is polite, precise, and signed by Derek himself. Worse, the attached photograph shows Derek lying dead on the floor of his own office, timestamped tomorrow at 11:03 a.m.

The clocks in the room disagree.
The universe disagrees.
The paperwork, unfortunately, does not.

To solve a murder scheduled in the future, Derek must understand what the universe is doing—and why
