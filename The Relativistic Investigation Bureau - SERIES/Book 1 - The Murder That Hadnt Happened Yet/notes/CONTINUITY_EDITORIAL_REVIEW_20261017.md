## Changes made

- **Chapter 6:** removed the second full explanation of the apple/photograph comparison. The new exchange keeps the gravity idea and applies it directly to the photograph’s limits as evidence.
- **Representation:** removed the generic accent label from Weinstein’s introduction; his speaking rhythm remains individual, without spelling accent into the dialogue.
- **Figures:** added a visible note that the apple diagram’s curved grid is schematic. The active metric-equation PNG is correct; an unused JPG with a duplicate, erroneous equation was moved to the local `bak/` archive.
- **Chapter 7:** explained geodesic incompleteness through the path a model can describe and a clock the reader can imagine, while retaining the scientific term and finite proper-time idea.
- **Production:** added REV18 source/DOCX/EPUB builders. The DOCX builder now follows the repository's Kindle-proportioned page and image-width and available-page-height requirements. The updated DOCX was built, but page rendering was unavailable because no LibreOffice executable is installed on this host.
- **Continuity and handoff:** REV14 remains the preserved release master. REV18 is the active review candidate; REV17 is superseded. No new plot, character, age, or marketing claim was introduced, so the listing copy remains accurate.

## Recommendation

Recommended for readers who enjoy humane, wry mysteries in which real physics changes how the clues must be read. The stated 14+ guidance is appropriate for the length and some dense optional material; interested strong readers aged 12–13 may benefit from adult support with the physics sections.
