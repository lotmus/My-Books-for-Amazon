PROLOGUE
In Which a File Arrives Before Its Author
A warning is only useful if it changes what you do. A warning that changes what you do may remove the reason anyone sent it. The Bureau had a name for most contradictions. It did not yet have a name for a sender who had not been born.
Rule: A message from outside your past is not a message you can file as received. Find out what it is before you call it advice.

COURSE
Tense for Investigators
A short course from the Relativistic Investigation Bureau
Book Two's case was not Case 1047. Three labels for one event are the previous file. This one is one message and two acts, and the acts do not share a tense.
You should finish able to say, without this page: a cause has to sit in the past of its effect; being in the same room does not let you reorder what already happened; a warning can be late for one act and early for the next; a fixed number of minutes is the size of the miss, not a force; a birthday is the wrong field for an author who is an act; a loop is a trap you do not need if ordinary order already explains the paper.
The story can be read without this course. The course is how you check the story.
Six lessons. Chapters 1 to 6 follow them in order. Chapters 7, 8, and the epilogue use Lesson 6, because by then the only honest work left is the close. No Lorentz transformation is repeated here. If you want that algebra, it is in Book One, and it was the wrong tool for a door that had already opened.

Lesson 1
Too Late Is Not a Frame
Learning objective: Say why a later event in the same room cannot be the cause of an earlier one, and why a change of frame will not rescue it.
Special relativity lets the time-order of some pairs flip. Those pairs are spacelike: nothing you can ride, light included, connects them. Two events in one office, seventeen minutes apart, are not that pair. They are timelike. Every inertial observer agrees which one happened first. Munich does not get a vote.
The door was seen at 10:05. The warning was read at 10:22. The reading is not in the past of the seeing. It cannot have prevented the opening. Calling this a coordinate problem is what Herbert did, and the coordinate was already in the right order. The remaining fact is lateness, which is ordinary.
Keep this: same room, minutes apart, order fixed.
Common trap: reaching for a frame change because the book before this one used frames. That tool answers a different question.
Quick test: A notice pinned at 18:17 tells you not to open a door at 18:00. Can it be the cause of the door staying shut? No.

Lesson 2
One Message, Two Acts
Learning objective: Assign a separate tense to each act a warning names.
A warning is not a single moment. It is a message paired with an act. Pair it with the door: the message comes after, so the sign is late. Pair it with boarding: the message comes before, so the sign is early. Nothing in that requires the two acts to be the same event. They are not. That was the other case.
Keep this: tense belongs to the pair, not to the paper alone.
Common trap: averaging the signs into one mood called "the warning was mysterious." Mystery is not a tense.
Quick test: The paper is in your hand. The door is open. The train has not been boarded. Which pair can still change what you do? The train.

Lesson 3
Seventeen Minutes
Learning objective: Use the size of a gap without promoting the number.
Sunday's notice was seventeen minutes after Sunday's act. Monday's reading was seventeen minutes after the door was seen. The repetition is a clue that the file misses by a characteristic amount. It is not a force, a curse, or a reason to reorder cause and effect. Four minutes, or an hour, would have had the same sign and the same conclusion. You refuse what is still early. You admit what is already late.
Keep this: sign first, magnitude second.
Common trap: building a cult of the number because an earlier case was fond of seventeen. Fondness is not a mechanism.
Quick test: The gap is four minutes late instead. Do you board? Still no. Do you claim you shut the door in time? Still no.

Lesson 4
The Wrong Field
Learning objective: Explain why a date of birth is the wrong datum for this author.
The form asked for a birthday because it assumed a person. A person who has not been born is not in the past of the paper, so the form correctly refused tomorrow. A person who has been born, if he is the subject of the warning, cannot be filed as the sender of his own prohibition without a loop. The author that satisfies ordinary order is the refusal: an act with a time, after the door, before any boarding, recorded at 16:41. Acts have times. They do not have birthdays.
Keep this: TIME OF ACT can be in the past of the record even when DATE OF BIRTH cannot.
Common trap: searching the building for a baby because the field said not yet born. The field was the wrong question.
Quick test: What did the form accept in the end, a birth or a time? A time.

Lesson 5
The Loop You Don't Need
Learning objective: State the closed-curve trap, and why this file did not require it.
If later Derek posts the warning, he learned the sentence from a life in which he boarded, or from a life in which he did not. The first makes the warning a record of failure. The second never teaches him the sentence. That paradox is real in the sense that stories get stuck in it. It is not required here. Ordinary order already accounts for the paper: late for the door, early for the boarding, authored by the refusal you can timestamp. Hawking's chronology protection conjecture says the laws we have do not let closed timelike curves form. This office did not test that conjecture. It declined to explain a lost-property slip by means of one.
Keep this: do not hire a loop when a clock and a refusal will do.
Common trap: treating "unborn sender" as a child. A blank is not a birth. Nearness of a name is not parenthood and not guilt.
Quick test: Did anyone in this case have to travel into their own past for the file to close? No.

Lesson 6
Capstone: File the Later Time
Learning objective: Close a case of this kind in one paragraph.
Write the earlier act first. Admit you are late for it. Write the later act second. Refuse it while the sign is still early. Put the author after the act he cannot change and before the act he can, and give the author a time rather than a birthday. If a name appeared in an empty person-field, check whether that person had an act. If they had none, strike the name. Do not strike it to make the page calm.
The control is a real departure: a passenger on the platform before the doors shut, a train that leaves after the boarding. If your file cannot be put in that order, you have not finished.
Keep this: earlier time left in the past, later time made past on purpose, by a refusal.
Common trap: congratulating yourself for not boarding and leaving a child's name in the recipient line because the board was frightening. Frightening is not a finding.
Quick test: Say the close without looking up. Door at 10:05, reading at 10:22, late. Boarding refused before it happened, early. Author is the refusal at 16:41. Sophie had no act. The name comes off.

GLOSSARY
Cause. What sits in the past of its effect. If it doesn't, it is not the cause of that effect, however official the paper looks.
Early. The sign on a warning that still comes before the act you pair it with. You can still refuse.
Late. The sign on a warning that comes after the act you pair it with. You can record it. You cannot undo it.
Seventeen minutes. The size of the miss in this file, twice. Not a force. See Lesson 3.
Tense per act. The rule that one message gets a separate before-or-after for each act it is paired with.
Time of act. The field that replaced a birthday. An author who is a refusal has one of these and does not have the other.
Pending author. A blank the form held while it was still asking for a person. Closed by naming the refusal and the time it was recorded.
Recipient. Whoever a file claims the next warning is for. Empty person-fields attract the nearest name. Nearness is not an act. Strike only when the act is known and is not theirs.
Closed curve. A history in which a signal runs back along a life and helps produce the signal. A known trap in this subject. Not the mechanism of this case.
Chronology protection. Hawking's conjecture that the laws we have do not let closed timelike curves form. Named in Lesson 5 so you do not confuse a conjecture with the subtraction that closed the file.
LOCALHOST. The originating address of the photograph in Case 1047. Not the sender of this paper. Still unidentified. Not solved by being mentioned.
YESTERDAY. On the board, a direction. On the calendar of this opening, Sunday. They are not the same object.
