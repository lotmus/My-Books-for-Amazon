# Book 3 seeds (filed 2026-10-09)

Three pieces from the In Love with Murder, Actually material that did not belong in Book 2 but could earn a place in Book 3. Lothar decided on 9 Oct 2026 to keep them as seeds. None of them is canon until a Book 3 manuscript makes it so.

The sources are in `notes/Material from In Love with Murder/`. Line numbers refer to `Set aside for possible Book 2.md`.

## Rules every seed has to obey (from `SERIES_SEAM.md`)

- The anomalous railway is never explained. A seed may not add a device that explains it, or a second explained impossibility next to it.
- Seventeen is a measurement, not a force.
- The morning sheet's sender stays blank. LOCALHOST stays unexplained. Sophie is never the unborn author.
- The seam already proposes a Book 3 case: eleven residents' statements about a Thursday-evening fire ("the alarm, then the dark"), contradicted by two logs that put the power cut first by forty seconds. The seeds below are written to sit beside that case, not replace it.
- No In Love with Murder names. Tobias, Catarina, Fournier, Ana Vale, Inês, Fernanda, Zé, the Gastons, Hale, Delacroix, Mara and Hugo stay in that book. The places Saint-Oran and Les Aulnes stay there too.

## Seed 1: the doors that appear

**Source:** set-aside Book One ch12 (lines 85-117), ch14 (lines 203-243) and ch20 (lines 425-477). A narrow green door appears in a corridor that "lengthens by six feet". The skirting board runs behind it and stops "in a clean right angle". It opens onto the same corridor it came from.

**Why it might fit Book 3:** the RIB doorway already holds a platform. A second door that opens onto *the same room from the other side* is a geometry joke, not a new mechanism: it is the closed curve from Book 2's glossary, built in plaster. Mrs Marsh would treat it as a lease issue ("a door is a fixture"), and Mr Quayle would measure it and write OUTSIDE DEMISE again.

**Risk:** two anomalies in one building. Use it only if it *is* the railway (another face of the same unexplained thing), and only if nobody explains why it appeared.

## Seed 2: the horn "for the sentence you cannot say"

**Source:** set-aside Book One ch12 (lines 89-117). A curved silver horn, forearm length, plaque worn to FOR THE SENTENCE YOU CANNOT SAY. When someone starts a refusal and does not finish it, the horn sounds and the house makes room for the unfinished sentence.

**Why it might fit Book 3:** the fire case is about statements that were shaped afterwards by telling. An object that marks the moment someone *stopped* saying what they saw is a strong visual for "the first statement versus the agreed statement".

**Risk:** Book 2 ends on Derek's own refusal, and a magic horn that does refusing for you would cheapen it. If used, it has to be an ordinary object that the residents *believe* in (a fire warden's horn, a hearing trumpet in a dead tenant's flat). It must never actually make a door. Penny should be allowed to say so.

## Seed 3: the unscheduled night-train stop

**Source:** set-aside Book Two ch23 "The Night Train" (lines 2541-2575). A sleeper slows at a station with no name on the board: one lamp, a bench, a timetable case, a door in the retaining wall with no building behind it, and a paper notice reading WAIT HERE IF YOU ARE EXPECTED. The platform clock shows 6:42. "There are no stops between here and the border."

**Why it might fit Book 3:** it is the closest thing in the material to the RIB platform, and the notice is a perfect Bureau sentence: it addresses the reader without a sender. It could open Book 3 as a cold open, a passenger statement filed with the Bureau from a real train.

**Constraints:**
- Do not use the Sud Expresso for it. That train was suspended on 17 March 2020, and Weinstein has already used it, in the past tense, in Book 2 Ch2. Use a train that runs at the time of the story (check this before drafting), or set the statement before 2020 and say so.
- Its board must not explain itself. "Previous station" (Book 2 Ch20) is the furthest the railway goes.
- Decide first whether the anomalous railway may appear outside the Holborn building. That is a series decision for Lothar, and this seed depends on it.
- 6:42 is In Love with Murder's recurring time. Give the RIB version its own time, and do not make it seventeen.

## Not seeds

Ada the android, the Vatican and Swiss Guard set pieces, the time device that "works on Tuesdays", Hugo's courtroom stunts, the amnesiac waiter and the second epilogue stay out. The reasons are in `notes/Material from In Love with Murder/INTEGRATION_NOTES_ILWM.md`.
