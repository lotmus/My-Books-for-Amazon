# What was used from "Material from In Love with Murder" (2026-10-09)

RIB Book 2 is a closed, three-day London case (Monday to Wednesday) with Munich phone calls. It has one anomaly, the platform, which by series rule is never explained, and its solution is the printer loop. Material that would add a second impossible device, move the story abroad, or change the series' one-anomaly logic was not forced in.

## Used

| Source | Where in RIB2 | How |
|---|---|---|
| Night train / Hendaye sleeper (ILWM surreal ch38; set-aside Book Two ch23) | Ch2 | Turned into Weinstein's true anecdote about the border clocks on the Sud Expresso in 1992. The romance, cool-box and customs gags stay with ILWM. Facts corrected: the service ended in 2020, so it is told in the past. The real route ran along the western end of the Pyrenees by way of San Sebastián rather than through "the longest tunnel in the Pyrenees", and the line crossed two borders, not three. |

## Left out, with reasons

- **Appearing rooms and doors, the magic horn ("for the sentence you cannot say"), the reshaping house.** These would be a second impossible device beside the platform, and the Seam rules that impossible objects are never mechanically explained. The horn also overlaps the book's own theme, refusal as an act, and would weaken Derek's "No." Possible Book 3 material only.
- **Ada the android.** RIB's only machine is CASEBOOK, a dumb form. An android would bring in a mechanism the series has avoided, and the adult scene does not fit the tone.
- **The unscheduled night-train stop** ("WAIT HERE IF YOU ARE EXPECTED", platform clock 6:42). It echoes the RIB platform, but using it would imply that the anomalous railway has appeared elsewhere. That is a series decision, so it was not added. Kept as a candidate Book 3 seed.
- **Swiss Guard / Vatican set pieces (Brunner, the umbrella, Leonardo's "historical correction apparatus").** A time machine that "works on Tuesdays" contradicts the RIB rule that nothing is explained by a device. It is also set in Italy, France and Bavaria, which have no place in a London case.
- **Hugo's courtroom stunts.** RIB2 has no courtroom, and Sophie already fills the precise-child role. The draft's "Article 331" does not say what it claims: the real Article 331 of the French Code de procédure pénale covers how witnesses testify at the assize court. So it could not be used as written.
- **Amnesiac Armand Vial as a wedding waiter; the second epilogue (offspring).** These are ILWM plot and character payoffs with no RIB counterpart.
- **Zoo break-out, Bavarian interlude, Zarathustra hermit, fever-dream chapter, animals.** Wrong register and setting.

## Name collisions

- No ILWM names were brought in. The added lines use only RIB's own cast (Weinstein, Trevor, Pendleton).
- The surreal ILWM draft has a chapter "Margaret and Derek". That Derek is not Derek Gent. If that chapter is ever reused anywhere, rename him.
