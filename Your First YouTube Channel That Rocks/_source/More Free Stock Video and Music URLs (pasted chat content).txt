**Important Note on Licenses:**
"Royalty-free" means you generally pay a one-time fee (or no fee if it's free) to use the music indefinitely, without having to pay royalties each time it's used. However, it doesn't always mean "attribution-free." Always double-check the specific license for each track you download to ensure you comply with its terms, especially if you're using it for commercial purposes. Look for licenses like CC0 (Public Domain), Creative Commons (CC BY, CC BY-SA, etc. – check the specific version), or a simple "free to use" statement from the website.

Remember, even with "royalty-free" music, it's always best practice to briefly check the licensing terms on the specific download page to ensure you're compliant, especially for commercial projects.
