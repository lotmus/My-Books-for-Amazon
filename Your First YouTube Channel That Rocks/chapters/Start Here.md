# Start here

*A channel that rocks is one a stranger chooses, finishes, and comes back to. YouTube pays for that behavior through programs with published gates. This book is the route from the first upload to those gates, and then through every income stream a channel can use.*

## How to read this book: four layers

You do not need every chapter today. Each chapter opens with a layer label, so you know what you can ignore for now.

| Layer | What it covers | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Start now | The viewer, the click, cheap production, recording, the first eight videos, the first six weeks | Start this week, chapters 1–6 |
| Use after publishing | Reading Studio’s counts, growing toward the gate, affiliate links and direct sponsors, fixing a stalled channel | Chapters 7, 8, 13 (§I, III–V), 14 (§I–II) |
| Set up before monetization | The two gates, the review, and the rules that can switch the money off | Chapters 9 and 10 |
| Use after eligibility | Ads, RPM, Premium, the Shorts pool, fan funding, Shopping, Creator Partnerships, spreading the income | Chapters 11, 12, 13 (§II, VI), 14 (§III–VII) |

If you already post, start with chapter 14, §I. It finds the earliest break in the channel and sends you to the chapter that fixes it.

## How claims are labeled

Every claim in this book is one of four kinds. Where the kind is not obvious from the citation, the text names it.

- **Official rule.** A requirement, limit, or number YouTube publishes in its Help Center, its policies, or its blog, cited where it is used. Thresholds, revenue shares, character limits.
- **Documented platform guidance.** Advice YouTube publishes, such as its title tips or its recommendation-system page. YouTube’s own words, but advice, not a rule.
- **Creator heuristic.** What creators commonly report. YouTube does not publish it. Treat it as a test.
- **Author recommendation.** This book’s method: the eight videos, the six weeks, the sheets, the order of fixes.

Dollar amounts in worked examples are invented arithmetic, not forecasts.

## The fact that shapes the plan

> **Thresholds change.** Before planning around any monetization target, check the live Earn tab in YouTube Studio and YouTube’s official Partner Program eligibility page for your country. This book reflects information checked in October 2026.

Official rule. On 10 August 2026 YouTube announced, on [its blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-partner-program-updates-2027-new-opportunities-earn/) and on its [Changes to the YouTube Partner Program](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009) help page, that from 1 February 2027 new applicants for ads and Premium revenue need 8,000 qualified watch hours in the last 365 days, or 20 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days, plus 1,000 subscribers. Until then the gate is 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 hours or 10 million Shorts views. Channels already in the program keep their status, and the 500-subscriber tier for fan funding and Shopping does not change. A channel that will not reach 4,000 hours before 1 February 2027 should plan for 8,000. Chapter 9 has the details.
