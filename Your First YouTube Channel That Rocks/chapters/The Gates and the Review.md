*A working guide for creators who want a channel, not a hobby*

# The Gates and the Review

*YouTube pays through two gates with published numbers. Meeting a number starts a review. It does not start the payment.*

*Layer: Set up before monetization. Workbook: the money sheet (chapter 15, §V) starts here, with hours and costs before any income.*

Every chapter before this one moves three counts. This chapter is what YouTube does with them. Everything below is an official rule from YouTube’s Help Center or blog, checked in October 2026, unless it is labeled otherwise. In 2026 the rules changed: from 1 February 2027 the ad gate doubles for new applicants.

## I. Before either gate

YouTube’s ads on your videos, before you are accepted, are not your revenue. [YouTube’s eligibility page](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851) says YouTube may serve ads on any content, and a channel that has not met the higher criteria is not yet eligible to receive a share.

That does not make the early views worthless. Every public long-form hour and every qualified Shorts view inside the window counts toward the gate. Start the money sheet now anyway: the hours and the costs are real before the income is.

## II. The first gate: fan funding and Shopping

In countries where YouTube offers the expanded program, the first gate opens channel memberships, Super Chat, Super Stickers, Super Thanks, and Shopping. It does not include a share of watch-page ads. [YouTube’s monetization overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/94522) and [how to earn money on YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72857) set it at:

| Requirement | Expanded program (first gate) |
| --- | --- |
| Subscribers | 500 |
| Recent uploads | 3 public uploads in the last 90 days |
| Watch time or Shorts | 3,000 qualified long-form watch hours in the last 365 days, or 3 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days |
| Age | At least 18 |

> **Thresholds change.** Before planning around any monetization target, check the live Earn tab in YouTube Studio and YouTube’s official Partner Program eligibility page for your country. This book reflects information checked in October 2026.

YouTube’s [Changes to the YouTube Partner Program](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009) page says these thresholds do not change in 2027, and that fan funding, Creator Partnerships, and Shopping continue to start at this level.

> **Country availability.** The expanded program, the Partner Program itself, and each fan-funding feature are offered country by country. In a country without the expanded program, these features open at the second gate. Check the lists on the overview page and on each feature’s eligibility page for the country set in your channel.

## III. The second gate: ads, Shorts, and Premium

The second gate adds a share of watch-page ads, Shorts Feed ads, and YouTube Premium revenue, on top of the fan-funding features.

| Applying | Subscribers | Long-form route | Shorts route |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Through 31 January 2027 | 1,000 | 4,000 qualified watch hours in the last 12 months | 10 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days |
| On or after 1 February 2027 | 1,000 | 8,000 qualified watch hours in the last 365 days | 20 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days |

> **Thresholds change.** Before planning around any monetization target, check the live Earn tab in YouTube Studio and YouTube’s official Partner Program eligibility page for your country. This book reflects information checked in October 2026.

YouTube announced the change on [its blog on 10 August 2026](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-partner-program-updates-2027-new-opportunities-earn/), and its Changes page states it in full: new creators need 8,000 hours or 20 million Shorts views, “in addition to still needing 1k subscribers.” Channels already in the program keep their status.

What qualifies is narrow. Official rule: Shorts-feed hours, hours from private, unlisted, and deleted videos, ad-campaign views, and live streams that are unlisted, deleted, or not converted to video on demand do not count. Image posts that appear in the Shorts feed do not count as Shorts views.

Author recommendation: a channel well short of 4,000 hours in October 2026 should plan for 8,000. YouTube’s pages present the new numbers as entry thresholds from 1 February 2027. This book reads that as: a channel that meets the current numbers and applies before that date applies under them. Confirm in the Earn tab when you apply.

## IV. What else you need

Both gates sit on the same checklist. The eligibility page lists:

- following the channel monetization policies (chapter 10);
- living in a country or region where the program is available;
- no active Community Guidelines strikes;
- 2-Step Verification on the Google Account;
- advanced features access; and
- one active AdSense for YouTube account linked to the channel, or being ready to set one up in Studio.

The ad tier accepts a creator under 18 only with a legal guardian over 18 who handles payments through AdSense for YouTube. Fan funding needs you to be 18.

## V. Apply, then wait for the review

Meeting the number does not accept you. In Studio, open Earn and choose Apply Now. Accept the base terms. Set up an AdSense for YouTube account, or link an active one. Studio then shows the review step as in progress.

Automated systems and human reviewers look at the channel as a whole. The eligibility page says a decision typically takes about a month, longer when volumes are high or reviewers disagree. If the counts drop while you wait, the review still proceeds. Making videos private or deleting them can affect the application.

If the review says no, you can appeal within 21 days, or apply again after 30 days — after 90 days if this was not the first rejection. A rejection usually means reviewers found a significant part of the channel outside the policies. Read chapter 10 against your own uploads before you reapply. More uploads of the same kind will not change the answer.

## VI. Inside: modules, payments, and staying active

Acceptance opens a menu, not a payment. In Earn, each way to earn is a module with its own terms:

- **Watch Page Monetization Module** — ads and Premium revenue on long-form and live videos;
- **Shorts Monetization Module** — Shorts Feed ads and Premium revenue on Shorts, accruing from the date you accept it; and
- **Commerce Product Module** — memberships, Super Chat, Super Stickers, and Super Thanks.

Accept the modules you will use, then switch on monetization per video (chapter 11).

> **Taxes.** Payment comes through AdSense for YouTube once the balance passes the payment threshold for your currency. Complete the tax information AdSense for YouTube asks for in your account before you expect a payment. Treat everything the channel earns, fan funding included, as income, and ask a tax adviser how it is taxed where you live.

Two dates from the Changes page. Creators must accept the updated terms in Studio by 31 January 2027 to keep monetizing fully; if they do not, the affected features stop earning on 1 February 2027 until they accept, and the channel stays in the program. Creators who turned on fan funding before 2023 under the older Commerce Product Addendum must accept the Commerce Product Module by the same date.

Staying in also takes activity. Today the eligibility page says YouTube may turn off monetization on a channel that has not uploaded a video or posted to the Posts tab for six months or more. From 1 February 2027, the Changes page says a channel counts as active if it has any one of: 1,000 qualified watch hours in the past 365 days, 1 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days, or two long-form videos or five Shorts uploaded every 90 days. A channel that drops below gets an extended 90-day window to restore active status — through the hours or the Shorts views, not through uploads alone.

## VII. A second channel starts at zero

Running a second channel is allowed. It does not inherit the first one’s monetized status. It clears the thresholds on its own — subscribers, watch hours, review — under whatever thresholds apply on the day it applies. Chapter 14, §V covers when a second channel is worth the split.

*In short:* Before the gate, YouTube keeps the ad money. The first gate opens fan funding and Shopping at 500 subscribers where offered. The second opens ads at 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 hours, or 8,000 hours for new applicants from 1 February 2027. Meeting the number starts a review.

## VIII. Claims that do not survive the eligibility page

- That hitting the number is approval. It starts the review.
- That the 2027 change affects every channel. It applies to new applicants for the ad tier. Channels already in the program, and the fan-funding gate, do not move. The Shorts floor and the activity rule apply to everyone.
- That a rejection is final. Appeal within 21 days, or reapply after 30 or 90 days. The fix is the policy, not the volume.
- That acceptance turns the money on. You accept modules, set up AdSense, and switch on monetization per video.
