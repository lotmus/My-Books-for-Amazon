# Start this week

*Layer: Start now. One page, five working days, then week two. Each day names the chapter that explains it and the workbook sheet that records it.*

**Day 1 — Name the viewer.** Write the viewer sentence: “This channel helps [specific person] who wants [specific result].” Write the poor-fit sentence: “This channel is a poor fit for people who want [different result].” Write twenty searchable ideas, each a question that viewer would type. Pick two: the “who it is for” video and the deep dive. Chapters 1 and 5. Viewer sheet.

**Day 2 — Set up the channel, then package video one.** Turn on 2-Step Verification on the Google Account. Set the audience setting, made for kids or not, at the channel level. Open Settings, Channel, Feature eligibility, and complete phone verification so advanced features can follow. Set the country truthfully. Then, for video one, write the title with the viewer’s phrase first, sketch a thumbnail you can read at phone size, and write the first thirty seconds word for word. Chapters 1, 2, and 4. Set-up sheet and video-job sheet.

**Days 3 and 4 — Record and finish.** Record with the microphone close, in the softest room you have, with the fridge off. Cut what does not teach. Add captions from your script, or review the automatic ones before you publish them. Add video chapters to the description: 00:00 first, at least three. Name the next video inside the video and on the end screen. Write an honest description: the first two lines say what the video does, and any sponsor or affiliate link is disclosed beside it. Chapters 2, 3, and 4. Video-job sheet.

**Day 5 — Publish, and leave the brand alone.** Publish video one. Do not redesign the banner, rename the channel, or start a logo this week. Write the date and what you published on the week sheet. Chapter 7.

**Week 2 — The deep dive, and three honest viewers.** Publish the deep dive. Show both videos to three people who match the viewer sentence, not three people who like you. Ask each one where they would have stopped watching. Write the three answers in the decision log, and change nothing else until you have them. Chapter 6 takes over from week three.

> **Before every upload:** read the license on anything you did not make, and set AI use, paid promotion, and the audience setting truthfully. The checklist is chapter 10, §XI.
