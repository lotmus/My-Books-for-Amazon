# Independent First-Week Reader Test

**Status:** Protocol prepared; no participant test has been completed.

## Purpose

Check whether a first-time creator can use the evening page, the six-week calendar, and the workbook without coaching. The test evaluates whether the method is understandable and actionable; it does not evaluate channel performance or promise growth.

## Participant and setup

Recruit one adult beginner who has not launched a regular video channel and is willing to plan a first video. Give them the REV23 manuscript and printable sheets. Allow 60–90 minutes for the first session. Ask them to think aloud. Do not explain the method, point out relevant chapters, or correct misunderstandings while they work. They may skip publishing or account changes; a completed upload plan is sufficient. If the listing is to claim older-teen suitability, run a separate session with a 16–17-year-old and parent or guardian, and check comprehension and comfort around account, payment, rights, and sponsorship decisions.

## Tasks to observe

Ask the reader to work from the evening page and Start this week, using the book as instructed:

1. Write one viewer sentence and a second sentence naming who the channel is not for.
2. Draft twenty titles and circle two that form a sensible first video and deep dive.
3. Complete a video-job sheet and explain how the eight planned videos connect.
4. Find the pre-upload checklist and explain the audience, paid-promotion, altered-content, and rights decisions.
5. Explain how they would ask three strangers for feedback, choose one change, and record the result.
6. Locate chapter 10, §XI before upload and find the current threshold-verification instruction.

## Observation record

For each task, record: completed independently / completed after rereading / needed a hint / unable to complete. Note the page or instruction where the reader paused, the exact words they misunderstood, what they expected to happen, and any step they guessed. Do not record channel views or growth as usability outcomes.

## Debrief

Ask: “What would you do next? Where did you get stuck? What did you have to guess? Which step felt unlike advice you have seen elsewhere? What would you want clarified before using this on your channel?” Separate observed difficulty from personal preference.

## Revision rule

Fix observed blockers in the instruction where they occur before adding new explanation elsewhere. Update this note with the participant date, anonymized reader profile, task outcomes, changes made, and unresolved questions. A prepared protocol is not evidence that the book has passed a reader test.
