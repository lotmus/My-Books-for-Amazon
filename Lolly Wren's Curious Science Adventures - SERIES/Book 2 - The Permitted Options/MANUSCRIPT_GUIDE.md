## Quotes and styles

Curly quotes and apostrophes only (`“` `”` `’`), and em dashes for interrupted speech.

When converting quotes, walk `w:t` nodes in document order and reset the previous character at every paragraph. A quote at the start of a paragraph opens. A quote immediately after an em dash, with no space, closes. Carrying the previous character across paragraphs turns every paragraph-initial quote into a closer.

`paragraph.runs` skips text inside a hyperlink. A full pass has to iterate `w:t`. Do not collapse runs in a paragraph that contains a hyperlink.

`First Paragraph` is the chapter-opening no-indent style. Leave it. Short displayed equations stay `Source Code`. A sentence that merely contains a formula stays Body Text.

Story prose does not say "this book", "either book", "two books", or "the book's business". A lesson may say "this book's fiction" or "Book One".

## How to edit the docx

Use python-docx. A save can raise `OSError: [Errno 22]` while OneDrive or Word has the file. Retry the same save.

PowerShell breaks on the apostrophe in `Lolly Wren's`. Put the script under `C:\Users\lomus\AppData\Local\Temp` and build the folder name with `chr(39)`. Do not use `H` as a function name. Do not chain git commands with `&&`.

Inserting after a paragraph copies that paragraph's style. After a Heading, set Body Text on the new paragraph or it enters the contents.
