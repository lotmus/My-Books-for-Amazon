================================================================================
OVERALL STRATEGY
================================================================================

**Suspense Arc:**
- Chapters 1-6: Plant questions, establish stakes aren't obvious
- Chapters 7-12: Escalate - characters discover things are worse than they thought
- Chapters 13-15: Maximum tension - they know the problem, unsure if they can stop it
- Chapters 16-18: Resolution with consequences

**Stoic Moments:**
- Mrs. Chain: Already has this; amplify her "accept reality, act anyway" voice
- Fainrose: Add moments where she accepts the terrible truth, then proceeds
- Lolly: Journey from noticing (passive) to accepting (and acting)
- Gideon: Stumbling into wisdom through acceptance of chaos
- Beatrix: Pragmatic stoicism - sees what needs doing

**Surprise Reactions:**
- Characters surprise each other with discoveries
- Characters surprised by their own courage/realizations
- Physical/emotional reactions that reveal personality
- Moments where characters realize they've known something all along
