Rather than choosing one system exclusively, I'd use them according to their strengths:

That gives us five or six distinct voices, but only two systems to manage.

Why Polly for the narrator?

This is where I'd spend the money/quality.

The narrator appears for essentially the entire book, and the dry literary voice is fundamental to the novel.

Brian is a very natural choice for the main storyteller.

And then Brian also narrates the Physics Notes, so the whole 217-page book has one consistent intellectual voice. The manuscript deliberately makes the Physics Notes a companion layer to the novel.

Why Piper for Lolly?

Because Lolly is going to have a lot of dialogue, and giving her a genuinely different voice makes conversations much easier to follow.

Cori High is the Piper voice I'd audition first.

Polly Emma for Mrs Chain

I like this pairing particularly well.

Mrs Chain has a very distinctive personality, and giving her a separate Polly voice means she won't get confused with Lolly or Fainrose.

Piper Jenny_Dioco for Beatrix

Again, a completely separate sound.

Beatrix's dialogue is often short and extremely dry. A distinct voice will make her instantly recognizable.
