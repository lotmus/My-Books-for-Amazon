SCHRÖDINGER'S PAPERWORK - ARC REVIEWER OUTREACH LIST
Build this list by searching Goodreads reviewers (goodreads.com/reviewers)

================================================================================
HOW TO USE THIS:
================================================================================

1. Search each book title on Goodreads
2. Go to "Reviews" section
3. Click "See all reviews" → sort by "Most Helpful"
4. For each reviewer, note their:
   - Goodreads username
   - Email (from profile, if public)
   - Last review date
   - Sample of their review style

5. Mark Status:
   - TO CONTACT = ready to email
   - CONTACTED = email sent, awaiting response
   - ACCEPTED = they want the ARC
   - READING = they opened the file
   - REVIEWED = they posted a review

6. Track dates: when you sent ARC, when they accessed it
