# Image Audit - 13 October 2026

## Verdict

The illustrations support the books. Book 1 has a distinctive, high-resolution illustrated cover and 19 clear teaching diagrams in the optional lessons. Their monochrome and restrained-colour diagrams fit the book's explanatory sections; the labels remain legible at full size and the graphics clarify the concept rather than decorate the story. Book 2 is intentionally text-led. Its strong departures-board cover communicates the premise, and the absence of interior diagrams does not leave a concept in the novel unexplained.

The audit found two fixable Kindle issues. Book 1's back-cover panel was embedded after the final reader page, while Book 2's designed back cover was not in its reading file. Both back-cover panels now appear on their own page at the very end of their respective DOCX and EPUB files. Descriptive alt text was added to Book 1's title illustration, 19 diagrams, and back-cover panel, and to Book 2's back-cover panel. At the author's requested Kindle display limit, all 19 Book 1 interior diagrams now measure no more than 800 x 440 pixels, scaled proportionally. Book 2 contains no interior diagrams. Front covers, title illustration, and final back-cover panels are cover art rather than diagrams and remain at their publication sizes.
