# Book 1 content review — REV7

Date: 17 October 2026  
Scope: current REV7 DOCX and rebuilt Kindle EPUB; continuity and content review informed by the prior full-reader, line, science, representation, and image audits. This is an internal review, not an independent physicist report or device-rendering sign-off.

## Verdict

**Recommend to readers who enjoy humane, bureaucratic comic science fiction.** The premise is distinctive and the satire keeps the human cost in view. Mrs Chain's case gives the quantum dispute consequences; Lolly's refusal to certify an answer she cannot defend pays off the story's ethical line. The consultants are most engaging when their competing models change what the characters can do.

REV7 fixes a continuity ambiguity: Phase Two and the care-home freeze now explicitly end Sunday morning. It keeps the newer REV6 interlude title, scanner beat, and more careful description of decoherence and einselection. Lolly's brief choice to look away from the care-home lounge now connects her private avoidance to the case without resolving it too neatly. The EPUB navigation and story text carry the same updates.

## Remaining improvement opportunities

1. In a future external cold read, mark any middle-scene explanation that neither changes a decision nor raises a cost; trim or stage only those passages. Do not cut lessons or scientific qualifications by default.
2. Have a physicist independently check the quantum-foundations explanations, especially measurement, decoherence/einselection, and the boundary between prediction and observation. Existing internal science review is positive but not a substitute for external expertise.
3. Run Kindle Previewer or a device check for navigation, image scaling, and the final back-cover panel. Structural EPUB validation cannot establish visual quality on every Kindle.

These are remaining quality gates, not known story-breaking errors. A universal 10/10 is not a credible promise; the score reflects this reviewer's stated audience and benchmark.
