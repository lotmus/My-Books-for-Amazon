## Open questions for Lothar (not changed)
1. **Book 1 author joke.** The title page says "By Quentin Edmund Darling (Q.E.D.), allegedly. / By Lothar J. Musiol, obviously.", and "A Note on the Author" presents Q.E.D. as a pseudonym. KDP's author field is now Lothar J. Musiol. Keep the joke, or simplify to plain "Lothar J. Musiol"?
2. **"Also by".** Only *Schrödinger's Paperwork* is listed in Book 2. Are there other published titles to add? Book 1 has no "Also by" (Book 2 isn't out yet), and its "About the Series" says Book 2 "is already waiting". Should it name *The Permitted Options* once it's live?
3. **Covers.** Book 1 root keeps `KINDLE_COVER_FINAL_2560.jpg`. The 1024/2024 versions, the inside and back covers and the Audible 1408² cover are in `bak\covers`. Confirm which is final. Book 2 has no cover and no KDP keywords file yet.
4. **Year anchoring (Book 2).** The bible says "no year", but the text is implicitly 2025–26: 65 years after 1961, the 1338/1347 widow, weekdays matching Tue 12 Aug 2025 and Mon 9 Feb 2026, Esaki 91 and Drill 88. Fine if intended. The countdown working days (148…108) were consistent in the spots checked.
5. **Ch5 supernova physics.** A 2% faster burn for six hours "advancing" core collapse by 11–14 thousand years is implausible. It also sits oddly with "within a few thousand years of limit" and the 206 cores "within 4,000 years". Suggest softening to "advanced by an amount nobody can bound" or similar.
6. **Ch6 "no computable answer for these nine stars".** This overstates Cubitt–Pérez-García–Wolf, which is about the general problem in the infinite-lattice limit. Lecture 6 now states it correctly. The story line could add "for all Ramanathan can show".
7. **Ch3 statute vs notebook.** "Schedule four, paragraph nine … you wrote the notebook they took that from" may conflict with the bible ("Schedule 4 is the moon form; the notebook is not a statute").
8. **Ch13 Drill.** He is 88 and built benches 1958–1994, but he speaks of complementarity as "my rule" while admiring "the man" who wrote the 1935 reply. Is Drill a Bohr figure or a disciple? It reads ambiguously.
9. **Ch15/16 "residue".** Ramanathan cites "a residue nobody could source, in my own words from March, that behaved like it belonged to two files at once", and "Eilstein's horizon … doing to my instruments for six months". Neither is set up in Ch1–4. Either plant a line in Ch2/Ch4 or soften it.
10. **Ch16 "All nine sit inside the uncertainty of van Casteel's correction".** The nine are stellar cores and the correction is a lab plate measurement. The link needs one explanatory clause.
11. **Ch11.** "10⁻¹²² vs 120 orders" is approximate (fine). The "his is four" sample line is unclear.
12. **Bible upkeep.** Viktor Eugenius, Szmulek Barbarian, Fenn Adair and Okonkwo-Barrett are not in the Book 2 people list.
13. **Bibliography.** It omits many works the lectures name (Bell, Bekenstein, Maldacena, Cubitt et al., MIP*=RE, Kochen–Specker, Bohr 1935, Sagawa–Ueda, Bérut, Toyabe, Jarzynski, Page–Wootters, STU 1993, Lamoreaux, Popescu–Rohrlich). Add them?
14. **Book 1 wording pass.** The "fashioned" artefacts suggest an earlier global synonym replacement. A slow read for similar stray substitutions is recommended (only clear cases were fixed today).
15. **Book 1 "How to Read".** "18 lectures in six parts. Chapter 14 has two of them" (there are 19 numbered lessons with 14a/14b). Fine if intended.
