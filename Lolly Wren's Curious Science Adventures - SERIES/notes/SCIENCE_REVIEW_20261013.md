# Science Review - 13 October 2026

## Scope and scores

Reviewed the current Book 1 Kindle master, `Book 1 - Schrodingers Paperwork/Schrodingers_Paperwork_BOOK_1_KINDLE_FINAL.docx`, and Book 2's reviewed continuity copy, `Book 2 - The Permitted Options/The_Permitted_Options_BOOK_2_REVISION_20261005.docx`, including their science back matter. This is a reader-facing accuracy and boundary review, not a claim that open research questions are settled.

- **Book 1: 10/10.** Established results and experimental findings are separated from interpretations and unresolved quantum foundations. The black-hole material identifies Hawking radiation as a robust prediction, notes that astrophysical emission has not been directly detected, and accounts for the cosmic microwave background when discussing a solar-mass hole. Bell violations are not presented as faster-than-light signalling or as selecting one interpretation.
- **Book 2: 10/10 after revisions.** The lessons distinguish theorems from the story's legal analogies, experimental effects from speculative proposals, and model calculations from astrophysical observation. The tunnelling, altered-vacuum, archive-authenticity, and wormhole mechanisms are identified as fictional extrapolations; the Casimir force is not treated as a usable macroscopic wormhole source.

## Revisions made

Book 2's second-incompleteness summary now states its consistency, formalisation, and arithmetic conditions. Its legal motion is described as a resemblance to self-reference rather than as Gödel's theorem acting on a government. The Page-curve discussion now calls replica-wormhole and island calculations theoretical results in controlled gravitational models, and explicitly says they are not observations of astrophysical black holes.

## Remaining limits

The black-hole information problem, island calculations, and black-hole complementarity remain theoretical. No fictional mechanism in either novel should be read as a demonstrated laboratory or astronomical effect. Those limits are now stated close to the relevant explanations.
