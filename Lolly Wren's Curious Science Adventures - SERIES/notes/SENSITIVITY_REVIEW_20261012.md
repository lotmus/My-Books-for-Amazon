# Sensitivity and Representation Review — 12 October 2026

## Scope and scores

Reviewed both active story manuscripts and their cast and continuity notes, with attention to nationality, regional identity, gender, age, disability, class, institutional portrayal, and the distinction between historical figures and fictional characters.

- **Schrödinger's Paperwork: 10/10.** The cast has distinct motives and agency; the British civil-service setting is grounded in specific institutions and places. Real physicists appear as disclosed fictional disguises, and the back matter separates story invention from historical identity. No group is presented as inherently foolish, dangerous, or morally uniform.
- **The Permitted Options: 10/10.** The international setting and scientific institutions are named concretely, while the invented Ministry remains recognizably fictional. Women in technical and administrative roles make consequential decisions. Regional references to Belfast, Aberdeen, Sligo, and Vienna are specific and not used to assign a stock personality.

The scores reflect the manuscripts after these fixes; no further actionable representation issues surfaced in this review.

## Fixes made

- Recast the Duc de Broccoli's speech description so his deliberate pace is a personal manner, not a judgment about his English proficiency.
- Kept John Bell's Belfast identity while removing the link between his accent and a combative personality.
- Introduced Dr Ramanathan and her colleagues by name and relationship rather than a count divided by gender; replaced a repeated "not looking up" gesture with a concrete instrument action.
- Replaced two references to van Casteel as "your Dutchman" with professional references, avoiding nationality as a shorthand for the character.

## Editorial judgment

The remaining identity markers serve place, biography, or historical context. The German officer's Eastern Front service, the Dutch printmaker reference, the Austrian theorem reference, and the regional crew members are specific contextual details, not personality templates. Minor crowd characters are brief by design; named recurring characters are given individual voices, choices, work, and consequences.
