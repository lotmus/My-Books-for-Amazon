“Because someone built a filter.” Now she turned to look at Lolly, and there was something close to fury in it, banked low. “Somewhere in that paperwork is a device (probably a receiver mast, modest and municipal) coupled to the observation quorum. It doesn’t touch the moon. It touches the town: damping the background contact that would decohere Outer Fenwick’s sky, releasing it again only once the forms say the humans have earned a definite one. Out of every shape a superposition can fall into, decoherence leaves only the dull, stable ones standing (einselection) and a moon that just hangs there being a moon is a pointer state: an answerthat survives being asked the same question by everything, constantly. This Ministry built a machine to interrupt that, so forty-one signatures could be sold back as the cure.”

2863|On the wavelength of a proton (from Lecture 12). A proton is about 1836 times more massive than an electron, so at a comparable speed its momentum, and therefore its wavelength, is nearly two thousand times shorter — its spreading and interference are correspondingly harder to see. That is the precise sense in which a nucleus behaves “more classically” than an electron, and it is why chemistry can lean on the Born–Oppenheimer approximation: treat the nuclei as nearly fixed points and give the electrons the full wavefunction treatment.
2864|A heavy particle is not thereby a classical one. Solar-core temperatures are nowhere near hot enough, classically, for protons to overcome their mutual electrical repulsion; the Sun fuses hydrogen only because protons can cross that Coulomb barrier by quantum tunnelling, the same barrier-crossing effect met in Lecture 6, applied here to stellar cores rather than event horizons. And the reason some isotopes are stable while their near neighbours are not is that protons and neutrons fill discrete nuclear energy shells, the same shell logic that organises electrons into atomic orbitals, just at nuclear, not atomic, energies.
2866|On the number nobody derived (from Lecture 18). Quantum electrodynamics runs on a single fixed dial: the fine-structure constant, α ≈ 1/137.036, which sets how strongly an electron and a photon interact. It is dimensionless — a pure number, with no units attached — because it is built from the electron’s charge, the speed of light, Planck’s constant, and the vacuum permittivity (a fixed number describing how easily an electric field penetrates empty space), combined in exactly the proportion needed to cancel every unit out:
2867|α = e² / (4πε₀ℏc)
2868|That combination fuses electromagnetism, quantum mechanics, and relativity into a single figure, and no theory has ever derived it from more basic principles. It has only ever been measured — currently to about ten significant figures — never explained. Richard Feynman called it “one of the greatest damn mysteries of physics,” and admitted nobody knows where the number comes from. Wolfgang Pauli spent decades obsessed with it and died, by a coincidence nobody has ever explained, in hospital room 137.
2869|It keeps company, in that respect, with Bellboy’s number and Mrs Chain’s seventy-eight per cent: a figure that is simply true, arrived at honestly, and owed to nobody’s theory.
2870|On the vacuum that is not empty (from Lectures 6 and 14). What a distant observer calls empty space around a hole is not a cleared desk. Quantum fields have a lowest-energy arrangement, and which arrangement counts as “lowest” depends on the observer’s motion and on the spacetime they inhabit. That is why Hawking radiation does not require a particle factory, and why the Unruh effect can warm an accelerating thermometer in a vacuum another observer still calls cold. The Ministry’s phrase for this would be “legacy emptiness.” It is not recommended.
2871|On the number that is a bound, not a score (from Lecture 13). Tsirelson’s 2√2 is not a target for staff appraisals. It is the most quantum mechanics itself will give you in a CHSH test. Local hidden variables cannot pass 2. Nature passes 2 and stops before 4. Any spreadsheet that prints a 4 has not discovered new physics. Check the sample, the selection rules, and the arithmetic before anyone commissions a new universe.
