## Fixes completed in REV12

- CHSH dialogue, lesson recaps, rules, and glossary now state independent-setting assumptions, the local-hidden-variable bound, and the limits on interpretation; no section implies that Bell tests eliminate every local account without assumptions. The clean run no longer claims every loophole has vanished; the lattice result is model-specific and does not prove houses are entangled.
- Bell terminology is qualified consistently in the dialogue, Lesson 13 recap, rule summaries, and glossary: experimental violations exclude local hidden-variable models under standard assumptions; they do not eliminate every possible account or select an interpretation.
- The Ministry’s quantum civic machinery is explicitly labelled fiction at the opening and in the Physics Prologue. Entanglement is defined by non-factorisable joint states without claiming that every local property lacks a value.
- The 78% result is renamed record coverage in the story and defined as the share of listed claims about Margaret corroborated by surviving independent records. It is not quantum-state fidelity, entropy, a fraction of Margaret, or the district’s 308/411 recovery rate.
- The Lesson 2 sieve-and-magnet comparison is replaced by sequential Stern–Gerlach measurements along the same and perpendicular axes.
- Quantum error correction is distinguished from the story’s classical, speculative records lattice. The lattice analogy is not presented as recovery engineering for people or homes.
- CHSH monogamy is confined to the stated three-party quantum bound and the Ministry’s fictional model.
- Entropy is described as a change; Brillouin’s information-centred reading is marked as interpretation; Landauer’s bound is tied to logically irreversible erasure under stated conditions.
- The Born–Oppenheimer approximation is explained through electron/nuclear timescale separation and potential-energy surfaces, with nuclear quantum effects retained.
- Holography and AdS/CFT are described as theoretical proposals and model-specific dualities, not evidence about our universe or a practical black-hole disclosure channel.
- Kindle output was rebuilt from REV12. The extra inline cover was removed; the front and back cover placements remain. ZIP/XML and navigation parse, all 20 in-reading images have alt text, the back cover is last, and all 19 interior diagrams are at most 800 × 440 px. EPUB size: 2,427,862 bytes.
