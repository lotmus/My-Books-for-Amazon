**Author:** Lothar J. Musiol

## Description

Book 6 owns conversion efficiency: regulators, switching converters, magnetics, the multiphase regulator that feeds a processor, isolation and gate drivers, uninterruptible supplies, distribution transformers and protection, grid-connected inverters, solar maximum-power-point tracking, wireless power transfer, and charging supplies for pulsed power.

Each chapter states its question, develops the idea in words, and works it through with declared numbers. Every number in a worked example was computed, and the answers to every practice problem are in the back of the book.
