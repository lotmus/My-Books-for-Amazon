ONE HOME (do not recompute elsewhere)
  Book 1  Phasors. Series R=30, L=40 mH, C=25 uF, 10 V rms,
          omega=1000: Z=30 ohm, I=1/3 A, S=10/3 W, Q=4/3,
          BW=750 rad/s. Off resonance omega=2000:
          |Z|=67.08 ohm, I=0.149 A. Square: odd 1/n.
          Triangle: odd 1/n^2. Wavelets do not replace
          H(j*omega). Solenoid law lives here.
  Book 2  Virtual short/open only in negative feedback.
          Follower is a wire, not an inverting buffer.
          Declared Bode: A0=1e5, GBW=1 MHz, pole=10 Hz.
          Noise gain 100 -> 10 kHz. 100 kOhm at 290 K:
          40 nV/sqrt(Hz). 200 Hz tracker = 5 ms. Safety
          layer wins. Inductor as a part, not Faraday.
  Book 3  Devices. FinFET then GAA. AlScN named as a film.
          The 2026 MEMS survey is Book 9.
  Book 4  Bowick then Pozar/Steer. mmWave is this book at
          shorter lambda. Friis at 2.45 GHz, 100 m,
          isotropic: space factor ~1.0e-8. Antenna gains
          live here. PA watts are Book 6. Laminate specs
          are Book 9.
  Book 5  Modem = bits to waveform. 6 GHz is 1200 MHz:
          three 320 MHz channels, 240 MHz left. Wi-Fi 7
          paper peak ~23 Gbit/s. 5G FR1 410 MHz-7.125 GHz,
          FR2 24.25-71 GHz. 6G wants 7.125-24.25 GHz.
          Mask/chamber is Book 7. PA is Book 6.
  Book 6  Chain: device -> load line -> class -> match ->
          bias -> heat -> combine -> filter -> line ->
          antenna -> EIRP/mask. 100 W: 50 V/5 V knee
          Ropt=10.13 ohm; 28 V/3 V knee Ropt=3.13 ohm.
          1 dB feed = 0.794 of power. Radar is R^4.
          PLL and receiver noise sit in this volume.
  Book 7  Loop field ~ area*I*f^2. 25 MHz clock, 100 MHz
          harmonic: 16x if current matched. CISPR 16 +
          C63.4 are methods. CISPR 32 / FCC 15 B are
          limits. EMC vs radio conformance: two reports.
          ADS/AWR/SPICE/2.5D/3D flow. 7layers and Hermon
          run the room. They do not design the board.
  Book 8  Waste = P(1/eta-1). 80 kW at 96% vs 98%:
          1.70 kW/rack, 1.70 MW per 1000 racks. 45 kW
          average: 0.96 kW. JLL 2026: ~100 GW new
          capacity, ~$30M/MW AI fit-out. Four magnetics
          shelves. Skin ~66/sqrt(f) mm. 875 A is Book 9.
  Book 9  HBM 2.8 TB/s vs DDR5-6400 51.2 GB/s ~ 55x.
          700 W / 0.8 V = 875 A. Join vs size vs skeleton
          vs interposer. FR-4 default; better for loss,
          Dk, heat, density. 10 GHz dielectric ballpark
          in the Book 9 file. Robots: VLA + Book 2
          tracker. MEMS 2026 survey only here.
