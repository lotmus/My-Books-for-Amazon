# KDP Description: Semiconductor Physics and Devices

**Title:** Semiconductor Physics and Devices
**Subtitle:** From the crystal and the band diagram to diodes, BJTs, MOSFETs, FinFET and GAA, compound semiconductors, fabrication, acoustic resonators, and compact models
**Series:** Electrical Engineering Series, Book 3 of 7
**Author:** Lothar J. Musiol
**Format:** Kindle (master file: Semiconductor_Physics_and_Devices_Book3.docx)

## Description

Book 3 explains why devices behave as their datasheets say: carriers, junctions, the bipolar and MOS transistors, scaling to FinFET and gate-all-around, GaAs, GaN, and InP, the fab steps that set device limits, the piezoelectric films behind RF filters, and the models that bring devices into a simulator.

Each chapter states its question, develops the idea in words, and works it through with declared numbers. Every number in a worked example was computed, and the answers to every practice problem are in the back of the book.
