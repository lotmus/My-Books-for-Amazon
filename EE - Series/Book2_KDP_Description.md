# KDP Description: Circuits, Components, and Control

**Title:** Circuits, Components, and Control
**Subtitle:** Op-amps, filters, real resistors, capacitors, and inductors, digital logic, oscillators and timing, and the classical control loop from PID to the root locus and stability margins
**Series:** Electrical Engineering Series, Book 2 of 7
**Author:** Lothar J. Musiol
**Format:** Kindle (master file: Circuits_Components_and_Control_Book2.docx)

## Description

Book 2 is built around the op-amp and the feedback loop. It covers analog and digital electronics, active filters, real passive components, comparators, instrumentation amplifiers, references, oscillators, digital timing and metastability, and control from PID and lead-lag design through digital control, state feedback, the root locus, and the Nyquist criterion.

Each chapter states its question, develops the idea in words, and works it through with declared numbers. Every number in a worked example was computed, and the answers to every practice problem are in the back of the book.
