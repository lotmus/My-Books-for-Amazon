# KDP Description: RF, Microwave, and Transceivers

**Title:** RF, Microwave, and Transceivers
**Subtitle:** Transmission lines, matching, S-parameters, filters, antennas, and arrays, then power amplifiers, receivers, oscillators, synthesizers, and radar, from 1 GHz to millimeter wave
**Series:** Electrical Engineering Series, Book 4 of 7
**Author:** Lothar J. Musiol
**Format:** Kindle (master file: RF_Microwave_and_Transceivers_Book4.docx)

## Description

Book 4 follows a radio from the copper to the air. Part I is the passive structure: lines, the Smith chart, S-parameters, filters, cavities, antennas, arrays, couplers, circulators, propagation, and antenna measurement. Part II is the active radio: load lines, amplifier classes, the Doherty amplifier, receiver noise, the LNA, mixers, the PLL and synthesizers, radar, the link budget, RF exposure, digital predistortion, and power control. A closing chapter sets the power amplifier classes side by side, A, AB, B, C, D, E, F and inverse F, J and the continuous modes, S, Doherty, and outphasing, with efficiency derivations, a comparison of every class, and worked designs, and then shows how supply modulation, from class G and H rail switching and average power tracking to envelope tracking, envelope elimination and restoration, and boost and bootstrap modulators that lift the supply above its nominal rail on the signal's peaks, raises average efficiency with OFDM and LTE signals.

Each chapter states its question, develops the idea in words, and works it through with declared numbers. Every number in a worked example was computed, and the answers to every practice problem are in the back of the book.
