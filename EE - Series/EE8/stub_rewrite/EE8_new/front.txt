SUBTITLE: Regulators, Switching Converters, Magnetics, the Grid, and Pulsed Power
HOW: This book is about converting electrical energy from the form in which it arrives to the form a load needs, with as little waste as possible. Chapter 1 sets out the formula that runs through the whole book, waste = P(1/η − 1), and applies it to the AI data center, where conversion losses are now measured in megawatts. Chapters 2 to 5 cover the converters: the linear regulator, the buck, the boost and buck-boost with their control loops, and the isolated flyback, forward, and LLC converters. Chapter 6 covers the magnetic components in four families, called shelves. Chapter 7 connects converters to the grid, and Chapter 8 treats high voltage and pulsed power.
HOW: Each chapter states its question, develops the idea in words, and works it through with declared numbers. Every number was computed, and the answers to every practice problem are in Appendix B. The reader should know circuit analysis at the level of Book 1 and feedback at the level of Book 2.
HOW: The series gives each subject one home. Semiconductor devices, including the physics of gallium-nitride and silicon-carbide switches, are Book 3's. High-power RF amplifiers are Book 6's, even when they draw kilowatts. Emissions from a converter's hot loop are measured in Book 7's chamber. Packaging, the processor's 875 A at 0.8 V, and the 2026 hardware snapshot are Book 9's. This book's anchor numbers are the rack comparison, 80 kW at 96% against 98% efficiency, a gap of 1.70 kW per rack and 1.70 MW per 1000 racks (0.96 kW at a 45 kW average), and copper's skin depth of about 66/√f mm. The texts in Appendix C go deeper; this book's explanations, examples, and problems are its own.
CONST: Waste: Pwaste = P(1/η − 1). Chain efficiency is the product of stage efficiencies.
CONST: LDO: η ≈ Vout/Vin; Pdiss ≈ (Vin − Vout)I; output ripple = input ripple × 10^(−PSRR/20).
CONST: Buck: Vout = DVin; ΔI = (Vin − Vout)D/(fswL); ΔV ≈ ΔI/(8fswC) + ΔI·ESR; switching loss ≈ ½VinI(tr + tf)fsw.
CONST: Boost: Vout = Vin/(1 − D); IL = Iout/(1 − D); fRHPZ = (1 − D)²R/(2πL). Inverting buck-boost: Vout = −DVin/(1 − D). Load step: ΔV ≈ ΔI/(2πfcC).
CONST: Flyback: Vout = VinD/[(1 − D)n]; forward: Vout = DVin/n; LLC: fr = 1/(2π√(LrCr)).
CONST: Skin depth in copper ≈ 66/√f mm: 8.5 mm at 60 Hz, 0.21 mm at 100 kHz.
CONST: Faraday: Vrms = 4.44fNBpkAe (sine), V = 4fNBpkAe (square). Inductor: N = LIpk/(BmaxAe), lg ≈ μ0N²Ae/L, E = ½LI².
CONST: PF = cos φ/√(1 + THD²); three-phase P = √3 VL IL PF.
CONST: Capacitor energy ½CV²; PFN: Ctotal = τ/(2Z0), Ltotal = τZ0/2, Vload = Vcharge/2; pulse droop ≈ Rτ/Lm. Air breakdown ≈ 3 kV/mm, uniform field.
