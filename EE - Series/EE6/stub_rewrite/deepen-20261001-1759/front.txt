SUBTITLE: Power Amplifiers, Load Lines, Doherty, Receiver Noise, the PLL, and Radar
HOW: This book owns the watts. It follows a transmitter from the device to the antenna: the device families, the load line, the amplifier classes, efficiency under modulated signals and the Doherty amplifier, and then the heat sink, combiners, filters, and feed line that stand between the transistor and the radiated power. It then turns to the receiving half of the radio, its noise figure and dynamic range, and to the oscillator and phase-locked loop that tune both halves. Chapter 8, radar, brings transmitter and receiver together. Chapters 9 to 17 deepen the chain: the low-noise amplifier, mixers and frequency planning, the link budget, bias and ruggedness, vacuum tubes, RF exposure, transceiver isolation, the design of class E and F stages, and envelope tracking, polar, and outphasing transmitters.
HOW: Each chapter states its question, develops the idea in words, and works it through with declared numbers. Every number was computed, and the answers to every practice problem are in Appendix B. The reader should know the matching and S-parameter material of Book 4 and the modulation and EVM material of Book 5.
HOW: The series gives each subject one home. Device physics, including why gallium nitride withstands high voltage, is Book 3's. Antenna gain, the Friis path factor, and matching networks are Book 4's. The waveform, OFDM, and the band plan are Book 5's. Emission masks, test chambers, and certification are Book 7's. Power conversion, including pulse modulators for tube transmitters, is Book 8's. This book's anchor numbers are the load-line pair for 100 W, Ropt = 10.13 Ω at 50 V with a 5 V knee and 3.13 Ω at 28 V with a 3 V knee, and the rule that 1 dB of feed loss leaves 0.794 of the power. The texts in Appendix C go deeper; this book's explanations, examples, and problems are its own.
CONST: dBm = 10 log₁₀(P/1 mW); 1 W = 30 dBm; 1 dB of loss leaves 0.794 of the power. EIRP = Pout − Lfeed + Gant.
CONST: Load line: Ropt = (VDD − Vknee)²/(2P). 100 W: 10.13 Ω at 50 V/5 V knee; 3.13 Ω at 28 V/3 V knee.
CONST: Efficiency: ηD = Pout/PDC; PAE = (Pout − Pin)/PDC. Class A 50%; class B π/4 = 78.5%; η(α) = (α − sin α)/[4(sin(α/2) − (α/2)cos(α/2))].
CONST: Back-off: class A η ∝ Pout; class B η = (π/4)√(Pout/Pmax); ideal symmetric Doherty 78.5% at peak and at 6 dB back-off.
CONST: OIP3 = Ptone + ΔIM3/2. SFDR = (2/3)(IIP3 − Nfloor).
CONST: Thermal: Tj = Tamb + Pdiss Σθ. Ideal N-way combiner: Pout = (Σ√Pi)²/N; one of N failed leaves (N − 1)²/N².
CONST: Noise: kT0 = −174 dBm/Hz at 290 K; Te = T0(F − 1); F = F1 + (F2 − 1)/G1 + (F3 − 1)/(G1G2) + ….
CONST: PLL: fout = N fref; in-band noise = Lref + 20 log₁₀N. Leeson: L(Δf) ≈ 10 log₁₀{(2FkT/Ps)[1 + (f0/(2QLΔf))²]}. σφ = √(2∫L df); jitter = σφ/(2πf0).
CONST: Radar: Pr = PtG²λ²σ/[(4π)³R⁴]; ΔR = c/(2B); Runamb = c·PRI/2; fd = 2v/λ; FMCW fb = 2RS/c.
CONST: LNA: F = Fmin + (4Rn/Z0)|Γs − Γopt|²/[(1 − |Γs|²)|1 + Γopt|²]. Mixer image = fRF ∓ 2fIF; spurs at |m fLO ± n fRF| = fIF.
CONST: Link: Lpath = 20 log₁₀(4πdf/c); C/N0 = EIRP − Lpath + G/T + 228.6 dBHz. 12 GHz, 38,000 km: 205.6 dB.
CONST: |Γ| = (VSWR − 1)/(VSWR + 1); 10:1 gives 0.818, 66.9% reflected. Beam P = VI; perveance K = I/V^1.5.
CONST: Exposure: S = EIRP/(4πR²); FCC public 10 W/m² (1.5–100 GHz), f/1500 mW/cm² (300–1500 MHz); ground factor 2.56.
CONST: Class E (50% duty): R = 0.5768 VDD²/P, C = 0.1836/(ωR), vpk = 3.562 VDD. Outphasing: Pout = Pmax cos²φ.
