**Title:** Communications, Wireless, and SDR

**Series:** Electrical Engineering Series, Book 5 of 7
**Author:** Lothar J. Musiol
**Format:** Kindle (master file: Communications_Wireless_and_SDR_Book5.docx)

## Description

Book 5 is about the waveform: how many bits it carries, how much spectrum it uses, how it survives noise and fading, and how a receiver written largely in software recovers it. It covers synchronization, equalization, spread spectrum, pilots, scheduling and HARQ, massive MIMO, cellular interference, non-terrestrial networks, and low-energy IoT radios.

Each chapter states its question, develops the idea in words, and works it through with declared numbers. Every number in a worked example was computed, and the answers to every practice problem are in the back of the book.
