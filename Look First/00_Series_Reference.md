## Shared, already in both books

From each book’s “How to Read,” the outlines, and `00_Book_Spine.md`.

- Three temperatures. **Hot:** measured, repeated, used to predict. **Warm:** best account, real gaps. **Cold:** allowed by the equations or the poster, not selected by the data. A temperature can change. A fountain does not get warmer because a local fix did.
- String theory, if it is named, cannot currently be tested. In Book 1 that refusal is row (18), a standing rule, not a formula.
- No film titles. Stories are invented scenes. The numbers in them are real. The people are invented.
- The main chapters keep the math in the sentence. Numbered relations live in the appendix. A reader can finish the book without opening the back.
- John D. Barrow’s short books are the formal model: brief, no swagger, a clean line between a measurement and an idea for ideas. Sentences stay short. The inventory is allowed to grow.
- No physics degree, and no medical degree, required.
- Look first. Seed later.
- Kindle ingest is the 6×9 `.docx`. One figure per chapter. Credits are not printed under the figures.
