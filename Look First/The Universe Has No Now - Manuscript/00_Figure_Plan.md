# Figure Plan — Kindle First (45-chapter remap)

Every figure must teach on a **6-inch e-ink Kindle in grayscale**. Color Fire tablets and phone apps get the same file for free. If it fails in gray, it is not in the book.

**One figure per chapter** (plus Fig 0 for the prologue). The old 52-chapter grid is retired. Extra pictures left over from merges are dropped or moved to the appendix.

## Hard spec (all figures)

- **One figure per chapter.** Full width. No side-by-side pairs, no insets, no tiny corner labels.
- **4:3 landscape.** Fills a Kindle page without a postage-stamp look.
- **Grayscale-proof.** Meaning is shape, brightness, or words — never hue.
- **Type in the picture ≥ 14 pt** at full-page width. If the label would be smaller, it goes in the caption instead.
- **Diagrams:** black strokes on white, ≥ 3 pt lines, no gray-on-gray.
- **Photos:** high contrast, crop to one object.
- **Two ideas:** two pages, stacked, never one wide strip — but still only one *popular* figure per chapter.
- **Files:** 1800 × 1350 px minimum. Photos: JPEG. Line art: PNG.
- **Alt text** on every figure.
- **Caption does the teaching.**
- **No AI stand-ins** for Hubble, JWST, Planck, EHT, rover, or Cassini frames.
- **No film stills.**

Caption: one sentence what it is, one sentence what to notice. Credit on its own line.

## Production checklist

1. Open on a 6-inch preview at grayscale.
2. Name the idea in two seconds without the caption; if not, crop or redraw.
3. Labels readable at 50% zoom; else move to caption.
4. Export gray check + color original.
5. Alt text ≤ 120 characters.
