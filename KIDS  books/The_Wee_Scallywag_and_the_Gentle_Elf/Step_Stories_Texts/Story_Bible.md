# The Step Stories: Story Bible

Working reference for the text and for every picture prompt. Hair colors and looks follow the proposed set; anything marked (new) is my invention for the v2 draft. Change what you like, and the book follows.

Rule for pictures: describe each character exactly as below every time, with the same hair, clothes and props. Only the two pointed-ear children (Sparefunk and Hopsi) have pointed ears.

---

## The children

### Hal (Halbert) Millersen (8, human boy)
- **Name:** Halbert, called Hal by everyone. Only the ghost uses the full name.
- **Looks:** Brown, tousled hair that never lies flat. Freckles. A slate in the crook of one arm. Patched blue jacket, brown trousers, sturdy old boots (he lost these in the bargain, and wears mended ones after).
- **Voice:** Level and dry. Short sentences. He says "Acceptable" when things are bad and "Done" when they are good.
- **Smell:** Flour dust, chalk and wet wool.
- **Touch:** Chalky fingers, a rough wool sleeve, a warm slate.
- **Sound:** Chalk squeaking. A sigh through the nose before he does something brave.
- **Habit:** Counts things under his breath. Moves the stone so the water runs true.
- **Wants:** To finish the work properly. Fears being laughed at.

### Titch Mitchel (8, human girl)
- **Looks:** Dark brown hair to her shoulders, usually under a red knitted cap. Round face, quick brown eyes. Green patched coat with a yellow scarf. Carries a crust cloth and a needle.
- **Voice:** Fast, practical, with a small laugh at the end of sentences. Calls people by their jobs ("the boot boy").
- **Smell:** Warm bread, wool and a little woodsmoke.
- **Touch:** Crumbs in her pockets, a woolly cap, thread-roughened fingers.
- **Sound:** Humming while she sews.
- **Habit:** Breaks every crust into even pieces. Turns a hem when she is nervous.
- **Wants:** To feed everybody and fix everything. Fears waste.

### Sparefunk (8, pointed-ear boy, the wee scallywag)
- **Looks:** Black spiky hair, pointed ears, a grin like a lit match. Muddy knees. Blue-grey hooded jacket, a red neckerchief, and two pairs of big boots (the pair he wears and the spare pair with the tag).
- **Voice:** Loud, fast, delighted. Exaggerates, then trims it down when caught. Laughs in the middle of sentences.
- **Smell:** Mud, hot iron and old cabbages (after the bog).
- **Touch:** Gritty hands, scratched knuckles, big heavy boots.
- **Sound:** Stamping. A whoop when he runs.
- **Habit:** Collects tyres and boots. Runs when the boots feel like it.
- **Wants:** To be clever and not to be bored. Fears being ordinary.
- **Elf rule:** His true name is the answer to the bargain. He only tells the truth when it is funny.

### Hopsi (Elfie Didlhop, 9, pointed-ear girl, the gentle elf)
- **Looks:** Light brown hair, long and wavy, with a hops wreath. Pointed ears. Green dress with a purple scarf, a needle in one hand and a bowl of hops in the other.
- **Voice:** Quiet and even. Speaks only what is true, so she is brief. “Fast is not the same as early.”
- **Smell:** Hops, green bread and fresh-cut grass.
- **Touch:** Cool fingers, a needle tip, small green leaves.
- **Sound:** A soft munch of hops. Silence, which is her loudest sound.
- **Habit:** Eats a hop, shuts her mouth, mends what is torn. Leaves rhymes on leaves.
- **Wants:** Peace and a true word. Fears lies that sound kind.

### Tabitha Smoke (8, Black girl)
- **Looks:** Black curly hair up in a bun, tied with a rust-coloured band. Dark brown skin. Brown dress and a grey coat, a bag of apples.
- **Voice:** Warm, steady, with a laugh she keeps for exactly the right moment. Asks the one question nobody else thought of.
- **Smell:** Apples, rain on stone and clean linen.
- **Touch:** Smooth apple skin, the soft bun, a firm handshake.
- **Sound:** A crunch. A low hum when she is thinking.
- **Habit:** Shares her last apple. Watches first, then stays.
- **Wants:** To belong. Fears being the stranger at the step. She belongs by the end of the third day.

### Gucci (wolf baby)
- **Looks:** A grey wolf pup, thin when found, filling out now. Silver-grey coat with a darker saddle and a pale belly. Blue eyes that will turn amber. Big paws it has not grown into, a long soft muzzle, and one ear that flops. Found sleeping in the spare boot, which it has outgrown; now it chooses Hal’s foot. A wolf, not a dog: longer legs, a narrower chest, a straight tail held low.
- **Voice:** It does not bark so much as cough. A tiny growl that sounds like a kettle coming to the boil. A thin, wavering howl at the moon that Sparefunk imitates badly.
- **Smell:** Warm fur, damp pup, old leather and a little woodsmoke. After rain, wet dog.
- **Touch:** A soft belly, rough tongue, needle-sharp milk teeth that nip but never bite. A cold wet nose in your palm.
- **Sound:** Paws on stone, claws on the step, a sneeze when excited, a thump of the tail, a sigh when it settles on a foot.
- **Habit:** Takes its crust and declines hops. Lies across Hal’s foot. Runs when they run and comes back when they sit. Chases anything that moves, then stops when it realises how big the moving thing is.
- **Wants:** Warmth, crusts and the foot. Fears nothing yet, which is the danger. Only afraid of the stranger’s shadow, and of the one thing it can smell and nobody else can.
- **Behavior in the story:** It nearly gets eaten by the pike, growls at the Soft Voice, and sits on the pile of children after the run. It does not talk, and does not need to.

## The pointy-eared folk (new, my invention: please check)

Only two kinds of people in the book have pointed ears: the elves and the little devils. Humans (Hal, Titch, Tabitha, the messengers) never do. Every elf or little devil in a picture has the ears of their kind, so nobody has to ask which is which.

### The elves (Hopsi’s kind)
- **Looks:** Slender and light-boned. Long ears, swept up and back like a leaf, with a soft curved point. Wavy hair in woodland colours (brown, gold, red-brown), often with leaves or hops woven in. Eyes green, grey or hazel. Soft greens, purples and moss colours; mended clothes, never new.
- **Voice:** Quiet, level and clear. They speak only what is true, so they speak seldom. When they say nothing, they mean it.
- **Smell:** Hops, cut grass, green bread, rain on leaves.
- **Touch:** Cool, light fingers. Hands that mend. A needle and thread are never far away.
- **Sound:** Soft footsteps that don’t crunch. A hum like a kettle at the back of a long day. Silence.
- **Habits:** Mend what is torn. Leave rhymes on leaves. Walk through flowers and the flowers lift their heads. Never lie, and never gloat.
- **Magic:** Small and practical. Plants grow better where they have been, loose threads close, and a true word settles an argument.
- **Weakness:** They can’t say a kind thing that isn’t true, which causes trouble at weddings.
- **How they treat others:** Patiently, and a little distantly, until you prove you will shut a gate.

### The little devils (Sparefunk’s kind)
- **Looks:** Small, quick and wiry. Short, sharp ears with a crisp point, which twitch when the owner is up to something. Black or very dark hair that sticks up. Tiny horn-buds hidden in the hair, no bigger than a pea (a little devil is not a big one). Eyes dark with a glint. Scuffed, patched clothes in reds and blues, big boots, always muddy.
- **Voice:** Loud, fast and delighted. They talk at speed, exaggerate, and laugh in the middle of sentences. They love a story, even if they have to improve it.
- **Smell:** Hot iron, mud, woodsmoke and, after any bog, old cabbages.
- **Touch:** Gritty hands, scratched knuckles, warm skin like a stone left in the sun.
- **Sound:** Stamping, whooping, slapping boots. A stamp of the foot makes the ground answer.
- **Habits:** Make bargains (always with small print). Collect boots, tyres and anything with a good rattle. Run when it suits them.
- **Magic:** Quick and showy. A stamp sends a crack of green light up a wall; they can run up it. Time stretches for them when they run fast. Bargains made with a stamp hold exactly as written.
- **Weakness:** They can’t break a bargain, and a true name beats them. They only tell the truth when it’s funny.
- **How they treat others:** Cheekily, then loyally. Once a bargain is kept, they stay.

### How the two kinds differ at a glance (for pictures)
- **Ears:** elf = long, slender, swept back and soft. Little devil = short, sharp, upright and twitchy.
- **Hair:** elf = long, wavy, with leaves or hops. Little devil = short, black, spiky.
- **Build:** elf = tall for her age, light and still. Little devil = small for his, wiry and moving.
- **Colours:** elf = greens, purples, creams. Little devil = reds, blues, muddy browns.
- **Gift:** elf = a true word that mends. Little devil = a stamp that moves the world.
- **Together:** the elf shuts her mouth and the little devil opens his, and the bargain gets kept.

## The creatures and strangers

### The Mill Ghost (new)
- **Looks:** Pale and thin as spilled milk. A flour-sack cloak, a dusty hat, sad eyes. See-through at the edges.
- **Voice:** Flat, polite, lonely. Says “Boo” as if reading it off a card.
- **Smell:** Cold flour and old stone.
- **Touch:** A cool breath, a prickle on the neck. Cannot hold a cup, but holds it anyway.
- **Sound:** The creak of the wheel. A sigh like a door.
- **Habit:** Keeps the wheel turning. Likes being chased.
- **Wants:** The wheel to turn. Hasn’t been chased in two hundred years.

### The Toad of the Ford (new)
- **Looks:** The largest toad in the county. Warty, moss-green and mustard, a moustache of pondweed, eyes half shut.
- **Voice:** A slow, deep croak. Speaks in tolls.
- **Smell:** Pond mud and wet stone.
- **Touch:** Cold, wet, bumpy.
- **Sound:** A throat that goes in and out. A soft burp.
- **Habit:** Takes a toll, keeps the bargain, gives back what is fair.

### The Pike (new)
- **Looks:** Old and long as a canoe, a head like a hatchet, silver and green flanks.
- **Sound:** A splash like a thrown door.
- **Habit:** Waits all day for something to bite.

### The Grasshoppers (new)
- **Looks:** A green cloud, thousands, bright and flickering. Hungry for hops.
- **Sound:** Dry clicks and a rising whirr. A smell of cut hay.

### The Soft Voice (the stranger)
- **Looks:** Tall and thin, in a coat the colour of dusk. A smile too wide. A bag that clinks. A shadow longer than he is.
- **Voice:** Soft, sugary, never in a hurry. “A quicker way.”
- **Smell:** Boiled sweets and cold fog.
- **Touch:** Nobody has felt it, and nobody wants to.
- **Sound:** A clink from the bag.
- **Rule:** He only knows the quick way. He cannot cross the three white stones, because they mark the true way.

### The King’s Messengers (new)
- **Looks:** Two men in blue and purple coats, feathered hats, white stockings, very clean.
- **Voice:** Loud and formal. Bow too low.
- **Smell:** Powder and boiled cabbage.

## The places

### The mill and the step
- **Looks:** Old stone and timber, ivy to the roof, a green door that sticks. Stone steps worn into dips. The wheel is dark wood with moss.
- **Smell:** Flour dust, wet stone and wood smoke.
- **Sound:** The wheel creaking. Water dripping from the slats. Boots on the step.
- **Touch:** Cool, rough steps, a warm slate, a smooth door handle.
- **Weather:** The sun hits the step at noon. The wind comes first from the north.
- **Feeling:** Safe. The place you can always come back to.

### The stream and the mill pond
- **Looks:** Quick, clear, brown over stones, black in the pond. Willows lean over the pond.
- **Smell:** Wet earth and fish.
- **Sound:** Chatter over stones, a plop, a heron’s croak.
- **Touch:** Cold. Slippery stones.

### The fallen log
- **Looks:** An old oak across the stream, grey and ridged. Feels like it has opinions.
- **Touch:** Rough bark, splintery. Moves under your feet.
- **Role:** The lever.

### The ford
- **Looks:** Flat stones across shallow water, thick grass on both banks, a toad stone in the middle.
- **Smell:** Pond weed and warm mud.
- **Sound:** Splash, croak, grasshoppers.

### The night wood
- **Looks:** Tall oaks and ferns in silver moonlight. Small drifting lights. Seedlings under the big oak, no two alike.
- **Smell:** Leaf mould, wild garlic and cold air.
- **Sound:** Owl, rustle, the bounce of pine cones.
- **Touch:** Damp ferns, bark, moss like velvet.
- **Danger:** The bog hides under green moss.

### The bog
- **Looks:** Bright green moss over black water. Looks like solid ground.
- **Smell:** Old cabbages and rot.
- **Touch:** Sucking, cold, up to the waist.

### The bend and the three white stones
- **Looks:** A turn in the path where the step can still be seen. Three white stones in a line across the path.
- **Sound:** A very faint hum when the stones light up.
- **Feeling:** A line that holds.

### The field of rectangular eggs
- **Looks:** Eggs with corners, in the long grass behind the mill. Cream, speckled, sitting in order.
- **Role:** A small mystery that is never explained.

### The road
- **Looks:** Pale dirt, ruts, hedges, dust in summer and mud in rain.
- **Role:** Where strangers come from.

## The weather, as a character
- **Morning:** Light that left the sun eight minutes ago. Dew. A smell of wet grass.
- **Storm:** Wind first, then rain sideways, then hail like peas. Ten minutes later: sun and a rainbow.
- **Fog:** White, silent, smelling of cold sweets. It comes up from the stream.
- **Dusk:** Orange sky, long shadows, the first bat.
- **Night:** Moon, cold stones, small lights.

## Food and drink
- Porridge with honey. Warm bread and crusts. Apples. Hops (Hopsi’s only food, the grasshoppers’ too). Hop tea. Elderflower cordial. Roast pike on a stick. Marshmallows at the campfire.

## The voice of the book
Deadpan, kind and quick. Small true things. Nobody lectures. The step does the talking. People talk too much; the work gets done.
