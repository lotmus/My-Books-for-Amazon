Morning came with light that had left the sun eight minutes earlier. Boot woke first and waited. Titch set out a small crust she had saved. Wilf opened the slate and saw it still needed marking. Sparefunk put on the walking boots and left the spare pair standing. Hopsi ate two hops and checked the needle.

They looked both ways. The rise behind them hid the mill and then showed it when they stood. The path ahead ran damper. No one spoke a long sentence. Boot trotted a few steps toward the stream, drank, and came back.

They sat again. The choice remained open. The light continued to arrive as it always had. Tomorrow would be made of the same things: slate, crust, boots, hops, and the stream that did not choose for them.

The stream continued.
