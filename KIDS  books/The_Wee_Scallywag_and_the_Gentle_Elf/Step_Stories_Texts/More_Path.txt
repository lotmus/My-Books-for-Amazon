They did not reach the coast. The path stayed with the stream, and the stream stayed narrower than the sea. Wilf cleared one last stone where the water slowed. Titch folded the crust cloth smaller. Sparefunk set the spare boots down for a moment so the mud could dry at the edges. Hopsi finished the hops she had brought and put the empty bowl in her pocket. The wolf baby drank once from the stream and returned to the boot.

A moonbeam, thinner now, cancelled its remaining wrinkles and lay across the path. No one reached. Behind them the mill was still visible if they turned. Ahead the way continued, damper by degrees. The rectangular eggs were far enough back to be out of sight. No stranger appeared. No speech grew long.

They sat while the light thinned further. The spare boots were ready. The slate was still under Wilf’s arm. Tomorrow both the step and this bend would still be possible. The coast, wetter still, could wait.

The stream continued toward it.
