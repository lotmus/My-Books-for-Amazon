There was still no vampire. The moonshine ghost thinned and cancelled. The field spirits left no footprints. Wilf set the slate aside. Titch put out the crust. Sparefunk left the spare boots empty and the tyres stacked. Hopsi finished the hops and the seam. The wolf baby slept on the boot.

The rectangular eggs kept their corners. The stream continued. No one was bitten. No one was invited further than the step. The light arrived and then was gone. Everyone was quiet enough.

Tomorrow would bring whatever it brought — slate, hops, possibly another tyre — and the same absence of vampires. That was sufficient.
