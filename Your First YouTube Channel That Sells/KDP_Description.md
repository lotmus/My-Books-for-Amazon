## Description (paste into KDP; plain paragraphs, about 1,900 characters)

Views are not income. A subscriber count is not income. A channel that sells has one thing a stranger can buy.

Your First YouTube Channel That Sells is a working guide for creators who want a channel, not a hobby. It takes you from no offer to eight videos that each do one job and point at one price on a page you control. You can make your first sale long before any platform pays you.

Inside you will learn how to:

• Choose one buyer and one offer, and put the price on a page before you film
• Win the click with titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions, and a posting rhythm you can keep
• Make videos cheaply with free tools, and read the licenses that keep a claim from becoming a strike
• Record for the ear, cut for pace, and keep the buyer watching until the price
• Read YouTube Studio’s numbers and know which count answers which question
• Understand the Partner Program as it stands now, including the changes that start on 1 February 2027: higher entry bars for new channels and a 10-million-view monthly floor for Shorts pool money
• Use the AI-disclosure, paid-promotion, and made-for-kids settings correctly, and stay clear of the inauthentic-content rules
• Run a six-week plan to a live offer, and diagnose the earliest break when nothing sells

Every platform rule is tied to YouTube’s own help pages. Anything about the algorithm is labeled as creator consensus, not fact. The worked numbers are arithmetic, not promises of income.

Independent guide. Not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube.
