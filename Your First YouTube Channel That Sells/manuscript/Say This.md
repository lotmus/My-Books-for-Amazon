*A working guide for creators who want a channel, not a hobby*

# Say This

*Words you can paste and then make true. Replace every bracket. Delete any sentence that is not yet real.*

These are your words, not a script for a viewer’s comment and not a fake testimonial. If a sentence overclaims, cut it. A channel that sells says a smaller true thing.

## I. Channel description

I help [specific person] who wants [specific result].

The offer is [name of offer] — [what it includes] for [price].

Start here: [one link]

New videos walk through [the method] so you can do a small version yourself. The paid version is for when you want [the shortcut, template, or help].

## II. Description of a teaching video

[Buyer’s problem, in their words]. This video shows [the one job of this video].

I cover [three concrete steps, named].

If you want the [offer] instead of doing it from scratch, it is here: [one link]. [If affiliate: This is an affiliate link. I earn a commission if you buy, at no extra cost to you.]

Chapters:
0:00 [who it is for]
[timestamp] [step]
[timestamp] [what the offer adds]

## III. Description of the offer video

[Offer name] is for [specific person] who wants [result].

It includes [list]. It costs [price]. It does not include [the thing buyers often assume].

Get it here: [one link]

You can do the basic version with the free steps in this video. The link is the faster path, not a locked door.

## IV. Pinned comment

The link for [offer name] is [one link]. [Price]. [One sentence on what is included.] [Affiliate disclosure in the same comment, if it applies.]

## V. The twenty seconds at the end of a video

If you want [result] without building [the tedious part] yourself, I made [offer name]. It is [price] and it includes [the single most useful part]. The link is in the first line of the description and in the pinned comment. If you would rather do it yourself, the steps in this video are enough.

## VI. The reply when someone asks the price in a comment

[Offer name] is [price]. Here is what is included: [one sentence]. Link: [one link]. Happy to answer a question about fit. I do not change the price in the comments.

## VII. The first email

Subject: [offer name] — [price]

I help [specific person] who wants [specific result].

The offer is [name]. It includes [what is included]. It costs [price]. It does not include [the thing buyers often assume].

Here is the link: [one link].

The next note, in [how often], will be [the next useful thing], not a surprise pitch. You can do the basic version from the videos. This link is the faster path.

## VIII. What you do not say

Do not say the video will change their life, guarantee income, or “blow up” their own channel. Do not tell a viewer to comment a keyword so you can pretend the comment count is demand. Do not ask for a subscription in the same breath as the price. One ask. The ask that matches a channel that sells is the offer, not the bell.

Do not paste a review you wrote yourself. If a buyer tells you a result, ask before you quote them, and do not enlarge the result.

> **Key takeaway:** Paste the sentence, replace every bracket, and delete any line you cannot say out loud.

## IX. Claims that do not survive being read aloud

- That clever copy outperforms a clear price. The buyer has to find the number. Cleverness that hides it is a lost sale.
- That you need a different link in every video. One primary link. Different videos may explain different parts. They send people to the same offer until that offer has earned a second one.
- That disclosure ruins affiliate sales. Hiding an affiliate relationship ruins trust, and on many platforms it breaks the rule. Say it next to the link.
- That the description can wait until you are “bigger.” The description is the shelf. A video with no shelf can be watched and still sell nothing.
- That the first email should warm people up before it names the price. The first email names the price. A warm-up with no number is the waitlist again.

A last limit. If you would be ashamed to say the filled-in version to a careful person, do not publish it. Rewrite the offer until the sentences are dull and true.
