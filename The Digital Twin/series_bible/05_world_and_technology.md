# World and technology texture (Brave New Times)

Tone: the future feels normal. Nobody talks about the technology; they complain about it like weather and parking. It is "in": SpaceX-style launches, AI everywhere, self-driving vehicles, robotics. Real company names appear only as ordinary scenery, with no claims about them; plot-bearing or satirical technology is invented (Nib, Matchwell, the Rental Presence bodies, the Department's portals). Present day: **October 2026** (Daniel, born 14 March 1988, is 38). The **Digital Persons Act** was passed in 2025 and came into force on **2 January 2026** (first registration issued that day). The Parrot Incident was in **March 2026**. Daniel signed Nib's terms on 11 January 2026; Daniel-A is nine months old. Do not date anything to 2031/2032.

## Everyday texture
- **Space:** reusable-rocket launches (Starship-class) are a Tuesday-afternoon livestream on YouTube, with a commentary track people argue about; people say "did you see the catch?" Daniel-A explains a launch in plain English to a million viewers. Starlink-style dishes on roofs. A lunar landing scheduled and postponed like a bin collection. Never more than a line of scenery.
- **Self-driving vehicles:** driverless taxis are normal and slightly judgemental (they ask if you are sure; they take a scenic route when confused); driverless lorries on the motorway at night; delivery pods crawl along pavements with the polite hesitancy of a creature unsure of its welcome; a bus with no driver still has a woman who says "move down the bus, please" from a speaker.
- **Robotics:** humanoid helpers in shops and care homes; kitchen robots; delivery bots; the **Rental Presence** (licensed humanoid body, blue collar light) is a hire product by the day. Sophie's insurer pays out on "robot misadventure". A hotel robot called Gerald.
- **AI everywhere:** every appliance has opinions; the fridge screen (passive-aggressive recipes); the kitchen radio and the smart speaker; calendar agents with default names (Kevin, Brenda, Dave, Sue, Derek, Priya, Barrington); AI notetakers in every meeting; AI therapists; AI dating interviewers; AI phone support that sighs.
- **Security and truth:** cameras and phones sign what they record (Sophie's work); synthetic media is watermarked; "provenance" is a profession.

## Daniel's relationship to the world
Daniel is slightly behind the curve, by temperament (footpaths, paperbacks, toast). The technology works fine; he distrusts being managed by it. He takes the driverless taxi and apologises to it.
