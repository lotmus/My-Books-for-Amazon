## What actually changed farming

Herbicide-tolerant soy/corn: simplified weed control, surge in no-till. USDA ERS: by 2006 about 45 percent of HT soybean acres no-till versus about 5 percent conventional. Glyphosate use rose; weeds followed (chapter 35).

Bt cotton: insecticide drop for bollworm. China from 1997, about 95 percent of national cotton by 2018 (Lu 2019; Journal of Cotton Research 2024). Jikun Huang and colleagues, early 2000s surveys, about half. India from the early 2000s; pink bollworm resistance where refuges failed.

NAS 2016: no rise in the underlying yield-growth rate. Two traits, often stacked, not a Green Revolution in a bag.

DroughtGard (cspB from Bacillus subtilis): modest, sometimes invisible, USDA ARMS. Nitrogen fixation in cereals: not commercial as of late 2026. Kernza (Land Institute, intermediate wheatgrass): a few thousand acres by 2025, niche, yield a fraction of wheat.

## China as of late 2026

Bt cotton since 1997. GM papaya grown. MARA variety registration for GE corn and soy from 2023. USDA GAIN / industry: a few million hectares by 2025, still a single-digit share of China's corn. September 2025 MARA announcement 96 GE corn and 2 GE soy varieties (MOA announcement 947). May 2026 initial review of another corn/soy batch. December 2024 biosafety certificates for gene-edited rice and wheat; no variety-registration standards yet, so not commercial seed. Food-security framing in MARA and No. 1 Documents. Not a secret biowar farm.

## 2035 / 2050 sketch (also in chapter 35)

Guess, marked as such. 2035: a Heilongjiang farm planting Chinese insect-resistant corn, some transgenic, some edited, less spray for borers, still walking rows for weeds. 2050: a visitor at a public crop gene bank in Beijing looking at drawers of rice and wheat edited on purpose, after the 2026 variety rules have been written. ## Law

Argentina Resolution 173/2015: case-by-case, not a GMO if no new combination of genetic material in the finished seed. Japan: GABA tomato not regulated as GE, December 2020. US: USDA mushroom letter April 2016; SDN-1 generally outside plant-pest rules. EU: CJEU July 2018 swept CRISPR into Directive 2001/18; Regulation (EU) 2026/1388 adopted 17 June 2026, applies 17 July 2028; implementing details still being written.
