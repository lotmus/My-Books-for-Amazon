# Plan: *Life, Actually*

_Series: Physics, Actually. Author: Lothar J. Musiol. Started 2 October 2026._

Lothar approved merging *Life: Evolution* (31 chapters, about 72,500 words) and *The Copy Is Never Exact* (prologue and 46 chapters, about 103,000 words) into one book, *Life, Actually*, with a new closing Part on exobiology.

## Arc

1. **Origins.** What life is, the chemistry under it, how it may have started, the first cells, quantum biology. (Part I)
2. **Evolution.** Darwin's machine, the big transitions, us, and the minds evolution built. (Parts II to V)
3. **Heredity and genetics.** The molecule and inheritance, from Cambridge in 1953 to the monk's garden and the fly room. (Parts VI and VII)
4. **The genome and modern biotech.** Reading and writing the text, food and the whole genome, your own text and the edit to come. (Parts VIII to X)
5. **Life elsewhere.** What life needs, habitable worlds, Mars, the icy moons, alien air, SETI, the Fermi paradox, a second genesis. (Part XI)

## Overlap decisions (keep the better version)

- **DNA basics.** Evolution ch. 9 ("DNA: The Molecule That Remembers") is cut. Genetics Parts I–II (now chapters 31–40) tell the same story at greater depth: Franklin and Photo 51, the double helix, the code, copying and repair, junk DNA.
- **Mendel.** Evolution ch. 8's Mendel sections are cut down to a short bridge; Genetics 15–17 (now 45–47) are the full story. Evolution ch. 8 keeps the Modern Synthesis (Fisher, Haldane, Wright, Dobzhansky, Mayr, Simpson, Hardy–Weinberg), which is now the book's one full account of it. The synthesis paragraph in Genetics 10 (now 40) becomes a short callback.
- **Natural selection.** Explained in full once, chapter 7. Other chapters call back to it.
- **Quantum tunnelling and mutation.** Full account in chapter 40 (Löwdin 1963, Slocombe et al. 2022). Chapter 5 keeps a short version that points forward.
- **Ancient DNA and interbreeding.** Full account in chapter 58 ("Reading the Dead"). Chapter 19 keeps the fossils and a short summary that points forward. *EPAS1* is told once in full (chapter 58); chapter 20 keeps a short version.
- **ATP and the proton turbine.** Full account in chapter 36. Chapter 4 keeps the origin-of-life angle (alkaline vents) and points forward.
- **Exobiology.** Evolution ch. 31 ("Did Life Come From Outer Space?") is retired; its material (panspermia, meteorite organics, Mars, ocean worlds, the Fermi question) is rewritten and brought up to date in Part XI.
