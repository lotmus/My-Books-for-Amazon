# Book Spine — Locked Constraints

**Title:** *The Universe Has No Now*
**Subtitle:** *Time, Origins, and Whether We Can Get Somewhere Else*
**Author:** Lothar J. Musiol
**Guideline voice:** Barrow — short, clear, sceptical of “ideas for ideas.”
**Audience:** average American reader; scientific; fun to read; never condescending.
**Length:** ~400 Kindle pages altogether (main text + appendix).
**Word budget:** ~127,000 words (same series conversion that made ~70,000 ≈ 220 Kindle pages).

The extra room is for the rooms this draft already opened: other body plans, leftovers in the air, a path that is not a last word, crews that do not sleep, and what an “I” is allowed to be. Still Barrow-short *per idea*. More ideas. Not thicker sentences.

**Stance (locked):**
- Weak anthropic filter — yes.
- Anthroposophic / Final Anthropic / required Omega Point — no.
- String theory, whenever mentioned: a mathematical framework that **cannot currently be tested**; it has not produced a unique prediction that would kill it if wrong. Extra dimensions and the “landscape” inherit that status.

**Equation formatting (same as *The Quantum Conversation*):**
- Default: minimal inline notation, “where X is Y” on its own line.
- Key equations (~18 total): numbered (1)–(18) in the appendix and called from the main text.
- Draft carets for exponents; finished .docx uses real superscripts.

**Pivotal questions:**
- Does time exist, or only events?
- What are the past and the future if spacetime is a block?
- If every allowed outcome is on the inventory, why does *this* worldline still matter?
- Why does the past look arranged for observers if the future may be empty?

**Core ontology:** all times + all allowed branches as inventory. Warm claim. Not a travel method.

**Story elements (invented, no titles):** survival on another world; radio lag; a clock beside a horizon; a possible signal. Foil to shortcuts and cosmic craftsmen. Meaning as local engineering on one thread.

**Never:** film titles, verse-jumping as physics, string theory as a tested result.
