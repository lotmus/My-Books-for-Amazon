# Sample — Longevity and “full brain”

Book 3 voice page. ~750 words. Healthspan, local DNA, the 10% myth, the straight-line lie.

---

A capsule arrives on the same delay as the orbiter’s voice. The label is optimistic. Her hands are not. She takes the dose because a joint that still works is a local win, and local wins are the only kind biology has ever reliably sold. She does not become a different species. She logs the pH.

Lifespan is not healthspan.

Lifespan is how long the clock on the wall can be embarrassed by your pulse. Healthspan is how many of those years you would vote to repeat. You can add frail years. That is easy to mistake for a miracle. It is a longer evening in a chair. The honest product, if you want one, is mornings that are still worth hitting an airlock for.

Aging is not one fuse. It is many clocks: letters that copy wrong, mitochondria that forget their job, an immune system that grows bitter, cells that refuse to die and then spoil the room, a brain that wears its own grooves. A pill that shouts at one clock does not reset the kitchen.

Gene fixes are local. That sentence should be printed on every slide that says *DNA* as if DNA were a fountain.

Hot: we can, in a growing list of cases, reach into a lineage of cells and correct a broken instruction — a blood disease, a rare enzyme, a tissue that will take the edit and keep it. CRISPR and its cousins are tools. They are not magic. They are scissors with paperwork.

Cold: a body-wide rewind. A thousand-year ape as a scheduled product. Helium. The rest of the house still ages while you celebrate the one door you unstuck. Cancer is not one door. The brain’s long goodbye is not one door. Atherosclerosis is a plumbing problem with a biological accent. Local is the word. Keep it.

Then the sentence that will not die, because it is useful to people who sell keys: *we only use ten percent of our brain.*

False. Hot as a correction. Imaging, lesions, the energy bill — the organ is on duty. Tissue that looks idle in one snapshot is still a staff with a shift you did not catch. You do not have a spare ninety percent in the cupboard, waiting for a password so you can meet “new challenges.” You have the organ you have, plus sleep, plus practice, plus tools, plus other people. That is already how we adjust. Plasticity is real and expensive. It is not a tank of unused mind.

Cognitive enhancement, two temperatures. Education, eyeglasses, a hearing aid, a stimulant with a known invoice: warm-to-hot as *tools*. A chip that returns a lost function: a local fix again, with scars. Unlocking a hidden full brain: cold. Do not dress the myth up as a future.

The carrot has no off-switch. Book 1 said it about a feast and a fake. It is still true if the feast is a long life. A Pleistocene wanting-loop in a body that lasts 120 years is not a wise king. It is a mismatch with more calendar. Look first before you call that an upgrade.

Now the other cartoon: take the last fifty years of tech, science, and medicine, and draw a straight line for ten thousand years.

The last fifty years were real. Vaccines that closed rooms of death. A genome you can read without a monastery. Packets that make a radio on Mars feel like a rude kitchen. Compute that got cheaper while we were looking at the basil. None of that is a myth.

None of it is a method for year 12,000.

Fifty into ten thousand is two hundred. That is arithmetic. It is not physics. Progress is more often an S-curve: a slow start, a drunk middle, a wall. The walls have names. Energy. Materials. Heat. Attention. Institutions. Wars. A meeting that cannot agree. A supply chain that snaps. Biology’s many clocks, which do not care that your slide is exponential. Diminishing returns are what a kitchen looks like when the easy fat has been skimmed.

Claim, cold: last fifty × 200 = the furniture of the year 12,000. We cannot name that furniture. Claim, warm: whatever is invented will still pay energy, coordination, and the body’s invoices. Claim, cold: progress is guaranteed if we wait. Knowledge can be lost. The loaf does not owe a sequel.

She names the basil. Sentiment does not get to vote. The capsule may buy her a useful season. It will not buy her helium, and it will not unlock a brain she was not already using. The dirt still has a chemistry. The chemistry can be bullied. That was always the whole trick.
