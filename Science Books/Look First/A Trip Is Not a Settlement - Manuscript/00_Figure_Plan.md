# Figure Plan — Kindle First

Every figure must teach on a **6-inch e-ink Kindle in grayscale**. Color Fire tablets and phone apps get the same file for free. If it fails in gray, it is not in the book.

## Hard spec (all figures)

- **One figure per chapter.** Full width. No side-by-side pairs, no insets, no tiny corner labels.
- **4:3 landscape.** Fills a Kindle page without a postage-stamp look.
- **Grayscale-proof.** Meaning is shape, brightness, or words — never hue.
- **Type in the picture ≥ 14 pt** at full-page width. If the label would be smaller, it goes in the caption instead.
- **Diagrams:** black strokes on white, ≥ 3 pt lines, no gray-on-gray.
- **Photos:** high contrast, crop to one object. Full dark star fields turn to mud — crop or replace.
- **Files:** 1800 × 1350 px minimum (1200 px on the short side). Photos: JPEG. Line art: PNG.
- **Alt text** on every figure.
- **Caption does the teaching.** The picture is the hook; the sentence under it says what to notice.
- **No fake NASA photos.** No AI stand-ins for rover, lander, or Earthrise frames. Use public-domain or licensed stills, or draw.
- **No film stills.**

Caption: one sentence what it is, one sentence what to notice. Credit on its own line.

## What we will not use (fails e-ink)

| Tempting original | Why it dies | Kindle replacement |
|---|---|---|
| Full night-sky Moon mosaic | Dust when shrunk | Single high-contrast disk, or a lander large in frame |
| Color program posters | Hue is the sales pitch | Line art: stack vs rendering, labeled in words |
| Rainbow Gantt charts | Dates become confetti | One horizontal line, three huge ticks |
| Mars globe in candy color | Gray mud | Simple disk, *6 mbar* in large type |
| Five clocks in one row | Icons become specks | One column of four large labeled pots |
| Deep-space black with a tiny ship | Lost silhouette | Craft against a bright limb or white |

## Production checklist (every file)

1. Open on a 6-inch preview at grayscale.
2. Can you name the idea in two seconds without the caption? If not, crop or redraw.
3. Are all labels still readable at 50% zoom? If not, move them to the caption.
4. Export gray check + color original. Keep both.
5. Write alt text ≤ 120 characters.

## First pulls that already survive e-ink

1. Earth disk, daylight, high contrast (public domain)
2. Moon disk filling the frame
3. A lander or rover **large in frame**, dusty ground, hard daylight — licensed or public-domain only
4. A kitchen clock in a window (original photo; we can take this one)
5. Line art for stacks, windows, three nouns, four pots

We do not drop in a program poster and hope. We do not invent a NASA still.
