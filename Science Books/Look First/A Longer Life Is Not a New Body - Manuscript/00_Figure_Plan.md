# Figure Plan — Kindle First

Every figure must teach on a **6-inch e-ink Kindle in grayscale**. Color Fire tablets and phone apps get the same file for free. If it fails in gray, it is not in the book.

## Hard spec (all figures)

- **One figure per chapter.** Full width. No side-by-side pairs, no insets, no tiny corner labels.
- **4:3 landscape.** Fills a Kindle page without a postage-stamp look.
- **Grayscale-proof.** Meaning is shape, brightness, or words — never hue.
- **Type in the picture ≥ 14 pt** at full-page width. If the label would be smaller, it goes in the caption instead.
- **Diagrams:** black strokes on white, ≥ 3 pt lines, no gray-on-gray.
- **Photos:** high contrast, crop to one object. No fake observatory posters. No film stills.
- **Files:** 1800 × 1350 px minimum. Line art: PNG. Placeholder filenames match captions: `fig01.png` … `fig16.png`.
- **Alt text** on every figure.
- **Caption does the teaching.** One sentence what it is, one sentence what to notice. Credit on its own line.
- **No AI stand-ins** for named clinic patients, named astronauts, or copyrighted brand packaging.
- **No fake photos.** Invented scenes are diagrams or honest still-lifes (hands, basil, a blister, a clock).

---

## What we will not use (fails e-ink or the voice)

| Tempting original | Why it dies | Kindle replacement |
|---|---|---|
| Rainbow “epigenetic clock” heat map | Color is the data | Two bars or one curve, words *faster / slower* |
| Full-color fMRI montage | Hue is the lesson; mud in gray | One high-contrast slice, labels *on duty* in words |
| Startup skyline for year 12,000 | Cold catalog; also unphotographable | A straight line crossing an S-curve; the line is marked *not a method* |
| Brand pill bottle, readable logo | Advertisement | Plain blister, word *local* |
| Deep-field or CMB leftover | Wrong book | Out. This kitchen is hands, clocks, invoices. |

## Production checklist (every file)

1. Open on a 6-inch preview at grayscale.  
2. Can you name the idea in two seconds without the caption? If not, crop or redraw.  
3. Are all labels still readable at 50% zoom? If not, move them to the caption.  
4. Export gray check + color original. Keep both.  
5. Write alt text ≤ 120 characters.  
6. No fake photos of named people. No brand packaging. No Book 1 CMB oval.
