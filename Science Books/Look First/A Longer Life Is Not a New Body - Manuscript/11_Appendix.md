# APPENDIX — The Scientific Detail

## How to Read These Notes

Each note A*n* twins popular chapter *n*. Units are SI unless stated. Brand names rot; classes of win do not.

String theory, if named: **cannot currently be tested**. It is not needed for a joint or a genome.

## A0 / A1. A Local Dose Is Not a Species Change

**Hot / warm / cold** as in Chapter 1. A local edit that reaches a clinic can warm. A fountain does not warm because the local edit did.

Local: this tissue, this season, this invoice. A joint drug, a lubricant, a replacement, an anti-inflammatory: tools. They do not rewind telomeres, immune grudges, or a hippocampus.

## A2. Life Expectancy, HALE, and the Supercentenarian Tail

**(1)**  Life expectancy at birth is a period summary of death rates, not a promise to a baby. HALE (healthy life expectancy) subtracts years lived with disability, weighted.

Rich-country LE: women often low-to-mid 80s; men a little lower (Japan, Switzerland, Spain, Australia, Italy, Nordics as a band). United States: high 70s in recent ledgers after a dip — a rich-country warning, not a law of biology.

Jeanne Calment: documented ~122 years (1875–1997). Tail, not a mean, not a protocol. Supercentenarians (≥110) are a validation sport, not a clinic.

Maximum observed lifespan has not sprinted the way averages did. Averages moved largely by not losing the young.

Modest further *healthy* years this century in rich-country medicine: **warm**. Everyone shares them: **cold**.

## A3. Hallmarks, Hayflick, and Why One Switch Is Not on the Counter

**(3)**  Hayflick range: many human cell types in a dish divide about 40–60 times, then senesce. Mechanism accent: telomere attrition and a DNA-damage alarm. Mice are not women (longer telomeres, shorter lives).

Hallmarks (López-Otín class lists grew from 9 toward 12): genomic instability, telomere attrition, epigenetic drift, loss of proteostasis, deregulated nutrient sensing, mitochondrial dysfunction, senescence, stem-cell exhaustion, altered intercellular communication, disabled autophagy, chronic inflammation, dysbiosis. A list of twelve is a confession that there is not one fuse.

Aging clocks (epigenetic predictors): **hot** as correlates, **warm** as mechanism, **cold** as a rewind of a person to twenty.

Cousins, not a menu: naked mole rat (decades; late hazard curve that does not climb like a mouse — **hot** as natural history, **cold** as a human dose); Greenland shark (centuries in the cold, a lens that clouds); *Turritopsis* reverse development (**hot** as a cnidarian trick, **cold** as a person’s Tuesday); a documented tortoise career is a tail, not a mean. A mouse living ~30% longer is a paper.

## A4. CRISPR-Class Edits: This Gene, This Tissue, This Invoice

**(4)**  Local fix: this gene, this tissue, this person, this price, this entry-fee harm.

Sickle-cell-class: a single-letter hemoglobin problem; autologous edit of blood-forming stem cells; often wake fetal hemoglobin by breaking a BCL11A-class switch rather than correcting the letter; conditioning chemotherapy; hospital stay; present prices in the low millions of dollars per person. Exagamglogene-class CRISPR and lentiviral gene-addition cousins: **hot** as a *class* (2023-era clinic facts). Not a fountain.

Delivery is most of the problem (AAV, lentivirus, lipid nanoparticles; liver ≠ brain). Immune memory can forbid a second truck. Germline editing is a front (A12), not a gadget.

## A5. Cancer, Plumbing, Neurodegeneration: Many Doors

Cancer: plural careers of a cell; prevention still the cheap hot work; some five-year survivals moved, others barely. A longer ape is a longer mutational exposure unless you invent a surveillance state inside every tissue (**cold** as a product).

Atherosclerosis: lipids, pressure, smoke, shear, clot. Pills, stents, bypasses: incremental, hot as classes. Not a rewind of seventy years of dinner.

Neurodegeneration: Alzheimer’s, Parkinson’s, vascular, Lewy, frontotemporal, ordinary narrowing — plural. A plaque-clearing drug with a modest delay is a local, argued result, not a resurrection.

Partial reprogramming: Yamanaka-class factors, 2013-class full push → pluripotency and teratoma risk (**hot** as a warning). Cyclic or reduced-factor dosing (three-factor eye result in mice, ~2020) is **warm** as a direction. A first-in-human trial is a phase-1 question, not a reset. Epigenetic age can move while mitochondria, senescence, vessels, and memory do not.

150 / 200 / 1,000 years as scheduled products: **cold**.

## A6. The Ten-Percent Sentence, Lesions, and the Energy Bill

**(2)**  Brain ≈ 2% of body mass, ≈ 20% of resting metabolic rate. A miser mesh does not keep a 90% dark warehouse at that wattage.

The “10%” sentence has no volumetric survey behind it. James-style effort talk mutated into a floor plan. Lesion maps (stroke, hippocampus, cerebellum, white-matter cuts) are a cartography of *on duty*. Hemispherectomy and slow tumors show development and time can reassign jobs — not that ninety percent was decorative.

False; correction **hot**. Do not dress as a future unlock.

## A7. Tools with Invoices: Sleep, Hearing, Stimulants, Implants

Sleep: consolidation, clearance, a night shift; fragment it and cognition pays. Hearing: loss is a dementia-risk factor in ledgers; aids are tools. Stimulants: small, messy, known invoices (sleep, appetite, habit). Education: the oldest cognitive tech.

Implants that return a lost function (cochlear, tremor loops): local restorations with scars. Unlocking a spare ninety percent: **cold**.

Wanting (seek, cue, go) ≠ liking (completion stamp). Supernormal cues outbid the thing (the fake egg). A longer healthspan is more calendar for the same Pleistocene loop, not a wise king as a default.

## A9. Vaccines, Packets, Sequence, Compute — and Named Walls

Last ~50 years, uneven and real: childhood-disease rooms closed in lucky kitchens; mRNA as a platform stair; sequencing from ~10⁹-dollar draft to clinic-scale invoices; packets and error-corrected links; cheap compute.

Walls: energy, materials, attention, war, institutions. A stair is not a ruler.

## A10. Logistic Growth, Moore’s Bend, Sequencing’s Collapse

**(6)**  Logistic / S-curve: slow start, steep middle, wall as the room fills.

**(5)**  Sequencing cost per human-scale genome dropped by many orders of magnitude from the first draft; interpretation and action did not drop with the same slope.

Moore’s packing had a drunk middle and a bend (watts, lithography as cathedral, parallelism and specialist silicon as the continuation). Exponential is often a middle photographed without shoulders.

## A11. Why 10,000 / 50 Is Arithmetic, Not a Method

**(7)**  10,000 / 50 = 200 is a ratio of years. It is not a physical method and fails in the rearview (any prior fifty, multiplied, prints the wrong furniture).

No gadget catalog for year 12,000. Honest remainder: energy, heat, coordination, many clocks, losable knowledge.

Proper time: a path near *c* ticks less than a stay-at-home path (**hot**: muons, GPS/airliner clock corrections, twin case). The traveler is younger, not longer-lived. A skip to a later Earth-year is not healthspan. The energy to do it with a cabin is not a pill.

## A12. Meetings, Ledgers, Famines, Fronts

A transistor does not vaccinate a neighborhood or keep a grid up. Institutions store methods; they fail; knowledge dies in fires and fronts. Famine is energy and coordination with a body count.

Germline “enhancement” is a political and ethical front, not a SKU. Look first: do not overwrite a childhood because a slide said the year 12,000 would thank you.

---

## A13. Trials, Harm, and Who Pays for the Local Fix

Trials are how a kitchen refuses a founder’s word; they include *no* as an outcome. Conditioning, cytokine storms, vessel risk: entry fees, not fine print.

Price-as-delivery: a low-millions local fix is a ministry-or-insurer object. Rich-country healthspan gains **warm**; universal share **cold**.

---

## A14. Plasticity Is Real and Expensive

Sensitive periods make children cheaper to reroute than adults. Stroke recovery is overtime, not a hidden room. Practice thickens a map and eats mornings. Tools and other people are part of the method.

Adjusting ≠ spare capacity.

---

## A15. A File Is Not a Worldline

A map of a brain is not a walk. A run of the map is, at best, a second person. The first person remains in the meat or is dead. Travel did not happen.

Restorative chips: local, warm-to-hot as *tools with scars*. Uploads / forever-mind / required Omega-ish last computation: **cold**. Book 1 already broke the required last mind on the shove; this note does not reopen the leftover glow.

Induced torpor: hibernator metabolism is **hot** as zoology; human therapeutic hypothermia is a narrow clinical tool with harms; a Mars-transit freezer is **warm** as logistics and **cold** as a healthspan. Sleep does not stop the many clocks. A long sleep is not a long life.

## A16. Inventory of Claims, by Temperature

**Hot:** lifespan ≠ healthspan; hands age; 10% brain sentence is false; sickle-cell-class local wins exist; last ~50 years saw real uneven gains; most near-term humans are not a different species because of a capsule.

**Warm:** many aging clocks (map real, unified switch not on the counter); modest further healthy years this century in rich-country medicine if institutions hold; S-curves as a pattern; plasticity as expensive reroute; restorative devices.

**Cold:** fountain / 150–200–1000 as scheduled products; everyone shares the gains by default; spare-tank unlock; last 50 × 200 = year 12,000’s kitchen; a gadget catalog for that year; progress guaranteed if we wait; uploads as travel; required forever-mind.
