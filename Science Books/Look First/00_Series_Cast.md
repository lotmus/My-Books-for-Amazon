# Look First — cast, places, and the joke

Written 30 September 2026 from the two live manuscripts. Part files win if this note disagrees with them. Two books. No third volume. Each book still stands alone. This note is how they stay the same kitchen when a reader meets both.

Shared science rules stay in `00_Series_Reference.md`. This file is the people, the rooms, and the humor. Do not invent a second Mara. Do not give Eli a ticket. Do not move Priya into the physics kitchen. Do not import the Moon classroom into Book 1.

## Tone, both books

Short sentences. American English. No swagger. No film titles. The numbers are real. The people are invented. John D. Barrow’s short books are the formal model: a clean line between a measurement and an idea for ideas.

Three temperatures, said the same way in both How to Read sections. Hot: measured, repeated, used to predict. Warm: a working account with real gaps. Cold: speculation sold as inventory. A temperature can change. A local win does not warm a cold costume.

Look first. Seed later. Sentiment is allowed. Sentiment does not get to vote.

Helium, when Book 2 uses the word, is a cold product sold as already on the dock. It is defined in that book’s How to Read. Book 1 does not need the word.

The loaf, the leftover glow, and a second dictionary stay in Book 1. Dirt, delay, dates, and the body stay in Book 2. Both may teach the crew that does not sleep and the library of Earth. Do not delete either copy.

## The joke

The joke is a measurement wearing ordinary furniture. It never cancels the number, and it never winks at a film.

What is allowed:

- A domestic object doing physics without announcing itself. A casserole. Two lamps. A stuck drawer. A seal that sighs. A fridge log.
- One dry line from a person who already has a job. Rohan’s pump joke is worse than his plumbing. Eli does not tell it. Mara logs the pH instead of laughing on cue.
- A nickname that overpays. “Immortal jellyfish.” “Surface asset.” The next sentence sends the bill.
- Hardware with a personality. The airlock, the seal, the pump. People are not mascots.

What is not allowed:

- A punchline that gets to vote.
- Swagger, a slide’s cheer, or a founder’s year played for laughs.
- The same joke moved into a chapter that does not contain that person. Rohan does not appear in a lunar hearing. Linh does not appear in the casserole kitchen.
- A new comic lead. If a scene needs a voice, use the person who is already in that room.

## Main characters

These four are the household. Later chapters may add a hearing. They may not add a second household.

**Mara.** Both books. Invented. She stands still when the argument is about a clock: pot holders, midway between two lamps, entitled to call the flashes one instant. She is the one who later leaves that kitchen. In Book 2 she is on Mars, in a tin, shift number on the radio, “surface asset” on a slide if the slide mentions her at all. She hits a stuck airlock with the heel of her glove. She names the basil and logs the pH. She does not vote. Her hands age. A capsule may buy a joint. It does not buy a new species. Do not give her a different childhood, a different name, or one-sixth gravity. One-sixth g is the Moon.

**Eli.** Book 1’s other lead. Book 2 names him and does not restage him. He walks. He keeps the clock. He and Mara agree about the casserole and disagree about Andromeda. He did not get a ticket. He is not Rohan, and he is not Priya. Do not put him in the tin. Do not give him Priya’s clinic or Rohan’s pump.

**Priya.** Book 2. Mara’s sister. A clinic on a genuine coast, one clinic, two ferries from a city with three hospitals. She still says “we” the way a slide says it. She will not get a ticket. What she wants is a fridge that holds its temperature, a surgeon who shows up for the operation already on her board, and a Tuesday in which her hands can still open a jar. Chapter 43 is that morning. Chapter 46 is the letter that the surgery went the boring way. Do not move her to Mars. Do not make her the woman with the pot holders.

**Rohan.** Book 2. In the tin with Mara. Better at the pumps, worse at the basil. His mother’s street is one of the places a cleanup myth would spend. His jokes arrive late, because the radio is late, and they are about the pump, the mole rat, or the diet that will not fix a marriage. He is not the man who kept the Earth clock.

## Places

**The physics kitchen.** Book 1. Unnamed city. An oven with a window, a second lamp over the stove, the two lamps a couple of meters apart, a sink, a fridge, a cutting board, a casserole. Mara stands. Eli walks. The phone map in a later chapter is the daily proof that the same math keeps a pin on a street. Andromeda is the far example, about two and a half million years of light, not a destination they visit. Do not rename this room, and do not set it on Mars.

**Priya’s coast.** Book 2. A real clinic, a real fridge, a tide that belongs to the people who stayed. Not a named resort. Not the physics kitchen. Eli is not on this coast; Priya is.

**Mara’s camp.** Book 2. Mars, named. Butterscotch sky, a pressure she cannot breathe, a sol of about twenty-four hours and forty minutes, a cheap path home about every twenty-six months, perchlorate in the finest grains, a one-way radio between about three minutes and about twenty-two, an ice vein the color of dirty chalk, a tin, a stuck airlock, basil under grow lights that look magenta because the plant wants red and blue. The poster’s gold is the lie. pH a little under six, logged every morning. A spare pump. This camp does not borrow the Moon’s gravity.

**The Moon.** Book 2’s classroom and the flown record. One-sixth g, a fortnight of night away from the poles, dust that is glass, polar cold that is not a well. Hearings, pencils, and cards. Not Mara’s tin, except when a chapter explicitly points back at her airlock as the same kind of door.

**The far ticket.** Both books, in their own chapters. A crew that does not sleep. A library that stays shut if the sea is already taken. Ice oceans and a second dictionary stay in Book 1. Book 2 may point once.

## What a new page is allowed to do

Use Mara if the scene is her kitchen or her tin. Use Eli if the scene is the clock she left. Use Priya if the scene is the coast. Use Rohan if the scene is the tin and the pump. Use a classroom name only inside that bill.

If a joke will not survive the next sentence’s number, cut the joke. If a new name is doing a job one of these people already has, use the person who has it.
