# APPENDIX — The Scientific Detail

## How to Read These Notes

Each note A*n* twins popular chapter *n*. Units are SI unless stated. *c* is the speed of light. Dates in this kitchen are September 2026; official years will move. Classes of mission are the durable objects.

String theory, if named: a mathematical framework that **cannot currently be tested**. It is not needed for dirt, delay, or dates.

---

## A0 / A1. Delay, Dirt, and Why a Kitchen Is Not a Loaf

**Hot / warm / cold** as in the front matter. A claim can change temperature. A stack that has flown is hotter than a rendering. A slipped year is not, by itself, a con.

**(1)**  *t = d / c*

*where t is one-way light-time, d is distance, c ≈ 3.00 × 10⁸ m s⁻¹.*

Moon: *d ≈ 3.84 × 10⁸ m* ⇒ *t ≈ 1.28 s* (about 1.3 s). Round trip ≈ 2.6 s. A conversation is possible if both ends are patient.

Mars: *d* runs from about 0.37 AU at a kind opposition to about 2.7 AU when the Sun is in the way of a straight radio path and the planets are far. One-way light-time is about 3–4 minutes at the kind end and about 21–24 minutes at the rude end. Round trip: eight to forty-some minutes. Treat *talk* as a retired verb.

A loaf — all times on one inventory — is a courtesy from another book. This appendix does not need it. Dirt and delay are local measurements.

## A2. What Counts as Flown Hardware

Flown: a vehicle or payload that completed the named job in public, with a serial number and a result (including a failure that still arrived).

This-century lunar inventory, as classes (logos will rot):

- Uncrewed crew-shaped loop and splashdown: Artemis I (2022). Empty seats on purpose.
- Sample return, near side: Chang’e-5 (2020).
- Sample return, far side: Chang’e-6 (2024).
- Soft landing and rover, south polar region: Chandrayaan-3 (2023).
- Precision landing with mixed posture: SLIM (2023–24).
- Commercial landers: mixed stand / tip / quiet. Data, not a downtown.
- Crewed lunar orbit or landing, this century: not flown as of this kitchen.

A rendering, a contract, a clean-room module, a hopped prototype: not flown as the job on the slide.

## A3. Slip as Invoice: Historical Pattern

A slip is a moved official date for a named class of mission.

Pattern (not a morality play): Apollo slipped and then walked. Shuttle slipped, flew, then taught a tile-and-wish lesson. Constellation was cancelled as a program, not as a proof that the Moon is unreachable. Artemis-class crew dates have moved in public more than once in the 2020s. Commercial heavy-stack dates have moved in public more than once.

Read a slip as information when it names the long pole (shield, suit, pour, weld, partner can). Read it as fog when it names only “integration.” Fog is not automatically fraud. An empty oven with a roast on the slide is the fraud test.

## A4. Three Days, 1.3 Seconds, One-Sixth *g*

Transit Earth–Moon at the speeds we actually use: on the order of three days, not a Hohmann season.

Surface gravity: *g_Moon ≈ 1.62 m s⁻² ≈ g/6*.

Lunar Δv sketch (order of magnitude, not a flight plan):

**(3)**  LEO → TLI ~ 3.1–3.2 km s⁻¹; lunar orbit capture and landing add several more; Earth return is a heat-shield problem as much as a propellant problem.

Polar ice: permanently shadowed craters show hydrogen signatures consistent with water ice in useful-if-true amounts. Useful-if-true is not a tank farm. ISRU from that ice is a middle-noun program.

## A5. Uncrewed Loops, Landers, Sample Returns

Loop ≠ landing ≠ return ≠ shift.

A loop tests stack, guidance, and Earth entry. A landing tests the last kilometer in a vacuum well. A return tests ascent, rendezvous or direct throw, and a second entry. A shift is a roster that repeats. This century has loops, landings, and returns. It has machine shifts beginning. It has not had a human winter-over on the Moon.

Mass gap, order of: Chang’e-5/6 returns ~1–2 kg; Apollo returns a few hundred kg; a night-surviving habitat is tonnes. 2024–25 truck-service class: a leak that never landed, a tip that still phoned home, a precision landing that ended on its head, a lunar-day shift that stood, a south-polar attempt that ended on its side. Hot as flown manners. Cold as a town.

## A6. Artemis-II-Class: Sortie, Not Town

Class of mission: crew in a cabin on a cis-lunar loop, home in days if the stack behaves. Warm as a *this decade* class. The exact month is a mood.

What it must demonstrate: life support with breath in it for a handful of people over about ten days, crew operations, comms, entry with people. A free-return loop, if used, makes *not landing* the success. What it is not asked to demonstrate: landing, ISRU, a town, a child. Apollo 8 (1968) was this class. A later loop is a resumption, not a promotion.

If the morning has already happened when you read this, you own a flown sortie. You still do not own a settlement.

## A10. Mass, Cadence, and the Unpaid Routine

Cheap per tonne is conditional on routine.

where routine means lights, flies, is refilled, repeats on a planner’s calendar, and does not make the news every time.

Refuel campaigns ≠ one pour. Reliability for a hull that is also a house is a funeral statistic until the rate is boring.

A hundred tonnes of *useful* cargo is one large object or a season of consumables for a small camp. Most of a stack is the propellant it burns. Food for one person for a year, shipped, is on the order of a tonne of dry calories. Left-mass (spares, dirt, a second pump) fattens an outpost. It does not close the loops.

## A11. Windows, EDL, ISRU, Light-Time

**(2)**  Hohmann-class Earth–Mars: ~6–9 months. Synodic repeat: ~26 months (about 2.14 years).

Mars surface pressure: order **6 mbar** (0.6 kPa), ~0.6% of Earth’s sea-level 1013 mbar. Parachutes are underemployed. EDL at house-mass is not rover-mass.

MOXIE-class: a box on a rover made grams-per-hour of O₂ from CO₂. **Hot** as a trick in miniature. Not a tank farm.

Light-time: see (1) and A1. Crewed Mars landing in the 2030s: **warm-to-cold**. City this decade: **cold**.

## A12. The Cargo-Cult Test

**(8)**  A settlement test: if ships from Earth stop, do the babies still eat?

Closed-enough loops to show: air, water, calories, spare parts, medicine, law, teaching. Headcount is not a loop. ISS and Antarctic stations fail the test on purpose and are honest outposts.

Orders: ~1 kg O₂ per person-day; a few liters of water; ~0.5–1 kg dry food. Four people, a year of food: on the order of a tonne. A missed Mars window is ~26 months of whatever you cannot make. A seal you cannot replace ends the kitchen even if the calories close.

ISRU is the middle noun trying to become the third. Papers exist. A city does not.

People ever in orbit: a village. People on the Moon: a last-century dinner party. Near-term Mars seasons, if a lucky decade arrives: a campus, not a nation. Species headcount: order 8 × 10⁹.

Launch cadence and tickets are not a species-move. “We left” is a grammar error unless the referent is a camp.

## A14. Air, Ice, Plastics, Extinction

**(6)**  An extra CO₂ pulse: a large share taken up by ocean and land in decades to a few centuries; a tail in the deep ocean and rocks over millennia. Not a weekend reset to 1800.

Ice sheets: committed response on centuries–millennia. Mountain glaciers faster; sea ice faster still and a different invoice (albedo vs sea-level).

Plastics: persistent solids; fragmentation, not resignation. Production can stop; the drawer remains.

Extinction: one-way on any human clock. A freezer genome is a library, not a grandmother.

## A15. Settlers vs CO₂; Collapse vs Cleanup

**(7)**  Order-of-magnitude: 10⁴ settlers × ~10 t CO₂e/person-year ≈ 10⁵ t/year. Global fossil-and-industry emissions: order 3–4 × 10¹⁰ t/year. The camp is a rounding error and may have the wrong sign if it is a construction site.

The thermostat is industry and land, not headcount on a ship.

If industry collapsed: emissions fall fast; sulfate/soot parasol falls too; short-term unmasking of heat is a warm caution. Collapse is not a cleanup method.

Leaving as cleanup: **cold**.

Rockets are not a carbon offset. Backup-world talk is not a climate policy.

## A25. Travel Time, Dose, and Autonomous Systems

At *v = 0.01 c* (*3000 km/s*), α Centauri (*4.37 ly*) is a *~437 yr* cruise before deceleration; the galaxy is not a weekend. At *0.1 c*, *~44 yr* plus a deceleration burn that is itself a propulsion problem. Kinetic energy per kilogram at *0.01 c* is *~4.5 × 10¹² J/kg* (*~1 kt TNT per kilogram*); at *0.1 c*, a hundred times that. Interstellar dust and ionized gas at *0.1 c* are an energy hazard: a 10 μm grain at that speed is a bullet. This note is not a starship catalog. Those two speeds are enough to see the invoice.

Dose. Galactic cosmic rays in deep space deposit *~0.3–0.6 Sv/yr* behind thin shielding, order of magnitude; cruise measurements (Mars Science Laboratory’s RAD; astronaut TEPC) sit near *0.3–0.5 Sv/yr* depending on solar modulation. A career limit of order *1 Sv* (age- and sex-dependent regulatory numbers; ICRP and NASA have moved the digit) is a few years of unshielded or lightly shielded interplanetary flesh, not a century. Solar particle events add rare, sharp sieverts unless you have a storm shelter. Mars surface is better than cruise (the Mars-surface dose, Appendix A11 of this book and Book 1’s old A25) and worse than Earth. Europa’s *surface* is a non-starter for flesh (*The Universe Has No Now*, the ice-ocean note). Autonomy is not a slogan: error-corrected memory, rad-hard processors, in-situ repair, and a control loop that still makes sense after decades of bit flips. Present pattern engines are not a century-stable control system. The chapter is an outlook. Form (android vs drill) is interface, not physics.

AI risk, temperatures: (i) misuse and scale-error by human principals — hot as a class; (ii) loss of institutional comprehension when procedures are model-generated — warm; (iii) agentic takeover via stable goals plus instrumental convergence — extra hypotheses, not a thermal-history-grade fact; treat as a design constraint for reactors, weapons, and long cruises. “Better” is task-relative. Mind as computation (physicalism) vs mind as non-algorithmic (incompleteness wedge; gravity-linked objective reduction): the second is a minority research program, not a detection. Current pattern engines are not a someone on either reading. Alignment = local control engineering, not an Omega Point.

## A26. Genome Information, Synthesis, and Planetary Protection

Haploid human genome *~3 × 10⁹* bases (*~0.75 GB* uncompressed, far less with compression and redundancy). A typical bacterium is *~10⁵–10⁷* bases; a biosphere archive is orders of magnitude larger than either if you want ecological function, not a mascot. COSPAR planetary protection: icy moons with oceans are Category IV/V concerns — forward contamination can erase the ice-ocean look-first measurement in the other book. Mars is Category IV for life-detection landing sites; a Category V restricted Earth return is the invoice if you bring a sample that might still be a kitchen. Directed panspermia (Crick–Orgel as idea) is engineering plus ethics, not a detection. A *first-ocean* seed on the Hadean/Archean Earth would be absorbed into the LUCA signature (one code, one chirality) and is not presently distinguishable from a local origin; it also does not remove the need for a first kitchen elsewhere. “Messages in junk DNA” have not survived as a channel nature does not use. Synthesis from sequence requires wet-lab machinery that must itself survive the trip — a printed “perfect” organism is logistics plus a library, not new dynamics. Eusocial Earth sketches (leafcutter agriculture; some dulosis) are the scale reminder: competence at nest-size is not a colleague. SR: no FTL mail. QM: no-signaling (entanglement ≠ telepathy). GR: handles still owe the exotic bill, which the other book prices. The QM–GR mismatch is a real gap; it is not a permit.

## A28. Life Expectancy, HALE, and the Supercentenarian Tail

**(9)**  Life expectancy at birth is a period summary of death rates, not a promise to a baby. HALE (healthy life expectancy) subtracts years lived with disability, weighted.

Rich-country LE: women often low-to-mid 80s; men a little lower (Japan, Switzerland, Spain, Australia, Italy, Nordics as a band). United States: high 70s in recent ledgers after a dip — a rich-country warning, not a law of biology.

Jeanne Calment: documented ~122 years (1875–1997). Tail, not a mean, not a protocol. Supercentenarians (≥110) are a validation sport, not a clinic.

Maximum observed lifespan has not sprinted the way averages did. Averages moved largely by not losing the young.

Modest further *healthy* years this century in rich-country medicine: **warm**. Everyone shares them: **cold**.

## A29. Hallmarks, Hayflick, and Why One Switch Is Not on the Counter

**(11)**  Hayflick range: many human cell types in a dish divide about 40–60 times, then senesce. Mechanism accent: telomere attrition and a DNA-damage alarm. Mice are not women (longer telomeres, shorter lives).

Hallmarks (López-Otín class lists grew from 9 toward 12): genomic instability, telomere attrition, epigenetic drift, loss of proteostasis, deregulated nutrient sensing, mitochondrial dysfunction, senescence, stem-cell exhaustion, altered intercellular communication, disabled autophagy, chronic inflammation, dysbiosis. A list of twelve is a confession that there is not one fuse.

Aging clocks (epigenetic predictors): **hot** as correlates, **warm** as mechanism, **cold** as a rewind of a person to twenty.

Cousins, not a menu: naked mole rat (decades; late hazard curve that does not climb like a mouse — **hot** as natural history, **cold** as a human dose); Greenland shark (centuries in the cold, a lens that clouds); *Turritopsis* reverse development (**hot** as a cnidarian trick, **cold** as a person’s Tuesday); a documented tortoise career is a tail, not a mean. A mouse living ~30% longer is a paper.

## A31. Cancer, Plumbing, Neurodegeneration: Many Doors

Cancer: plural careers of a cell; prevention still the cheap hot work; some five-year survivals moved, others barely. A longer ape is a longer mutational exposure unless you invent a surveillance state inside every tissue (**cold** as a product).

Atherosclerosis: lipids, pressure, smoke, shear, clot. Pills, stents, bypasses: incremental, hot as classes. Not a rewind of seventy years of dinner.

Neurodegeneration: Alzheimer’s, Parkinson’s, vascular, Lewy, frontotemporal, ordinary narrowing — plural. A plaque-clearing drug with a modest delay is a local, argued result, not a resurrection.

Partial reprogramming: Yamanaka-class factors, 2013-class full push → pluripotency and teratoma risk (**hot** as a warning). Cyclic or reduced-factor dosing (three-factor eye result in mice, ~2020) is **warm** as a direction. A first-in-human trial is a phase-1 question, not a reset. Epigenetic age can move while mitochondria, senescence, vessels, and memory do not.

150 / 200 / 1,000 years as scheduled products: **cold**.
