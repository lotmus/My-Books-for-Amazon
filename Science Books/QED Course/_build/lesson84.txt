# How to use this lesson
Wick rotation (Lesson 66) turns e^{iS} into e^{−S_E}. At temperature T the Euclidean time is compact with period β=1/T, bosons periodic, fermions antiperiodic. Loop integrals become sums over Matsubara frequencies 2πnT (bosons) or 2π(n+1/2)T (fermions). Expect five to seven hours. This course does not compute a thermal production rate in full. A thermal equilibrium state is a trace, Tr e^{−H/T}, which in the path integral is a closed loop in imaginary time of length 1/T. The Feynman rules of Lesson 49 still apply, with k⁰ replaced by iω_n and ∫dk⁰/2π replaced by T Σ_n. Infrared divergences of Lesson 73 are more severe in a thermal plasma because the Bose factor 1/ω is singular at ω=0; that is a research-level warning, not a calculation performed here.
## Learning objectives
- State Z(T)=∫_{periodic} Dφ exp(−S_E) with τ∈[0,1/T].
- Write bosonic Matsubara frequencies ω_n=2πnT.
- Write fermionic frequencies ω_n=2π(n+1/2)T.
- Note that T→0 recovers ordinary QED, and that thermal photons have a Bose distribution once the k⁰ contour is done.
