# How to use this lesson

This course quantized a U(1) gauge theory of electrons and photons. In the Standard Model the photon is a mixture of the weak hypercharge boson and the third weak isospin boson, after a scalar vacuum expectation value breaks SU(2)×U(1)_Y to U(1)_EM. This lesson states that mixing, identifies α as a low-energy parameter, and lists what this volume computed. It does not derive the Higgs potential or compute a weak decay. Expect four to five hours.

## Learning objectives

- Write the photon as a mixture A_μ=B_μ cosθ_W + W³_μ sinθ_W, with the orthogonal combination the Z.

- State that the photon remains massless because U(1)_EM is unbroken, while W and Z are massive.

- Identify QED as the effective theory below the weak scale for processes that do not change flavor or emit W/Z.

- List what this volume derived and what it did not.

# 1 The photon as a leftover

FIG:
   W³  ---- mix by θ_W ----  Z  (massive)
   B   ---- mix by θ_W ----  A  (massless photon of this course)
ENDFIG

The Standard Model starts with two gauge fields, W^a_μ of SU(2) and B_μ of U(1)_Y. After a scalar doublet acquires a vacuum expectation value, the charged combinations W^± become massive, and the two neutrals mix:

$$ A_μ = B_μ cos θ_W + W³_μ sin θ_W , qquad Z_μ = − B_μ sin θ_W + W³_μ cos θ_W

The generator that annihilates the vacuum is Q=T³+Y/2, Lesson 38's U(1) charge. Because that generator is unbroken, A_μ stays massless: Lesson 71's Ward identity still applies to it. Z_μ is massive and is not a particle of this course.

### Worked example 1

Why does a photon mass term m² A_μ A^μ, forbidden in Lesson 41, remain forbidden after mixing? Because the unbroken generator is still a gauge symmetry of the vacuum, so the same Ward identity k_μ ℳ^μ=0 that killed a photon mass in Lesson 71 still holds. Mixing is a change of basis, not a license to add m_γ.

# 2 QED as an effective theory

At energies well below M_W, W and Z cannot be produced, and their virtual exchange is suppressed by s/M_W². Flavor-conserving, charge-conserving processes of electrons, muons, and photons are then described by this course's ℒ_QED, with two addenda: (i) every electrically charged fermion, not only the electron, runs α (Lesson 78); (ii) hadrons enter vacuum polarization through quark loops, not computed here. Corrections of order s/M_W² and of order α_s are outside the volume.

The fine-structure constant α of Lesson 43 is a low-energy parameter, α(q²≈0). It is not a constant of the high-energy Lagrangian; Lesson 68 already showed that.

# 3 What this volume computed

- Tree-level QED amplitudes of Part VIII, including Compton's (1/4)Σ|ℳ|²=−2e⁴(s/u+u/s) and dσ/dΩ=|ℳ|²/(64π²s).

- One-loop renormalization: m=m₀+A(m₀²), running α(q²) for one lepton, β(e)=e³/(12π²).

- Ward identity k_μ ℳ^μ=0, gauge-parameter independence, Bloch–Nordsieck cancellation of infrared logs.

- Dirac g=2 from the Hamiltonian, and F2(0)=α/(2π) at one loop.

- Leading Lamb mechanism and Uehling's −27 MHz; Schwinger's exponent and prefactor.

- Path-integral re-derivation of the same Feynman rules.

# 4 What this volume did not compute

Weak decays, QCD, gravity, the Higgs potential, two-loop g−2, a complete Lamb shift to kHz, α(M_Z) including quarks, and thermal production rates. Those absences are the honesty of the title page, not defects in Lessons 53–57.

### Worked example 2

A reader who can derive Compton's |ℳ|², absorb A(m₀²) into m, run α with one lepton, cancel IR logs inclusively, and evaluate F2(0)=α/(2π) has completed this course. The Standard Model is the larger theory in which that QED is an accurate chapter.

# Summary

1. The photon is the unbroken mixture of W³ and B.

2. U(1)_EM is massless; W and Z are not.

3. Below M_W, this course's QED is the right theory for flavor-conserving electromagnetic processes.

4. The volume's derived results are the tree processes, one-loop running, IR cancellation, g=2, a=α/(2π), and the Lamb and Schwinger mechanisms.

5. Weak, strong, and gravitational physics are outside the book.

# Solutions

**1.** A=B cosθ_W+W³ sinθ_W, Z=−B sinθ_W+W³ cosθ_W.

**2.** Electric charge Q=T³+Y/2, which annihilates the scalar vacuum expectation value.

**3.** The Ward identity for the unbroken U(1)_EM.

**4.** s/M_Z²∼(1 GeV/91 GeV)²∼10⁻⁴, a 0.01% amplitude correction, smaller than typical one-loop QED.

**5.** Any four from Section 3.

**6.** Any four from Section 4.

**7.** Low-energy: α=e²/4π measured at q²≈0, Lesson 43 and Lesson 68.

**8.** Optical Compton has |q|≪m_hadron; quark loops are already absorbed into the measured α(0) and do not appear as extra diagrams at that scale. At q∼M_Z they must be included in the running.

**9.** Lesson 38's local U(1) is the unbroken electromagnetic subgroup of the electroweak group.

**10.** cosθ_W≈80.4/91.2≈0.882, sin²θ_W≈0.222, the tree-level estimate. Radiative corrections shift the quoted value; this course does not compute them.
