## Reader, voice, method

Students, working engineers who inherit a bench (or a rack), and serious hobbyists building a home lab. Didactic, bench-near, vendor-neutral. Instruments are named plainly as examples (Keysight, Rohde & Schwarz, Tektronix, Keithley, NI, Rigol, Siglent, and the open hobby tools) without endorsement; every quoted spec was checked against the current datasheet or product page (Appendix C), and anything else is labeled "typical" or "example".

Every instrument chapter follows one pattern: what it is for, how it works inside (block diagram in words or a figure), the specs that matter and what they mean, how to choose one, how to use it correctly (setup and pitfalls), worked measurement examples with checked numbers, remote control where it helps (Python/SCPI with a simulation mode), and What You'll Actually Use.

Threads: hook, "Where you meet this", From the Bench (every chapter), On the Job (at least one per part, 11 parts), Common misconception, Key idea, Practice (Core / Extension / Challenge) with full worked solutions in Appendix B naming the usual mistake.

One-home rule: the theory lives in Books 1-8 and is cross-referenced, not re-derived: uncertainty and the decibel (Book 1 Ch 15, 19), the Smith chart (Book 1 Ch 37, Book 4 Ch 4), loading and op-amp noise (Book 2 Ch 12, 14), device physics (Book 3), S-parameters, receiver noise, phase noise, PA measurement and power detection (Book 4 Ch 5, 25, 26, 37, 40), modulation, EVM, OFDM, chirps, IoT radios (Book 5 Ch 4, 6, 12, 21), regulators and batteries (Book 6 Ch 2, 12), the eye, TDR/VNA of interconnect, EMC detectors, chambers and LISNs (Book 7 Ch 14, 20, 23-27), Python and C# bench code and the LabVIEW chapter (Book 8 Ch 21, 22 and the new LabVIEW chapter). Book 9 owns the instruments themselves: inside, specs, choice, correct use.

Hardware threads: the reader lab (ESP32-S3, BME280, LoRa radio, 18650 module...) as the device under test; the budget bench (NanoVNA-H4, tinySA Ultra, RTL-SDR Blog V4, HackRF One, 12-bit entry scopes) and the professional bench side by side.
