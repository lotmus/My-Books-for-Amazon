## Description (KDP HTML)

<p><b>Follow every watt from the grid to the load, and learn where conversion losses become heat.</b></p>
<p>A power supply is not a box that turns one voltage into another. Its efficiency, control loop, magnetic components, thermal limits, battery, and connection to the grid all shape whether a real system works. <i>Power and Energy</i> develops those decisions with calculations and engineering examples.</p>
<p>Across 22 chapters, build linear regulators, buck, boost, buck-boost, flyback, forward, and LLC converters; choose and analyze magnetic components; and study rectifiers, power-factor correction, high voltage, switching losses, loop compensation, batteries and chargers, inverters and motor drives. Later chapters follow power through data centers and the grid, then examine multiphase regulators, gate drivers, pulsed power, solar tracking, and wireless power transfer.</p>
<p>Worked examples connect circuit equations to efficiency, component stress, thermal behavior, control stability, and system-level energy. Practice answers are collected in Appendix B, which is a concise answer key: routine substitutions are shown, while chapter examples provide the longer derivations.</p>
<p>For engineering students and practitioners working with power supplies, converters, batteries, renewable energy, and electrical systems. This volume assumes circuit analysis from Book 1 and feedback fundamentals from Book 2. Book 6 of the nine-book Electrical Engineering Series.</p>


<p><b>Reader level:</b> Recommended for ages 18 and up: engineering students and adults with circuit-analysis and feedback fundamentals. The book includes hazardous-energy topics; practical work requires appropriate training, rated equipment, and supervision. Age is not a safety qualification.</p>
