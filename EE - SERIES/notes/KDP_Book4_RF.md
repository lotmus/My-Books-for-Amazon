# KDP listing: RF, Microwave, and Transceivers (Electrical Engineering Series, Book 4)

Author: Lothar J. Musiol

## Description (KDP HTML)

<p><b>From the transmission line to the complete radio: matching, filters, antennas, amplifiers, receivers and synthesizers, with the numbers an RF engineer actually works with.</b></p>
<p>Why does a 10 pF capacitor turn into an inductor at 2.45 GHz? Why does half a decibel of cable cost a ground station almost two decibels of sensitivity? Why do base stations use the Doherty amplifier? <i>RF, Microwave, and Transceivers</i> answers those questions in 41 chapters:</p>
<ul>
<li><b>Passive structures:</b> transmission lines and reflection, L and pi matching, the Smith chart at work, S-parameters, filters and waveguide, antennas and arrays, couplers, microstrip by design, the Bode-Fano limit, cavities, propagation, circulators and antenna measurement.</li>
<li><b>Active circuits:</b> the load line, amplifier classes, linearity and the Doherty, heat and combining, receiver noise and dynamic range, phase noise and the PLL, radar, the LNA, mixers and frequency planning, and the link budget.</li>
<li><b>The transmitter in depth:</b> ruggedness and protection, vacuum tubes, RF exposure, transceiver isolation, class E and F design, envelope tracking and outphasing, PA measurement, digital predistortion, fractional-N and DDS synthesis, power control, and the classes side by side.</li>
</ul>
<p>Every chapter ends with <b>What You'll Actually Use</b>, an honest list that separates the everyday working tools from the theory you mainly need for exams or deeper study. <b>From the Bench</b> notes add the practical layer: self-resonance, stack-ups from the fabricator, LNA placement, ruggedness testing, spur charts and coupler directivity.</p>
<p>Inside: 41 diagrams, including Smith-chart matching, filter and antenna responses, load lines, noise cascades and link budgets, all computed from the worked examples; practice problems with answers; and a linked source list. Written for engineering students and working engineers moving into RF. Book 4 of the seven-book Electrical Engineering Series; builds on <i>Foundations of Electronics</i>, and Book 5, <i>Communications, Wireless, and SDR</i>, builds on it.</p>
