# KDP listing: Circuits, Components, and Control (Electrical Engineering Series, Book 2)

Author: Lothar J. Musiol

## Description (KDP HTML)

<p><b>Real parts, real op-amps, real control loops: the working core of electronics design, from an engineer who builds with it.</b></p>
<p>Why does a "10 µF" capacitor measure 4 µF on your board? Why does a follower that works on the bench oscillate once you add a cable? Why does a loop that was stable in analog get worse when you move it into a microcontroller? <i>Circuits, Components, and Control</i> answers those questions in 28 chapters:</p>
<ul>
<li><b>Analog and digital basics:</b> bias points and headroom, logic levels and noise margins, Boolean algebra, flip-flops, counters and timing with metastability.</li>
<li><b>Op-amps with real numbers:</b> feedback, followers and capacitive loads, gain-bandwidth and slew rate, noise, DC error budgets, comparators, instrumentation amplifiers, sample-and-hold circuits and voltage references.</li>
<li><b>Real components:</b> resistors, capacitors and inductors as you buy them, with derating, drift, pulses, current sensing, decoupling, DC-bias loss and inductor datasheets, each topic in one place.</li>
<li><b>Filters:</b> from first-order RC to Sallen-Key and multiple-feedback designs with standard parts.</li>
<li><b>Control:</b> plant and controller, PID with anti-windup, lead and lag, digital control and sampling delay, state feedback and observers, the H-bridge motor drive, root locus, stability margins and oscillators.</li>
</ul>
<p>Every chapter ends with <b>What You'll Actually Use</b>, an honest list that separates the everyday working tools from the theory you mainly need for exams or deeper study. <b>From the Bench</b> notes add the practical layer: Kelvin connections, isolation resistors, dead time, synchronizers, error budgets and how to measure a loop on real hardware.</p>
<p>Inside: 48 diagrams, 90 worked examples, practice problems with full solutions, a formula reference and a glossary. A textbook that expects you to work problems; it builds on Book 1, <i>Foundations of Electronics</i>. Book 2 of the seven-book Electrical Engineering Series.</p>
