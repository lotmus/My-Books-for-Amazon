## Description (KDP HTML)

<p><b>Make the physical product work: package, board, heat path, signal path, and emissions test.</b></p>
<p>A chip can be correct and still fail in its product. The package and board must deliver hundreds of amperes, preserve fast signals, remove heat, survive stress, and keep switching noise from escaping or corrupting a radio. <i>Packaging, Layout, EMC, and Test</i> follows those problems from silicon package to laboratory report.</p>
<p>Across 29 chapters, study advanced packages and chiplets, power delivery, substrate materials, return paths, thermal design, MEMS, reliability, decoupling, stack-up, crosstalk, high-speed loss, optical interconnect, antenna-in-package, and design for manufacture and test. The second part develops EMC practice: emissions and immunity, measurement limits and detectors, chambers, LISNs, simulation, and the work performed in a compliance lab.</p>
<p>Worked examples show how to calculate and interpret the quantities that guide layout and test. The final robot example brings together the nine series subjects while keeping each volume's technical boundary clear. Appendix B provides worked solutions to the practice problems.</p>
<p>For electrical and electronics engineers, students, and product teams who need to understand how package, board, thermal, signal-integrity, EMC, and test decisions meet. Book 7 of the nine-book Electrical Engineering Series.</p>


<p><b>Reader level:</b> Recommended for ages 18 and up: engineering students, working engineers, and technical product teams. Readers benefit from prior electronics, circuit, and measurement experience; this is a specialist reference, not a general introduction. Age is a guide; preparation matters more.</p>
