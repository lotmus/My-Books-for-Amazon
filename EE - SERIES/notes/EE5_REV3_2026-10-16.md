# Book 5 REV3 editorial and technical pass — 16 October 2026

`Communications_Wireless_and_SDR_Book5_REV1.docx` preserves the initially reviewed version. REV2 remains the preceding editorial copy. REV3 is based on REV2; the unnumbered master is unchanged.

## Changes in REV3

- Added early shaded “first-cut” notes to the modulation and OFDM chapters, making the ideal assumptions and the later implementation limits explicit.
- Made field consequences explicit for fade margin, an OFDM cyclic prefix shorter than the significant echo, failed HARQ recovery, timing drift, and LoRa airtime/off-time violations.
- Added a 100 ms synchronization example: a 100 ppm sample-clock mismatch accumulates ten symbols and loses framing without continuous timing recovery.
- Distinguished the 2–3 dB coding gap to Shannon from a hardware implementation allowance; instructed readers not to double-count measured EVM components.
- Added calibration and uncertainty limits for RTL-SDR and low-cost analyzer checks; cited ETSI/3GPP, NIST, Ettus, Analog Devices, and ETSI SRD sources.
- Added a usable temperature rule: measure the selected front end across its rated range; each 1 dB NF rise costs about 1 dB sensitivity. No universal NF dB/°C or lifetime-drift number is asserted because it depends on the selected device and assembly.
- Clarified the regional, sub-band-specific LoRa duty-cycle and access conditions.
- Added restrained bench observations and a two-movement checkpoint to break the Chapter 9–15 run.
