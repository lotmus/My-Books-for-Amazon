## Findings and Corrections Made

- Chapters 4 and 38 treated an isolation transformer as an easy general answer for non-earth-referenced scope measurements. Clarified that the scope stays protectively grounded, that differential/isolated probes or isolated-input scopes must be rated for the measurement, and that isolating a device under test does not make exposed live circuitry safe to touch. This follows [Tektronix probe-safety guidance](https://www.tek.com/en/documents/whitepaper/abcs-probes-primer).
- Chapter 38's mini curve tracer connected a possible 4.0 V collector signal to the ADS1115 without stating its supply or analog-input limits. Added the required 5 V analog rail and bidirectional I2C level shifting to the 3.3 V MCU, and clarified that ¬±4.096 V is the ADC's differential PGA range, not its allowed pin voltage. TI states that analog inputs must not exceed VDD + 0.3 V and that digital VIH is 0.7 VDD: [ADS1115 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ads1115.pdf). The MCP4728's high input threshold is also 0.7 VDD: [MCP4728 datasheet](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/en/DeviceDoc/22187E.pdf).
- Updated the related Appendix B answer, the DOCX master, the Python listing, and the extracted Book 9 mirror so the ADS1115 input limits, 5 V analog rail, and 3.3 V I2C level shifting are consistent.

A closer engineering read found that Chapter 38 blurred a simulated result and a buildable, protected instrument. The software had no hardware collector-current limit, treated a MOSFET gate as a drop-in BJT base drive, and implied that the simulated pulse average established a TO-92 thermal margin. The PC backend also imported a model helper not printed in the book, while the prose said Listing 7.1 read a CSV that it did not load.

Corrections now in the DOCX and regenerated mirror:

- Narrowed the stated build to a low-voltage NPN BJT demonstration; MOSFET drain testing requires a separately validated current-limited fixture.
- Explicitly state that the software does not limit current, require verified hardware current limiting, and direct readers to pulse SOA and transient thermal data. A 50 ms off time is no longer presented as proof of safe junction temperature.
- Printed the small `l07_1_extract.py` model helper and a NumPy CSV import example that passes the measured VCE and IC columns to Listing 7.1's `early_voltage` function.
- Corrected the nominal pulse duty from about 10% to about 12.3%, changed average power from 0.02 W to about 0.025 W, made the practice problem stay within the example range, and labeled the model fit as synthetic rather than evidence about a real transistor.

The simulation reran and produced 164 points; the 200 ?A curve yielded 41 selected points over 0 to 3.581 V. That proves only the software/model handoff. The MCP4728 internal-reference/gain settings and ADS1115 input restrictions were checked against the manufacturers' [MCP4728 datasheet](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/en/DeviceDoc/22187E.pdf) and [ADS1115 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ads1115.pdf).

## DAC command-format and simulated-path recheck (13 October 2026)
