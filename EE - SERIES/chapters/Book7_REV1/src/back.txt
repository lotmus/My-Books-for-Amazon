H1: Appendix A. Constants and Formulas Used in This Book
P: Memory bandwidth = width × per-pin rate/8. DDR5-6400 channel: 51.2 GB/s. HBM4 at 11 Gbit/s × 2048 bits: 2.8 TB/s (55×). Interface power = bit rate × energy per bit.
P: Current: I = P/V; 700 W at 0.8 V = 875 A.
P: Connection density = 10⁶/p² per mm² (p in µm). Reticle limit 26 × 33 mm = 858 mm².
P: Target impedance Ztarget = ΔV/ΔI. Copper sheet resistance ρ/t: 0.49 mΩ/square for 1 oz (35 µm); ρCu = 1.72 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m.
P: Stripline dielectric loss αd ≈ 8.686πf√εr tan δ/c dB/m. At 10 GHz: FR-4 (4.5, 0.020) 38.6 dB/m; low-loss (3.55, 0.0037) 6.3 dB/m.
P: Return-current fraction within ±a of a trace at height h: (2/π) arctan(a/h). Critical length ≈ tr·v/6. Via stub resonance ≈ c/(4l√εr).
P: Thermal: Tj = Tcoolant + PΣθ; interface θ = t/(kA); coolant P = ṁcpΔT; water cp = 4186 J/(kg·K); air 1005 J/(kg·K), 1.2 kg/m³.
P: MEMS: x = a/ω0²; C = ε0A/g; thermal noise an = √(4kBTω0/(mQ)); resonator bandwidth f0/Q.
P: Yield: Y = exp(−D0A); dies per wafer ≈ π(d/2)²/A − πd/√(2A). Shoreline bandwidth = lanes per mm × rate.
P: Bond wire ≈ 1 nH/mm; XL = 2πfL; ground bounce = NL ΔI/Δt.
P: Solder shear strain Δγ ≈ Δα ΔT DNP/h; Engelmaier Nf ≈ ½(Δγ/2εf)^(1/c), c ≈ −0.442, εf ≈ 0.325. Black: MTTF = A J^(−n) exp(Ea/kBT).
P: Capacitor |Z| = √(ESR² + (ωL − 1/ωC)²); fSRF = 1/(2π√(LC)); parallel count N ≈ 2πf ESL/Z.
P: Stripline (IPC-2141): Z0 ≈ (60/√εr) ln[4b/(0.67π(0.8w + t))]; delay √εr/c per unit length.
P: Crosstalk: Kb = ¼(Cm/C + Lm/L); Lsat = trv/2; far end Vf/Va ≈ (len/2v)(Cm/C − Lm/L)/tr; spacing rule 1/(1 + (s/h)²).
P: High-speed links: fNyquist = Rs/2; PAM4 penalty 9.5 dB; BER 10⁻¹² needs Q = 7.03; KP4 threshold 2.4 × 10⁻⁴ (Q = 3.49).
P: Optics: energy per bit = P/rate; single-mode fiber ≈ 0.35 dB/km at 1310 nm; margin = Ptx − Σlosses − Psens.
P: Gyroscope: aC = 2Ωv, v = x0ωd. Dead reckoning: x = ½abt²; θ = bt; θRW = ARW√t.
P: TSV R = ρL/A. Stack yield = yᴺ. Patch L ≈ λ₀/(2√εr); array EIRP = Pel + 20 log₁₀N + Gel. Assembly yield Y = exp(−Np); defect level DL = 1 − Y^(1−T); stencil area ratio d/(4t) ≥ 0.66. TDR: Z = Z₀(1 + ρ)/(1 − ρ), resolution tr·v/2; TRL usable 20°–160°.
P: Robot: Pmech = τω; Ibus = Pmech/(ηVbus); runtime = E/Pavg.
P: dBµV/m = 20 log₁₀(E/1 µV/m); 100 µV/m = 40.0 dBµV/m; 150 µV/m = 43.5; 200 µV/m = 46.0; 500 µV/m = 54.0.
P: Differential-mode loop over ground (Ott): E ≈ 263×10⁻¹⁶ f² A I / r (V/m; f in Hz, A in m², I in A, r in m).
P: Common-mode cable (Ott): E ≈ 1.257×10⁻⁶ f L Icm / r (V/m; L in m).
P: FCC Part 15 Class B at 3 m: 40.0 (30–88 MHz), 43.5 (88–216), 46.0 (216–960), 54.0 dBµV/m (above 960 MHz). Distance 10 m to 3 m: +10.5 dB.
P: CISPR 32 Class B at 10 m, quasi-peak: 30 dBµV/m (30–230 MHz), 37 dBµV/m (230–1000 MHz).
P: Field from a reading: E = V + AF + Lcable (dB). CISPR bandwidths: 200 Hz, 9 kHz (150 kHz–30 MHz), 120 kHz (30–1000 MHz), 1 MHz (above 1 GHz).
P: Conducted, CISPR 32 Class B: 66–56 dBµV QP (150–500 kHz), 56 QP / 46 AV (0.5–5 MHz), 60 QP / 50 AV (5–30 MHz). LISN 50 µH / 50 Ω.
P: ESD (IEC 61000-4-2): 150 pF, 330 Ω; first peak about 3.75 A per kV (contact). Surge (IEC 61000-4-5): 1.2/50 µs, 8/20 µs, 2 Ω.
P: Multiphase buck: D = Vout/Vin; ripple ΔI = (Vin − Vout)D/(L fs); output ripple at N fs. Fan laws: Q ∝ N, Δp ∝ N², P ∝ N³, sound ≈ 50 log₁₀(N₂/N₁) dB.
P: Silicon resonator TCf ≈ −30 ppm/K uncompensated. Bond-wire low-pass τ = L/(2Z₀), added rise ≈ 2.2τ. Arrhenius AF = exp[(Ea/kB)(1/Tu − 1/Ts)], kB = 8.617 × 10⁻⁵ eV/K.
P: MLCC effective C = C_nom × k_bias × k_temp (X7R ±15 %). Via aspect ratio = thickness/drill, about 10:1 standard. Crosstalk sums: worst case Σ|xᵢ|, statistical √Σxᵢ². Jitter TJ = DJ + 2Q·RJ; Q(10⁻⁶) = 4.75.
P: Placement Cpk = min(USL − µ, µ − LSL)/(3σ). Friis FSPL = 20 log₁₀(4πd/λ); noise floor −174 dBm/Hz + 10 log₁₀B + NF. VNA noise floor ∝ IFBW; sweep ≈ points/IFBW. Magnetometer heading error ≈ arctan(B_off/H); wire field µ₀I/(2πr).
P: Slot resonance f = c/2L; aperture SE ≈ 20 log₁₀(λ/2L). Cable resonance c/2L (λ/2), c/4L (λ/4). Scan to 1, 2, or 5 GHz for highest internal frequency below 108 MHz, 500 MHz, or 1 GHz; above, 5× (FCC ≤ 40 GHz, CISPR 32 ≤ 6 GHz). FCC peak limit = average + 20 dB; average = peak − 20 log₁₀(1/d).
P: Stepped scan: step ≤ RBW/2; T = steps × dwell. Fully anechoic above 1 GHz: S_VSWR ≤ 6 dB; horn footprint w = 2d tan(θ/2). Touch current I = 2πfCV; CM choke f₀ = 1/(2π√(L·2C_Y)).
P: Signal-line surge through 42 Ω; TVS V_C = V_BR + I_PP R_dyn. 80/80 rule: x̄ + kS ≤ L, k = 2.04 (3), 1.69 (4), 1.52 (5), 1.42 (6), 1.20 (12). Motor-drive common mode I = C dv/dt.
P: Substrates: 77 GHz microstrip αd = 27.3 εr(εeff − 1) tan δ/[√εeff(εr − 1)λ₀], αc ≈ 8.686 Rs/(Z₀w), Rs = √(πfµ₀ρ); spiral L = 2.34 µ₀n²d_avg/(1 + 2.75ρ). Vibration: Grms = √(π/2 fn Q PSD), Q ≈ √fn, Z₁σ = 9.8 Grms/fn² in; Steinberg Z = 0.00022B/(c t r √L). IMU: offset = (up + down)/2, scale = (up − down)/2, σ = noise density/√(2T). Optics: ER = 10 log₁₀(P1/P0), OMA = P1 − P0. I²C rise tr = 0.8473 RC. Scan time = patterns × chain length/f_shift. Optical: depth of focus ±Nc; Nyquist 1/(2 × pixel pitch). Filter heat P(1 − 10^(−IL/10)); drift = TCF × ΔT.
H1: Appendix B. Answers to the Practice Problems
H2: Chapter 1
P: 1. Four stacks give 4 × 2.8 = 11.2 TB/s. A DDR5-6400 channel is 64 bits × 6.4 GT/s/8 = 51.2 GB/s, so four channels give 204.8 GB/s, 55 times less.
P: 2. I = P/V = 1000/0.7 = 1429 A.
P: 3. 1024 × 9.6 × 10⁹/8 = 1.23 × 10¹² B/s, 1.23 TB/s.
P: 4. 8 TB/s is 64 Tbit/s, and 64 × 10¹² × 2 × 10⁻¹² J = 128 W.
P: 5. 2800/51.2 = 54.7, so about 55 channels.
P: 6. Its connections run millimeters through a silicon interposer or bridge at micrometer pitch, so thousands of signals fit and each costs little energy; a board-level DIMM connection is centimeters long at a millimeter pitch.
P: 7. Ridge = peak/bandwidth = 400 × 10¹²/3.2 × 10¹² = 125 operations per byte. Workloads below 125 operations per byte are limited by memory.
P: 8. Each token reads the whole model once, so 16 × 10¹²/140 × 10⁹ = 114 tokens per second.
H2: Chapter 2
P: 1. 10⁶/55² = 331 per mm² and 10⁶/25² = 1600 per mm².
P: 2. Density goes as 1/p², so (150/25)² = 36 times.
P: 3. A chip-scale package may be at most about 1.2 times the die in each dimension: 1.2 × 5 = 6 mm, so about 6 × 6 mm.
P: 4. Its join is wire bonding and its skeleton is a lead frame.
P: 5. The lithography tool's exposure field, the reticle, is limited to 26 × 33 mm.
P: 6. No. Its redistribution layer is built directly on the molded die to form the package itself; an interposer is a separate wiring layer between dies and a substrate.
P: 7. At 9 µm, 10⁶/81 = 12,346 per mm², and 50 mm² holds about 617,000 connections. At 40 µm, 625 per mm² gives 31,250, about twenty times fewer.
P: 8. Below about 20 µm the solder volume per joint becomes too small to accommodate warpage and coplanarity variation, and neighboring joints bridge.

P: 2. R□ = ρ/t = 1.72 × 10⁻⁸/70 × 10⁻⁶ = 0.25 mΩ per square.
P: 3. R□ = 1.72 × 10⁻⁸/17.5 × 10⁻⁶ = 0.98 mΩ; three squares give 2.95 mΩ, and 100 A drops 0.29 V, far too much for a core rail.

P: 5. 600 A/0.1 A = 6000 bumps for the supply, and as many again for the return.

P: 8. D = 1/12 = 0.083. ΔI = (12 − 1) × 0.083/(150 × 10⁻⁹ × 10⁶) = 0.917/0.15 = 6.1 A peak to peak.

P: 1. Half the return current lies within ±h, here ±0.2 mm, a band 0.4 mm wide centered under the trace.
P: 2. (2/π) arctan 5 = 0.637 × 1.373 = 0.87, so 87 %.
