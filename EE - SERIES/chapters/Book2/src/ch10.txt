H1: Chapter 10 — The Loop Under the Model
P: A robot arm reaching for a cup looks like intelligence. Underneath, it is a small, unglamorous loop running thousands of times a second: measure, compare, correct, repeat.
P: Where you meet this: Motor speed control, thermostats, voltage regulators, drone stabilization and robot joints.
P: Book 7 names the 2026 robots and the four layers: general model, task planner, motion tracker, mechanism. This book owns the tracker. It does not repeat the product names or the success percentages.
FIG: fig10_1.png | Figure 10.1 — The loop under every controlled machine: measure, compare with the reference, correct, repeat.
P: A joint tracker at 200 Hz has a period of 1/200 s, which is 5 ms. Five milliseconds is slow for a switching regulator and fast for a language model. It is the right speed for a joint that must not wait on a data center. The safety controller is not that model. When they disagree, the safety controller is the one allowed to move the joint. Åström and Murray, or Nise, are the books for this loop.
P: The same Bode habits from Chapter 5 apply: a plant, a compensator, a phase margin. A robot policy does not replace that loop. The model proposes. The tracker and the safety layer dispose.
P: Key idea. The 200 Hz tracker is a 5 ms period. That arithmetic lives only here.
