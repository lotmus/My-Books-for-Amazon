H1: Appendix A. Quick Reference: Formulas, Spec Cheat Sheets, Checklists, and SCPI Basics
H2: A.1 Formulas used in this book
P: Accuracy (Chapter 1): error = ±(% of reading × reading + % of range × range), or ±(% of reading + counts × resolution).
P: Uncertainty (Chapter 2): combine independent standard uncertainties as the root sum of squares; a rectangular limit ±a has u = a/√3; expanded U = k u with k = 2 for about 95 %. Test uncertainty ratio = tolerance/U, 4:1 or better.
P: Loading (Chapter 3): a source Rs read by a meter Rin reads Rin/(Rs + Rin) of the true value; a probe's capacitance has a reactance 1/(2πfC).
P: Remote sense (Chapter 5): the load voltage error without sense is I × (R_lead+ + R_lead−).
P: Diode from the log plot (Chapter 7): n = slope per decade/(Vt ln 10), Vt ln 10 = 59.5 mV at 300 K; Rs = excess voltage/I at high current; Zener rz = ΔV/ΔI.
P: Early voltage (Chapter 7): VA = Ic/slope − VCE at the point used; ro = 1/slope.
P: MOSFET (Chapter 7): subthreshold slope S = ΔVgs per decade, slope factor n = S/(Vt ln 10); gm = ΔId/ΔVgs; Rds(on) = Vds/Id at small Vds, four-wire.
P: Capacitances (Chapter 8): Cgd = Crss, Cgs = Ciss − Crss, Cds = Coss − Crss. Junction: Cj = Cjo/(1 + Vr/Vj)^M.
P: Gate charge and double pulse (Chapter 8): Q = Ig t at constant gate current; first pulse t₁ = L I/V; E = ∫ v i dt; Qrr ≈ ½ Irrm trr for a triangle; Zth = ΔT/P with ΔT = ΔV/K.
P: Integrating converters (Chapter 12): rejection |sin(πfT)/(πfT)| for aperture T; noise ∝ 1/√T; true RMS of a square wave equals its peak, of a triangle peak/√3; average-responding meters read 1.111 × the rectified average.
P: Impedance (Chapter 13): D = ESR/X = 1/Q; Cs = Cp(1 + D²); SRF = 1/(2π√(LC)).
P: Low level (Chapter 14): Johnson noise √(4kTRB), 4.07 nV for 10 kΩ in 0.1 Hz at 300 K; delta method V = (V₊ − V₋)/2; settling to 0.1 % takes 6.9 τ.
P: Counters (Chapter 15): reciprocal resolution = clock period (or interpolator resolution)/gate time; trigger jitter = noise/slew rate.
P: Oscilloscopes (Chapter 16): rise time ≈ 0.35/bandwidth; displayed rise time = √(signal² + scope² + probe²); capture time = memory/sample rate; SINAD = 6.02 ENOB + 1.76 dB.
P: Probes (Chapter 17): ground-lead inductance about 1 nH/mm, ringing at 1/(2π√(LC)); a 10× passive probe loads with 10 MΩ and 10 to 15 pF.
P: TDR (Chapter 18): ρ = (Z − Z0)/(Z + Z0); distance d = v t/2; resolution about v tr/2.
P: Generators (Chapters 19 and 20): DDS f_out = M f_clk/2^N; 0 dBm on 50 Ω = 0.224 V rms; AWG sinc(f/fs) roll-off and images at fs ± f; mismatch uncertainty 20 log(1 ± |Γs||Γl|); φrms = √(2 × 10^(dBc/10)), jitter = φrms/(2πf).
P: Spectrum analyzers (Chapter 21): DANL = −174 dBm/Hz + NF + 10 log(RBW); sweep time ≈ 2 to 3 × span/RBW²; optimum mixer level (2 TOI + DANL)/3; dynamic range 2/3 (TOI − DANL).
P: FFT and EMI (Chapter 22): bin = fs/N; RBW = ENBW × fs/N; dBµV = dBm + 107; field E = V + AF + cable loss.
P: Power (Chapter 23): pulse power = average/duty cycle; mismatch 20 log(1 ± |Γs||Γl|).
P: Noise (Chapter 24): F = ENR/(Y − 1) in linear terms; Friis F = F1 + (F2 − 1)/G1; L(f) = noise in RBW − carrier − 10 log(RBW); cross-correlation gains 5 log10(N) dB.
P: Network analysis (Chapters 25 to 27): Γ = (Z − Z0)/(Z + Z0); RL = −20 log|Γ|; VSWR = (1 + |Γ|)/(1 − |Γ|); group delay = −dφ/dω; time-domain range = 1/Δf, resolution ≈ 1/span; OIP3 = Pout + ΔIM3/2; PAE = (Pout − Pin)/Pdc.
P: Analog modulation (Chapter 28): AM sidebands 20 log(m/2) dBc; FM β = Δf/fm, Carson 2(Δf + fm); carrier nulls at β = 2.405, 5.520, 8.654.
P: Digital modulation (Chapter 29): EVM from noise 1/√SNR, from phase noise φrms in radians, from gain imbalance half the split, combined as a root sum of squares; MER = −20 log(EVM) with average normalization; device EVM = √(measured² − residual²); BER = ½ erfc(Q/√2); occupied bandwidth Rs(1 + α).
P: Transmitter spectrum (Chapter 30): ACLR = P_adjacent/P_main; noise in a channel = N(dBm/Hz) + 10 log(BW); uncorrelated contributions add in power, correlated ones in voltage; mask integration + 10 log(limit BW/RBW); backoff ≥ CCDF value at 0.01 %.
P: OFDM (Chapter 31): subcarrier spacing 15 × 2^µ kHz; symbol and CP scale by 1/2^µ; NR occupied bandwidth = RBs × 12 × spacing; path covered by the CP = c × CP.
P: Chirps and pulses (Chapter 32): LoRa symbol 2^SF/B, bit rate SF × B/2^SF × CR; FMCW beat fb = 2RS/c, resolution c/(2B); duty = τ × PRF; average = peak × duty; lines 20 log(duty) below the peak.
P: Optical, thermal, and EMC (Chapter 33): Δf = cΔλ/λ²; OTDR resolution ≈ cτ/(2n); spot = IFOV × distance, three pixels per target; common-mode radiation E ≈ 1.257e−6 × I f L/d.
P: Automation (Chapters 34 and 36): block header #<n><length>; time per reading = integration + round trip unless buffered; Cpk = min(USL − μ, μ − LSL)/(3σ); GR&R = 6σ_ms/tolerance; UPH = 3600 × sites/(test + handling).
P: Threshold voltage (Chapter 35): by linear extrapolation, Vth = Vgs(gm,max) − Id/gm,max − Vds/2; by constant current, the Vgs at which Id reaches the stated current (often 250 µA or 1 mA) with the drain tied to the gate. Name the method with the number.
P: Temperature soak (Chapter 35): after a step ΔT with time constant τ, the error falls to a band b after t = τ ln(ΔT/b); 300 s × ln(50/0.2) = 1656 s, plus the stability window.
P: Budget bench (Chapters 37 and 38): a VNA proves a match only to about 10 dB short of its S11 floor (|Γ| ± floor); 8-bit SNR 49.9 dB plus 10 log(Nyquist bandwidth/bin); mini tracer current resolution = ADC LSB/R_sense; test uncertainty ratio = tolerance/reference uncertainty.

H2: A.4 Specification cheat sheets
P: Power supply: ranges and power envelope, programming and readback accuracy, load and line regulation, ripple and noise (rms and peak-to-peak, with bandwidth), transient recovery, remote sense, output impedance; ask for the current-limit behavior.
P: SMU: quadrants and power envelope, lowest current range and its noise, force and measure accuracy per range, settling time, pulse width, guard and triax; ask for the speed at the needed accuracy.
P: DMM: digits and counts, accuracy ±(% of reading + % of range) for each function and period, NMRR at NPLC ≥ 1, input resistance (10 MΩ or above 10 GΩ), true-rms bandwidth and crest factor, CAT rating.
P: LCR meter: frequency range and resolution, test signal level, basic accuracy and its dependence on impedance, frequency, and level, DC bias, fixture compensation.
P: Counter: single-shot resolution (ps), digits per second, timebase aging and temperature stability, input sensitivity and bandwidth, burst and pulse modes.
P: Oscilloscope: bandwidth and rise time, sample rate per channel when all are on, memory, ADC bits and noise floor (µV rms at a stated bandwidth), ENOB, trigger jitter, waveform rate, probe bandwidth and loading.
P: Generators: frequency range and resolution, amplitude accuracy and flatness, harmonics and spurs (dBc), phase noise at the offsets that matter, modulation bandwidth, AWG sample rate and memory, residual EVM for a VSG.
P: Spectrum analyzer: frequency range, DANL (with and without preamplifier), TOI, phase noise, RBW range, amplitude accuracy, analysis bandwidth and real-time POI, EMI detectors.
P: Power meter and sensors: frequency and power range, linearity, zero and drift, calibration factor uncertainty, sensor match (SWR), video bandwidth for peak sensors.
P: VNA: frequency range, dynamic range at a stated IFBW, trace noise, source power range, receiver compression, calibrated directivity and source match for the kit used, number of ports, time-domain option.
P: Signal and modulation analysis: analysis bandwidth, residual EVM for the standard, supported standards and their licenses, capture memory, ACLR dynamic range.
H2: A.5 SCPI basics
P: Syntax: keywords separated by colons, short forms in capitals (MEASure:VOLTage:DC? as MEAS:VOLT:DC?), parameters after a space, queries ending in ?, several commands separated by semicolons; numbers in SI units with an optional suffix (10E-3 or 10 mV where supported).
P: IEEE 488.2 common commands: *IDN? identify; *RST reset; *CLS clear status and errors; *OPC? wait and reply 1; *OPC set the operation-complete bit; *WAI wait before the next command; *ESE and *ESR? event status enable and register; *SRE and *STB? service request enable and status byte; *TST? self-test; *TRG software trigger.
P: Errors: SYST:ERR? returns the oldest error (0,"No error" when empty); −100 to −199 command errors (−113 undefined header), −200 to −299 execution errors (−222 data out of range), −300 to −399 device errors, −400 to −499 query errors (−410 query interrupted).
P: Data: FORM ASC or FORM REAL,32; FORM:BORD NORM or SWAP; binary blocks #<n><length><bytes>; a terminating newline after the block.
P: Triggers and buffers: TRIG:SOUR, TRIG:COUN, SAMP:COUN, INIT, then FETC? for buffered readings; READ? = INIT + FETC?; MEAS? = CONF + READ? with default settings.

H1: About the Author
P: Lothar J. Musiol is a graduate of Munich University of Applied Sciences who spent more than forty years in the semiconductor industry, first at Germany's largest company in the field, then at a string of American start-ups. He studied physics alongside all of it, seriously enough to know exactly how much he is simplifying. Dual citizenship, put to its most sensible use, lets him split his time between San Clemente, California, and Passau, Bavaria, where Austria begins at the garden fence. The household includes one married daughter, one grumpy Teacup Pomeranian named Gucci, and eleven chickens, listed in order of seniority rather than volume.
P: He writes to make complex ideas clearer and more engaging than most treatments manage, without ever pretending they are simpler than they actually are.
