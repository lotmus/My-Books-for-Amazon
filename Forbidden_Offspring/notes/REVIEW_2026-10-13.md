**Overall: 7.4 as read, 7.9 now. Sellability: 6.8 of 10** (premise and voice are strong and commercial; the mid-book needs tension and the hero needs an edge).

## Recommend?

Yes, as a strong draft, and I would finish it. An addicted reader would stay through chapter 7 for Stone alone, and the epilogue earns its tears. The two things standing between this and a book people press on friends: a real confrontation with Julian, and a hero with a real flaw.

## Craft fixes still open (ranked)

1. **Give Julian a move.** Before the shooting, let him act against Danny or the cohort (a deadline, a threat to expose them, a sample taken), and let Danny choose something costly (expose him, or bury him) so the stranger's bullet pre-empts a decision, not a problem. This also fixes the middle's lack of a ticking clock.
2. **Give Danny a flaw and a younger, funnier voice.** Let him get something badly wrong (he reads a pulse wrong, lies to Mira, breaks his own rule), and let him be less composed as a boy. Differentiate his sentences from Stone's: shorter, more concrete, fewer "a man who" similes.
3. **Trim the tics** listed above to the strongest instances, and cut a third of the epigram endings.
4. **Close or cut the open threads** (four troubled members, the last-batch girl, the vault pages).
5. **Bridge the cure.** One paragraph in chapter 17 or 20: the 2033 molecule protects acutely; the later treatment reopens plasticity in old injuries.
6. **Add the 25/50/25 diagram** and verify the appendix and bibliography.
7. **Reach 180 pages** by adding scenes (about 9,000 words): the confrontation in item 1, a Danny and Teo reunion, a lighter Munich chapter, and the troubled members' resolution.
