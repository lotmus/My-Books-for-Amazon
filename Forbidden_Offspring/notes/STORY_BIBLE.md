# Forbidden Offspring

**Pen name:** Frank Stone (set by Lothar, 12 Oct 2026). Not yet recorded in `notes/LOTHAR_RULES.md`. The scientist character is also named Frank Stone.
**Scientist:** Frank Stone
**Spelling:** American English

## Premise
Frank Stone, a geneticist at a fertility clinic, illegally edits fertilized eggs. Officially he corrects lethal mutations. Secretly he runs a second protocol aimed at producing exceptional cognitive ability (an Einstein-level intellect) plus, on at least two eggs, a package of rewritten dolphin (*Tursiops truncatus*) auditory-spatial circuits. The dolphin inserts are non-phenotypic: no external changes, only cortical timing, echo-resolution, multi-object acoustic mapping, and social-prediction loops driven by human promoters. All edits are kept heterozygous to limit automatic generational fixation. The work is a felony. Eleven years later the primary subject (the boy) has reconstructed much of what was done, located other edited children, and begun acting on his own timeline. Stone suffers a stroke that destroys his complete indexed notes and design logic. A married pair among the edited cohort becomes pregnant. Containment collapses.

## Core Science (in-story)
- Edits performed at the fertilized-egg / early-embryo stage so changes are present throughout the body.
- Heterozygous: altered sequence on one chromosome copy only. Each insert transmits to offspring at ~50 % probability. Full-package reconstruction in the next generation is rare from one parent, markedly higher if two carriers reproduce together.
- Cognitive package: dozens of loci tied to processing speed, working memory, synaptic plasticity, extended developmental window.
- Dolphin package (selected eggs): rewritten auditory cortex mapping, microsecond-scale temporal resolution, spatial echo processing, tighter social-prediction from partial acoustic cues. Passive and active acoustic spatial abilities far beyond human norms; no skull, jaw, or external phenotype.
- Detection: routine consumer tests miss the inserts; deep whole-genome sequencing catches non-human fragments and out-of-variation human loci immediately. Performance anomalies (sound tracking through walls, atypical reaction times) are harder to hide with age.

## Principal Characters
- **Frank Stone**: The scientist. Arrogant, now physically diminished by stroke. Loses the master index. No longer controls outcomes or interpretation.
- **The boy**: Primary edited subject, ~11-12 at main action. Cognitive package + dolphin auditory/spatial circuits. Deadpan, precise, low ordinary fear/attachment friction. Already copying drives, sequencing himself, locating others. Treats Stone as obsolete resource.
- **The married pair**: Two edited individuals who found each other. Overlapping heterozygous inserts. Higher odds of transmitting substantial trait combinations to children. Cooler, instrumental attachment style. First active propagation node.
- **Other edited children**: Scattered cohort. At least one additional pair identified. Variable expression levels. Some already showing acoustic performance.

## Plot Spine and Twists
1. Stone performs the illegal dual protocol (disease cover + cognitive/dolphin enhancement) during a brief unsupervised window.
2. Years later the boy is raised off-books. He sequences himself, reconstructs the edits, and begins locating the rest of the cohort.
3. Partial sequence overlaps appear in external papers (Singapore and others). Detection risk rises.
4. The boy reveals he has already copied critical drives and identified multiple others, including dolphin-insert carriers whose performance (wall tracking, microsecond sound resolution) is active.
5. Stone attempts to reassert control and flee. The boy refuses; heterozygous design requires multiple carriers to be meaningful.
6. Stone suffers a stroke. Complete design notes, dosing logic, and full index are lost. Only fragmentary reconstructions and the boy's copies remain.
7. One edited pair has married under their own names and is pregnant (or appears to have lost the pregnancy). They declined clinical genetic testing.
8. Twist: The apparent miscarriage is staged or the products were privately sequenced. A second, previously unindexed pair has also found each other and is exchanging performance data.
9. The first pair is waiting on the second pair's outcome before deciding how to handle their own pregnancy/child. Stone no longer possesses the map that would let him predict or stop either.
10. All remaining authority, samples, and decisions sit with the edited cohort. The original four minutes are irreversible, unannotated, and already propagating.

## Key Consequences
- **For the children**: Cognitive and acoustic advantages bundled with social isolation, atypical attachment, identity fracture once origin is known, and permanent sequence-detectability. Adult relationships (including the marriage) operate with reduced ordinary emotional friction.
- **Inheritance**: Partial and probabilistic until carriers reproduce together. The married pair is the first high-fidelity node. Their children are the first real test of reassembly.
- **After the stroke**: No living interpreter of the original intent or trade-offs. The edited people become the sole authorities on what the edits actually do.
- **Detection equilibrium**: Fragile. Any deep sequence, performance slip, or data leak exposes the lineage. Secrecy survives only while every carrier chooses it.

## Themes
Hubris and loss of control. Creation without full annotation. The gap between designed intent and living outcome. Responsibility after the creator is diminished. What remains of ordinary human relation when fear, reward, and sensory reality have been rewritten. Containment that fails because the subjects are intelligent enough to find each other.

## Tone and Style Notes
Deadpan precision. American English. Dry, understated irony rather than broad humor. Close third or first-person (boy) that registers acoustic and temporal detail others miss. Suspense driven by information asymmetry and irreversible biological facts rather than chase mechanics.

## Structure (settled 12 Oct 2026)
- **Part One: Stone.** Stone narrates. His voice: arrogant, self-justifying, precise, unreliable about his own motives.
- **The stroke** ends Part One. His full index and design logic are destroyed, but his narrative account survives (written before the stroke, or dictated after).
- **Part Two: the offspring.** The boy, now a teen or adult, finds Stone's account and carries on. He can read, correct and answer Stone, and can only know what Stone wrote plus what he found himself. Reconstruction replaces omniscience, which solves the limit of a boy narrator who cannot witness the lab.
- The offspring finds a lot: Stone's account plus much of his material, and holds copies. No one else knows. Secrecy is his, and later the cohort's, to keep or break.
- Continuity point: what is lost in the stroke is Stone's own master index and design logic (his ability to predict or stop anything). The offspring's copies are fragments and do not restore the full map.
- The apartment confrontation fits Stone best in Part One: he narrates what he cannot perceive, while the reader watches the boy out-hear him. Trial drafts: `drafts/scene_apartment_Stone_POV_v1.md` (chosen direction) and `drafts/scene_apartment_boy_POV_v1.md` (voice test for Part Two).

## Birth and parentage (settled 12 Oct 2026)
- The edited embryos go to the intended parents, the couple who came to the clinic for IVF. The mother always carries and gives birth, by standard embryo transfer (replacement). No surrogates, no secret second household.
- Each child is genetically the couple's own child plus Stone's inserts, so the edits stay heterozygous and the child is raised by ordinary parents.
- This replaces the earlier phrase "raised off-books." What is off the books is the second protocol: the parents consented to disease correction only and do not know about the rest. Stone is a clinic doctor in the background, not a guardian.
- Consequences to use: the parents are betrayed too, and the boy's secret includes hiding the truth from them. The cohort children are scattered across ordinary families, so finding each other means tracing clinic records and shared performance anomalies. Stone's cover is the clinic file.
- Family life (settled 12 Oct 2026): there are odd moments in the families (a child who hears the neighbor's argument through a wall, answers a question before it is asked, reacts to a sound no one else notices). Each one gets resolved; none festers into a break. Families stay intact. Tone: warm and slightly comic, with the dry understatement of the rest of the book, and no menace from the parents.
- Friction pattern (settled 12 Oct 2026): families with less educated parents have the most trouble with their super kids, and the kids with them. The gap in vocabulary, curiosity and pace is where the odd moments hurt most (a parent who feels corrected or outpaced, a child who is bored, ashamed of being bored, and guilty about it). Better-educated parents cope more easily. Write the low-education parents with dignity: love and work ethic are not in doubt, only the fit. The resolution still comes, usually through a practical kindness, not a lecture.
- Parents' knowledge (settled 12 Oct 2026): the parents never find out during the children's childhood. Possibly, once the children are adults, a parent learns because the offspring who holds Stone's paperwork investigates (so the reveal is the investigator's act, not an accident). Treat adult disclosure as optional and late, and decide parent by parent.
- Open: does the married pair's pregnancy count as the first generation born outside Stone's clinic, with no doctor involved at all?
