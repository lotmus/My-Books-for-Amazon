ARC DISTRIBUTION STRATEGY - THEFT MITIGATION + TRACKING

================================================================================
OPTION 1: GOOGLE DRIVE (Recommended)
================================================================================

SETUP (5 minutes):
1. Go to drive.google.com
2. Upload your ARC PDF/DOCX
3. Right-click file → "Share"
4. Change to "Anyone with link" → "Viewer only"
5. Copy shareable link
6. Shorten it: bit.ly/[custom-name] (easier to remember)

BENEFITS:
✓ You can see who accessed the file (when, from where)
✓ Can't download as easily (viewer mode)
✓ Track which reviewers actually opened it
✓ Can revoke access anytime
✓ No email attachments (cleaner)

EMAIL TEMPLATE WITH DRIVE LINK:
"ARC here: [bit.ly link]"

BEFORE SENDING:
- Add watermark to PDF: "ARC for [Reviewer Name]"
- This makes sharing riskier for them
- Use free tool: smallpdf.com/edit-pdf (add text overlay)

================================================================================
OPTION 2: EMAIL ATTACHMENT (Simpler, Less Tracking)
================================================================================

IF using email attachment:
1. PDF only (smaller file, harder to edit)
2. Add watermark with reviewer's name
3. Subject line: "Your ARC - Schrödinger's Paperwork"
4. Note in email: "This is personalized for you—thanks for not sharing"

RISK: Higher chance of sharing, but easier for reviewer to access.

RECOMMENDATION: Use Google Drive for 60% of reviewers, email for 40%
(some older reviewers prefer attachments).

================================================================================
WATERMARKING YOUR ARC (Free)
================================================================================

METHOD 1: Free online (smallpdf.com)
1. Go to smallpdf.com/add-watermark
2. Upload PDF
3. Add text: "ARC for [Reviewer Name] - Confidential"
4. Download
5. This appears on every page, semi-transparent

METHOD 2: Google Docs
1. Convert DOCX to Google Doc
2. Insert → Header & Footer
3. Type watermark text
4. Download as PDF

METHOD 3: Simple (send .DOCX instead of PDF)
- DOCX files are harder to share (viewer needs MS Word)
- Less likely to pirate than PDF
- Many reviewers prefer DOCX anyway

================================================================================
PHASED ROLLOUT STRATEGY
================================================================================

WEEK 1 (Next 7 days): Test Phase
- Send ARC to 10-15 "safe bet" reviewers (highly active, established profiles)
- Use Google Drive link
- Track: How many open it? How many respond? How long before they download?
- This tells you if your email is working

WEEK 2: Full Rollout
- Send to next 35-40 reviewers
- Monitor responses daily
- Follow up with non-responders mid-week

WEEK 3: Follow-up Phase
- Check who's reading (Google Drive shows activity)
- Send gentle reminder: "Just wanted to check in—ARC here if you want it"
- Start seeing reviews post (ambitious readers finish in 2-3 weeks)

WEEK 4: Pre-Launch
- Monitor who's posted reviews
- Note any early piracy (search your book title on Scribd, etc.)
- Final push for launch momentum

================================================================================
RED FLAGS (Reviewers NOT to Send ARC to)
================================================================================

❌ Profile created in last 30 days (probably scammer)
❌ Only 5-10 reviews ever (low commitment)
❌ All 5-star or all 1-star reviews (not discerning)
❌ Reviews are one-liners ("Good book!") (won't write substantive review)
❌ Haven't reviewed in 6+ months (probably inactive)
❌ Profile is private (can't message them)
❌ Reviews mention sharing/posting books on other platforms (piracy risk)

================================================================================
LEGAL SAFETY NOTE
================================================================================

You own the copyright. Asking reviewers to keep ARCs confidential is:
✓ Standard publishing practice
✓ Not legally enforceable (they can share if they want)
✓ But makes it socially awkward for them to pirate
✓ Deters casual sharing (enough friction to matter)

The watermark + confidential request reduces piracy from ~5% to ~1-2%.
Worth it.
