## What changed (REV6 to REV7)

- **Chapters 11 to 15 re-paced** (same method as 16 to 20): each runs against a stated clock, has a concrete obstacle, has dialogue explanation cut, and ends on an action or a turn.
  - 11: Operations clock (51:32 at 05:45; window at 07:40); coil store; load plate weighed at 07:05 (148 kg out); briefing cut to a corridor exchange; ends on Agnes hiding the list and "They sign at five. Copy." with the mantel clock at twenty to three.
  - 12: the schedule has to be on paper before five; Rutter and Cresswell arrive at twenty to four; yard fight kept; Agnes signs at two minutes to five; return token at twenty-five to six.
  - 13: return at 11:12 (198 kg); Pelling has thirty minutes; Form 7B has a hard deadline (central scanner at ten to seven); Garrick signs at 18:43 and keeps the file out of the tray. Pelling and Arthur's explanation of Glock's refusal cut to three lines. Ends on "I did worse."
  - 14: the protected line logs any call over four minutes; the call runs 3:58; Arthur writes 08:40 on his hand (which is where chapter 16 starts).
  - 15: the cabinets lock at ten (twenty minutes' notice); the 21:52 entry now sits on a visible clock; the duty officer's round at 04:15; new closing action: entry 33 at 04:31.
- **"as though": 24 to 6.** Remaining uses are at words 448, 9,200, 20,800, 27,900, 37,000 and 45,500 (at least 7,000 words apart). Eighteen were rewritten with concrete phrasing (for example the Hester farewell, the Gant nod, Cresswell's shout, Miss Ahmed's "These read as an application to be human").
- **"like a/an": 16 true similes to 6** (plus non-simile uses such as "would like a receipt" reworded): kept at words 2,500, 5,800, 17,500, 26,000, 39,200 and 48,600. Four stray "as if" in the same cluster of chapters (ch 19, 22, 13) were rewritten; one remains (word 32,800).
- **Continuity fixes:** the 1746 receiver's agent was Lyle, the same name as the 1815 under-secretary; he is now Mr Rutter. Saye's "Mr Pembroke" / "Your fever seems better" exchange made no sense (Pembroke is Arthur) and is fixed. Form 7B duration contradicted the clock ("two hours and ten minutes" ending at a quarter to seven); now 90 minutes from 17:15.
- Length: body 52,371 words to 52,477. This pass re-timed and trimmed rather than added; the 55k aim is still not met.
