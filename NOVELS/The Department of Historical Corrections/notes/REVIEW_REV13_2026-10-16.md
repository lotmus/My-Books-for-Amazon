## What changed

**Twist 1, Beatrice's dot (reveal ch 23, planned 22b).** Plants: ch 6 (Glock's bargain gains "a round lapsed on the eastern lines, in 1988"), ch 9 (he "named the year, 1988, and the round"; she did not ask to see the volume), ch 15 (her oldest dot is "November 2007", she cannot say why). Reveal: the receipt's "reason for return: personal" sits beside register line 291, Transfer Schedule 14, retained as drafted by B.C., 14 March 1988, which left out inspector Alan Corley. Why it cannot just be fixed: 1988 is witnessed and ratified, and is reopened only by the Receiver's override, which is Glock's lever. Corley is alive (retired 2011, Selby), so nobody is resurrected. Payoffs: ch 35 (her evidence, line 291) and ch 39 (her first entry, her letter, his silence). The ch 22 farewell line now reads "Corrected, 16 Sept. Not only personal." (REV12's "15 Oct" was a date slip in a September story).

**Twist 2, Fanshawe's village (reveal ch 32).** Plants: ch 1 (the village "and the moss it burned"), ch 7 ("a prejudice about estates"), ch 12 (Tom Tully carries peat "off the moss", Nan calls it Tullymoss, no account does). Reveal: Fanshawe in the hold; Eleanor names the drain (1761) and the nine cottages; he is explained, not forgiven; Arthur's notebook entry is the first one against his name that is not a debt. "On my last mission" is turned once and ends there.

**Twist 3, the nurse was Margaret Glock (reveal ch 36).** Plants: ch 7 (the photocopy says Leeds General Infirmary, Ward 4), ch 9 (cold hands, "stay with me, love", the staff roll lost in a trust merger, Eleanor looked in 2012 and 2014; the nurse never time-travelled), ch 15 (file entry: nurse not traced). Reveal: the custody visit. One line on why it is not coincidence: "Your name was on the band, in her mouth for twenty-seven years and at the foot of the reply. It wanted only one person to read all three." Glock stays unrepentant: he withheld the nurse because "you would have been moved, and I needed you signing", and Arthur will not let him use her as an argument. Eleanor's left watch goes into a drawer. Ch 39 first dot: Margaret Glock, staff nurse.

**Ch 27 (old 26): nothing added.** Ch 37 (old 35): only renumbered.

**Appendix:** chapter numbers updated; one sentence added to Lesson 2 (the 1991 to 2018 chain runs through records and nobody causes themselves).
