## Before and after (ch 26, the refusal)

Before: "I do not waive any person's right to keep or present contradictory evidence. No previous signature of mine should be read as consent to powers concealed from me."

After: "... I have signed findings in this department whose consequences I did not follow. Fourteen of them are in Mr Glock's notebook, and I ask that it be recorded that they are mine. A record that holds my signature has to be one I can be asked about in public. That is what I decline to give up. No previous signature of mine should be read as consent to powers concealed from me."

4. Twists 4 and 5 not used; the "rule bites back" idea has only an adapted line.
5. Hallett's turn is late (ch 28); he has no scene in the first two-thirds.
6. The KDP blurb has not been updated to use the new hook.
7. Historical details (a convent hospital in Brussels, the Gazette lists, Hallett's regiment unnamed) are plausible but not specialist-checked.
8. Chapters 11-13, 23, 33 and 35 were not read in this pass (only searched); an unread chapter could hold a clash I did not see.
