# Chapter renumbering map (pre-REV13 notes to REV15)

REV13 added two chapters and REV15 split old chapter 19 in two, so every note written before REV13 (DISPATCH_LEDGER.md, REVIEW_REV2 to REVIEW_REV12, HANDOVER.md REV2 to REV12 sections, continuity.md, TWIST_LEDGER_PLAN.md) uses the old numbers. Notes written at REV13 and REV14 (REVIEW_REV13, REVIEW_REV14) use the REV13/REV14 numbers; add 1 to any number from 20 upwards to get the REV15 number. Chapters 1 to 18 never changed.
