## Science check

Every figure in the appendix was recomputed in Python (listed in HANDOVER.md). Formulas used: proper time with the small-speed rule and the full factor; the singlet probability cos squared of half the angle and the 2 versus 2 root 2 bound; the uncertainty relation and photon momentum h over lambda; the thick-barrier tunnelling estimate e to the minus 2 kappa L. Corrections made during the pass: the billiard-ball studies found far too many consistent histories, not too few; the singlet exercises were changed so the "usual mistake" really gives a different answer; the person-scaled tunnelling example no longer compares a count of zeros with the number of atoms in the universe (the zeros are far fewer than that). Honest limits: the thick-barrier estimate drops a factor of order one (the lesson says so); the physics has not been checked by a specialist; five of the citations were not re-checked.
