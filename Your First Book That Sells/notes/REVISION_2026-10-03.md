- 70% also requires the ebook to be at least 20% below the print list price; public-domain titles and price-matched sales below the band earn 35%.
- Per-page KU rate "$1.27 per 300 pages" removed: Amazon publishes no fixed rate. July 2026 fund $67.6M confirmed [10]. KENP cap 3,000 pages per title per customer.
- Hardcover royalty is 60% at $9.99+ and 50% below (not a flat 60%). Printing cost depends on marketplace, page count and ink (trim only for hardcover).
- Pre-orders: up to 18 months (books created from 2 Sep 2026), file due 72 h before, first miss forgiven, second costs pre-order rights for 6 months, max 10 active (book said "a year" and "lose entirely").
- Categories: three, changeable any time in Edit details; not BISAC; KDP support does not recommend categories (book said "chosen once" and "ask KDP to add").
- Descriptions may not contain review quotes, review requests, prices, time-sensitive claims or URLs (old Ch22 advised pull-quotes).
- Keywords: no program names (old listing keyword "kindle unlimited kdp select guide" violated this); "50 characters" claim not on KDP's page, removed.
- Title creation limit: two per format per week from 21 Sep 2026, reset Sunday midnight UTC.
- Review eligibility: $50 on Amazon.com by card in 12 months, promotional discounts excluded; unverified "each marketplace" and gift-card claims dropped.
